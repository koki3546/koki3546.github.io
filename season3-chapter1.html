The Adventures of KOKI · Season III
Beyond the Edge of Time
CHAPTER 01

The Ship That Arrived Tomorrow

Every horizon holds a new beginning.
But what if tomorrow has already happened?

The first cannonball fell from a cloudless sky.

It struck the ocean less than twenty feet from KOKI's ship, sending a towering wall of water across the deck.

Milo screamed.

Brann dropped his breakfast.

Finn grabbed the wheel.

"Where did that come from?"

KOKI drew his sword.

Above them stretched a perfectly blue sky.

There were no enemy ships.

No islands.

Not even a cloud.

Then a second cannonball fell.

And a third.

"EVERYONE BELOW DECK!" KOKI shouted.

Nox looked upward.

His golden eye narrowed.

"Captain..."

The sky was beginning to crack.

✦ ✦ ✦

I. Thirty-Seven Days of Peace

Thirty-seven days had passed since the seven friends escaped the silver sea.

For the first time in months, their lives had become wonderfully ordinary.

Brann complained about the food.

Finn complained about Brann.

Veyra challenged Nox to sword fights that neither of them ever admitted losing.

Lyra spent her evenings studying unfamiliar constellations.

And Milo had begun building a wooden fleet large enough to conquer the kitchen.

KOKI loved every moment of it.

He had spent so long fighting to bring his friends home that he had almost forgotten what it meant simply to live beside them.

One evening, Nox found him sitting at the bow.

"Getting bored?"

KOKI smiled.

"Not at all."

"Thirty-seven days without monsters, ancient kings, or collapsing worlds."

"Sounds wonderful."

Nox laughed.

"You're becoming a terrible pirate."

KOKI looked toward the sunset.

"Perhaps being a good pirate isn't about finding trouble."

Nox sat beside him.

"Then what is it about?"

KOKI watched a flock of seabirds disappear beyond the horizon.

"Being free enough to decide where tomorrow takes you."

Neither captain knew how cruel those words would soon become.

✦ ✦ ✦

II. The Broken Sky

The crack above the ship widened.

Beyond it lay another ocean.

An ocean hanging upside down in the heavens.

Ships sailed across its surface.

Lightning flashed beneath its waves.

And enormous creatures moved through the darkness between its stars.

Lyra stared upward.

"That's impossible."

Brann looked at her.

"You said that about the last twelve impossible things we survived."

"This is different!"

A burning ship appeared within the opening.

Its masts were broken.

Its sails were covered in flames.

Figures ran desperately across its deck.

Then gravity seemed to change.

The vessel fell from the sky.

"TURN THE SHIP!" KOKI roared.

Finn spun the wheel.

The burning vessel crashed into the ocean beside them.

The impact lifted KOKI's ship almost completely out of the water.

Cannons broke loose.

Barrels rolled across the deck.

Veyra grabbed Milo before he was thrown overboard.

For several terrifying seconds, the ship balanced upon the crest of an enormous wave.

Then it crashed back into the sea.

Silence followed.

KOKI rose slowly.

The other vessel floated beside them.

Its black sails hung in ruins.

Nox approached the railing.

His expression changed.

"KOKI."

"What?"

"Look at its flag."

Through the smoke, a torn pirate flag fluttered in the wind.

Upon it was the unmistakable symbol of an elephant.

KOKI stopped breathing.

The burning ship was theirs.

✦ ✦ ✦

III. The Other Deck

"That isn't possible," Finn whispered.

The two vessels floated side by side.

Every detail was identical.

The same cannons.

The same carved railings.

Even the crooked wooden step Brann had promised to repair three weeks earlier.

But the other ship looked as though it had survived a hundred battles.

Its hull was covered in deep scars.

Its deck was stained with ash.

And no one was moving.

KOKI tied a rope around his waist.

"I'm going aboard."

Nox grabbed his arm.

"Not alone."

The two captains crossed together.

Their boots landed upon the ruined deck.

KOKI looked toward the captain's chair.

It was empty.

Beside it lay a small wooden ship.

Painted gold.

KOKI recognized it immediately.

Milo's gift.

The little vessel that had room for everyone.

But something was different.

Six tiny figures remained upon its deck.

The seventh was missing.

Nox picked it up.

Neither captain spoke.

Then a faint sound came from beneath the stairs.

Someone was breathing.

✦ ✦ ✦

IV. The Messenger

A young woman lay beneath the wreckage.

Her silver coat was torn.

A strange mechanical device surrounded her left wrist.

KOKI knelt beside her.

"Can you hear me?"

Her eyes opened.

She looked at his hat.

His eyepatch.

His red scarf.

Then tears filled her eyes.

"Captain KOKI..."

"You know me?"

The woman began to laugh and cry at the same time.

"I found you."

Nox lifted the broken timber from her legs.

"Who are you?"

"My name is Yan."

KOKI helped her sit upright.

"Where did you come from?"

Yan looked toward the burning sails.

"Tomorrow."

Nox frowned.

"What does that mean?"

Yan reached into her coat and pulled out a small metal cylinder.

Inside was a folded letter.

She placed it in KOKI's hands.

"You told me to give you this."

KOKI unfolded the paper.

The handwriting was his own.

But the message contained only three sentences.

Do not sail toward the island with two suns.

Do not trust the clocks.

And whatever happens, do not let Milo disappear.

KOKI read the final sentence again.

His hands began to tremble.

✦ ✦ ✦

V. The First Missing Hour

Back aboard their ship, Lyra examined Yan's strange device.

Its hands moved backward.

Finn brought fresh water.

Brann prepared bandages.

Veyra stood beside Milo.

KOKI placed the letter on the captain's table.

"Tell us everything."

Yan looked at the seven friends.

"Beyond the eastern ocean lies a civilization called Aevora."

Lyra leaned forward.

"I've never heard of it."

"You wouldn't have. Its people discovered how to remove hours from history."

Brann stared at her.

"Remove hours?"

"An hour of suffering. An hour of war. An hour in which someone died."

Milo looked up.

"They can bring people back?"

Yan lowered her head.

"That was what we believed."

She turned the device upon her wrist.

"But time is not an empty ocean. Every hour belongs somewhere. Every moment touches another."

The device stopped ticking.

"When we began removing the moments we feared, something began removing us."

Outside, the ship's bell rang.

Once.

Twice.

Then thirteen times.

Lyra rushed toward the window.

The sun had vanished.

Stars filled the sky.

A moment earlier, it had been morning.

Finn ran onto the deck.

"CAPTAIN!"

KOKI followed.

The burning vessel was gone.

The crack in the sky had disappeared.

And upon the eastern horizon stood an enormous island.

Golden towers rose above its mountains.

Waterfalls flowed upward into the clouds.

Two brilliant suns hung above its shores.

Yan stepped onto the deck.

Her face turned pale.

"No..."

KOKI looked at the letter.

Then at the island.

"We turn around."

Finn grabbed the wheel.

But the ship continued moving east.

The sails were empty.

The wind had stopped.

Something beneath the ocean was pulling them toward the island.

Then Veyra screamed.

"MILO!"

KOKI turned.

The boy stood beside the railing.

His wooden toy fell from his hands.

For a moment, his body became transparent.

KOKI ran toward him.

He grabbed Milo's hand.

"Captain?"

"I'm here."

Milo looked down at his fading fingers.

"What's happening to me?"

KOKI pulled him into his arms.

"Whatever it is, we're going to stop it."

Above the island, the two suns began to move in opposite directions.

And somewhere beneath its golden towers, an ancient clock began counting backward.
