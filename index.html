Important Trading & Security Notice

Initial liquidity was very small, approximately 0.01 ETH and 1 billion KOKI. Even relatively small trades may cause substantial price impact, slippage, and changes in the token's market price.

Liquidity provider tokens were not locked at launch. Liquidity may be withdrawn, and trading may become difficult or impossible.

Uniswap displayed an impersonation warning for KOKI through its security screening. A review request was submitted to Blockaid on October 23, 2026. The warning has not been confirmed as resolved or removed. This request does not constitute security approval or certification.

KOKI has not undergone an independent security audit. Always verify the official token contract, review the transaction details, and assess the risks before interacting with any token.
