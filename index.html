Important Trading & Security Notice

Initial liquidity was very small, approximately 0.01 ETH and 1 billion KOKI. Even relatively small trades may cause substantial price impact, slippage, and changes in the token's market price.

Existing project LP tokens were locked on November 26, 2026. However, the liquidity lock does not guarantee sufficient liquidity or continuous trading.

As of November 26, 2026, approximately 99.9% of the total token supply was held by the deployer. This concentration presents significant market risks. Planned token distribution is not the same as completed on-chain distribution.

Uniswap displayed an impersonation warning for KOKI through its security screening. A review request was submitted to Blockaid on October 23, 2026.

Blockaid confirmed the LP lock on November 26, 2026, but the security warning remains. Liquidity depth, token ownership concentration and organic trading activity remain concerns. The review does not constitute security approval or certification.

KOKI has not undergone an independent security audit. Always verify the official token contract, review the transaction details, and assess the risks before interacting with any token.
