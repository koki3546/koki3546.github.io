CHAPTER 09

The Ocean of a Thousand Goodbyes

Letting go does not mean forgetting. It means finding the courage to sail again.

Finn had forgotten how to let go.

For years, he had sat inside a broken wooden boat beneath a lonely star.

Thousands of ropes stretched from his bleeding hands into the darkness.

At the end of every rope was a sinking ship.

Whenever he saved one, another began to disappear.

He had not slept.

He had not smiled.

He had not heard his own name spoken in kindness.

Until a familiar voice echoed across the black ocean.

"Need another pair of hands?"

Finn looked up.

A black pirate flag appeared beneath the star.

Captain KOKI had arrived.

✦ ✦ ✦

I. The Sea That Remembered Every Loss

KOKI's ship entered the silent ocean.

There was no wind.

Yet thousands of torn sails moved beneath the water.

Lyra raised her lantern.

Faces appeared within the waves.

Sailors who had never returned home.

Children waiting beside empty harbors.

Captains who had gone down with their ships.

"This ocean remembers every life lost at sea," she whispered.

KOKI climbed aboard Finn's little boat.

Finn immediately pulled away.

"Don't touch the ropes!"

Another ship began to sink.

Finn desperately pulled it toward the surface.

"Just a little longer. I can save them."

KOKI looked at his wounded hands.

"Who told you that you had to save everyone alone?"

Finn stared at him.

"I don't remember."

Beneath the boat, two enormous eyes opened.

The ocean began to breathe.

✦ ✦ ✦

II. The Leviathan of Regret

An island rose from the water.

Then another.

And another.

Nox looked through his telescope.

"Those aren't islands."

An enormous creature emerged beneath the fleet of sinking ships.

Its body was covered with broken anchors, shattered masts, and the remains of ancient vessels.

Thousands of ropes disappeared into its chest.

Lyra's lantern flickered.

"The Leviathan of Regret."

The creature spoke with the voices of a thousand lost sailors.

"Every life you fail to save belongs to me."

Finn screamed and pulled harder.

The Leviathan grew larger.

KOKI understood.

The creature was feeding upon Finn's guilt.

Brann raised his hammer.

Veyra prepared the cannons.

But KOKI stopped them.

"Look at the ropes."

Every rope passed through the creature's heart.

Destroying it would drag every trapped ship into the abyss.

The Leviathan opened its enormous mouth.

"Choose, Captain."

"Your friend or a thousand strangers."

KOKI drew his sword.

"I've never liked those kinds of choices."

✦ ✦ ✦

III. A Thousand Pirate Flags

KOKI returned to his ship.

"Nox, turn us toward the creature."

Nox smiled.

"I was hoping you'd say that."

The pirate ship raced across the black ocean.

Veyra fired her cannons at the enormous waves.

Brann struck the floating wreckage, clearing a path through the darkness.

Lyra sent her lantern into the sky.

Its golden light traveled beyond the ocean.

Through the library.

Through the liberated fleet.

Through the harbor of forgotten histories.

And into the ships that KOKI had saved throughout his voyage.

One by one, distant lights appeared.

The refugees of Eldoria.

The sailors of Veyra's fleet.

The blacksmiths from Brann's fortress.

Hundreds of vessels emerged from the darkness.

Then hundreds more.

Each carried a black flag.

Not because they had become pirates.

But because they remembered the pirate who had refused to abandon them.

A little fishing boat approached Finn.

Its captain caught one of his ropes.

"Let us help."

Another ship took a second rope.

Then a third.

Across the ocean, a thousand hands reached into the darkness.

For the first time in years, Finn felt the weight upon his shoulders grow lighter.

✦ ✦ ✦

IV. The Captain Beneath the Waves

The Leviathan roared.

Its enormous tail shattered the surface of the ocean.

KOKI's ship was lifted into the sky.

KOKI grabbed a rope and jumped.

He landed upon the creature's back.

Broken anchors flew toward him.

He cut through them with his sword.

The Leviathan plunged beneath the waves.

KOKI held tightly to its armor.

Deeper and deeper they descended.

Until the stars disappeared.

Inside the creature's chest, KOKI discovered a small wooden cage.

A younger Finn sat inside it.

He was crying.

Around him floated the memories of every person he believed he had failed.

KOKI broke the cage.

The little boy looked at him.

"I couldn't save them."

KOKI knelt beside him.

"I know."

"Does that make me a bad person?"

KOKI gently shook his head.

"No. It means you cared."

He offered his hand.

"You cannot change every yesterday, Finn."

"But you can still sail toward tomorrow."

The boy reached for him.

And the enormous heart of the Leviathan began to crack.

✦ ✦ ✦

V. The Last Rope

Above the ocean, Finn watched the final sinking ship rise toward the surface.

Only one rope remained in his hands.

At its end was a small, empty boat.

He recognized it.

It belonged to someone he had once loved.

For years, he had believed that pulling hard enough would bring that person back.

KOKI emerged from the water.

He climbed aboard the little boat and sat beside Finn.

Neither spoke for a long time.

Finally, Finn asked a question.

"If I let go, will I forget?"

KOKI looked toward the lonely star.

"No."

He placed a hand upon Finn's shoulder.

"Love doesn't disappear just because we can no longer hold it."

Finn closed his eyes.

Slowly, he opened his hands.

The rope slipped into the ocean.

The empty boat transformed into a thousand golden butterflies.

They rose toward the stars.

Finn began to cry.

KOKI stayed beside him.

As the Leviathan dissolved beneath the waves, the black ocean became a brilliant blue.

A thousand ships sounded their bells.

Finn looked at KOKI.

"Can we go home now, Captain?"

KOKI smiled.

"Of course."

✦ ✦ ✦

VI. Seven Friends Against the End of Time

For the first time since their ship had disappeared, all seven friends stood together upon its deck.

Nox.

Brann.

Veyra.

Lyra.

Finn.

Milo.

And Captain KOKI.

Milo placed his paper boat upon the wooden railing.

Brann repaired the final broken plank.

Upon it were four familiar words.

NO ONE LEFT BEHIND.

Suddenly, the sky turned golden.

An enormous tower appeared beyond the horizon.

Seven rotating rings surrounded its summit.

The stars began to move backward.

Lyra opened her black book.

Her face became pale.

"The Meridian Engine has entered its final sequence."

Veyra raised her telescope.

Thousands of golden warships surrounded the tower.

But KOKI's ship was no longer alone.

Behind it sailed the fleet of a thousand rescued vessels.

Finn tightened the ropes of the sails.

"Where to, Captain?"

KOKI pointed his sword toward the tower.

"Tomorrow."

Far above the ocean, Vaelor stood beside his daughter.

She watched the stars disappearing.

"Father, where are all the lights going?"

"Somewhere they cannot hurt us anymore."

The little fox looked at him.

"But what if someone lives there?"

Vaelor froze.

Behind him, the second golden ring began to turn.

A distant galaxy vanished.

Fifteen hours remained.
