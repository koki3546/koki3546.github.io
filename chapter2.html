THE KOKI CHRONICLES · CHAPTER 02

The Unknown Sea

Sometimes courage means sailing toward the very thing you fear.

The first bell rang at midnight.

KOKI opened his eyes.

The little wooden boat rocked gently beneath a sky without stars. Only a few hours had passed since he had left his island behind.

Yet the world already felt different.

The wind had stopped. The sea had become unnaturally still. Even the waves seemed afraid to move.

Then the bell rang again.

Deep. Hollow. Distant.

KOKI searched the horizon. There was no lighthouse. No harbor. No ship.

"Who's out there?"

Only silence answered.

✦ ✦ ✦

A cold drop of rain landed on his nose.

Then another.

Within moments, the sky split open.

Thunder shook the ocean. Wind tore through the sails. A wave as tall as a mountain rose before his tiny boat.

KOKI grabbed the wheel.

The broken compass spun wildly. The mast groaned. The boat climbed the wall of water, trembling beneath his feet.

"Come on! Just a little farther!"

The wave crashed.

The boat rolled sideways. KOKI was thrown against the railing. A sharp pain shot through his shoulder.

For one terrible moment, he saw nothing but black water.

He thought of home.

Of the warm lights in the windows. Of the voices that had warned him never to cross the horizon.

Perhaps they had been right.

Perhaps he was only a foolish little elephant who had mistaken a dream for destiny.

His grip began to loosen.

Then, through the thunder, he heard something else.

"Help..."

KOKI froze.

It was a voice.

✦ ✦ ✦

Lightning flashed across the sea.

For a heartbeat, the darkness disappeared.

Beyond the waves stood a ruined ship, trapped between enormous black rocks. Its sails hung in shreds. Strange blue light glowed beneath its hull.

And from somewhere inside, a small voice cried again.

"Please... don't leave me!"

KOKI stared at the wreck.

His boat was barely holding together. Sailing closer could destroy it.

He could turn away.

Nobody would ever know.

But he would.

Slowly, KOKI tightened his grip on the wheel.

"Hold on!" he shouted. "I'm coming!"

He turned straight into the storm.

✦ ✦ ✦

The next wave struck like a hammer.

KOKI tied a rope around his waist, fastened the other end to the mast, and climbed onto the slippery bow.

The wreck was close now.

Close enough to see a narrow opening between its shattered planks.

Close enough to hear someone crying.

He leaped.

His feet struck the ruined deck. Rotten wood broke beneath him. He caught a beam with his trunk and pulled himself into the darkness.

Beneath a fallen wooden chest, a tiny fox cub was trapped.

The cub wore a faded blue scarf and clutched a small brass key.

His eyes widened at the sight of KOKI.

"You're... an elephant?"

"A pirate elephant," KOKI replied. "And we're leaving. Now."

He pushed against the chest. It barely moved.

Water poured through the broken hull.

KOKI planted his feet, wrapped his trunk around the timber, and lifted with everything he had.

The chest shifted.

The fox scrambled free.

Together, they raced toward the opening as the wreck began to collapse.

✦ ✦ ✦

They reached KOKI's boat just as the ruined ship disappeared beneath the waves.

KOKI collapsed onto the deck, coughing seawater.

The fox sat beside him, shivering beneath his blue scarf.

"My name is Finn," he whispered.

KOKI managed a tired smile.

"I'm KOKI."

Finn looked at the dark water. His expression changed.

"You shouldn't have come here."

KOKI frowned.

"Why?"

Finn slowly opened his paw.

The brass key was engraved with the shape of a bell.

"Because that ship sank a hundred years ago."

KOKI looked back.

The storm had stopped. The sea was perfectly still.

Then something enormous moved beneath the boat.

Two golden eyes opened in the black water.

And the bell rang a third time.
