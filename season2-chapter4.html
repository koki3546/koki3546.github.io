THE KOKI CHRONICLES · SEASON TWO

The Shadow Captain

The Twelve Stars — Chapter 04

Three days after leaving Aetheria, KOKI still could not sleep.

Every time he closed his eye, he saw the same terrible vision.

Twelve falling stars. A burning ocean. And a captain wearing his face.

Before their departure, Lyra and Milo had watched from the harbor of Aetheria.

Milo had given KOKI his little wooden ship.

"So you won't forget us," he had said.

Now the wooden ship rested beside the ancient star map in KOKI's cabin.

It reminded him of everything he had promised to protect.

Suddenly, the alarm bell rang.

"Ships!" Finn shouted from above.

"Captain! They're everywhere!"

✦ ✦ ✦

The Black Armada

KOKI rushed onto the deck.

The moon was enormous. The ocean was unnaturally dark.

Across the horizon, dozens of black ships emerged from the mist.

Their torn sails carried the symbol of a crimson star surrounded by twelve blades.

Veyra lowered her telescope.

"I know every pirate flag in the Forgotten Sea. I've never seen that one."

Brann counted the approaching vessels.

"Thirty ships against one."

KOKI drew his sword.

"Then they'd better bring more."

The first cannon exploded.

A burning projectile struck the ocean, sending a wall of water over the deck.

"Battle stations!"

Veyra took command of the cannons. Brann secured the damaged rigging. Finn climbed the mast to guide them through the enemy formation.

KOKI turned the wheel sharply.

Their ship slipped between two enemy vessels just as both fired their cannons.

The black ships struck each other.

Flames erupted across the sea.

But more ships surrounded them.

At the center of the armada sailed an enormous warship with a broken golden crown mounted upon its bow.

KOKI recognized the crown immediately.

It was identical to the one Chronos had held in Aetheria.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Enemy Wearing His Face

Iron hooks crashed onto the deck.

Shadow warriors poured aboard, their armor glowing with crimson light.

KOKI fought his way toward the railing.

Then a figure descended from the enemy flagship.

A tall elephant wearing a torn black coat. A weathered pirate hat. A black eyepatch over his right eye.

And a familiar scar across his cheek.

KOKI froze.

It was the captain from his vision.

The stranger slowly drew his golden sword.

"Hello, KOKI."

KOKI raised his blade.

"Who are you?"

The stranger smiled bitterly.

"I am what remains of you when there is nothing left to save."

Their swords collided.

Golden sparks exploded between them.

KOKI attacked again and again, but the stranger anticipated every movement.

Every strike. Every feint. Every desperate attempt to escape.

It was as if he had already fought this battle a thousand times.

The stranger knocked KOKI's sword away and pressed his blade against KOKI's chest.

"Still protecting everyone?"

KOKI stared into his enemy's glowing red eye.

"Always."

For a moment, the Shadow Captain's expression changed.

He looked almost heartbroken.

✦ ✦ ✦

The World That Died

The Shadow Captain lowered his weapon.

He removed a small silver object from beneath his coat.

It was a broken telescope.

Finn stopped moving.

"That looks exactly like mine."

The stranger turned toward him.

"Hello, old friend."

His voice trembled.

Finn stepped backward.

The Shadow Captain closed his eye.

"In my world, we completed the voyage. We gathered the Twelve Stars. We believed we had saved everyone."

He looked toward the heavens.

"We were wrong."

The stolen First Star appeared above his open palm.

Within its golden light, KOKI saw another ocean.

An ocean filled with shattered ships.

A ruined Aetheria. A silent world beneath a broken sky.

He saw Veyra's fallen hat. Brann's broken weapon. And Finn's telescope lying on an empty shore.

KOKI could barely breathe.

"What happened?"

The Shadow Captain clenched his fist.

"The Twelve Stars are not a treasure. They are twelve locks on a prison. And we were the fools who opened every one of them."

KOKI looked at the ancient star map.

Everything he believed about his second voyage suddenly seemed uncertain.

"Then help us stop it."

The stranger shook his head.

"There is only one way. Every star must be destroyed before the final lock is opened."

KOKI remembered the frozen children of Aetheria.

"And the innocent people who depend on those stars?"

The Shadow Captain looked away.

"Some lives must be sacrificed to save the rest."

KOKI retrieved his sword.

"Then you're not the captain I intend to become."

✦ ✦ ✦

No One Left Behind

The Shadow Captain raised his hand.

Every cannon in the black armada turned toward KOKI's ship.

"Perhaps you need to learn the lesson that I learned."

The cannons fired.

KOKI seized a hanging rope and swung toward the enemy flagship.

His sword sliced through the chains supporting its enormous mast.

The mast collapsed across the deck, sending the shadow warriors scattering.

Veyra fired into the exposed flagship. Brann pulled their ship away from the burning wreckage.

But Finn lost his footing.

He fell overboard.

"FINN!"

Without hesitation, KOKI dropped his sword and jumped into the raging ocean.

A powerful current dragged Finn beneath the waves.

KOKI reached for him.

Their hands were inches apart.

Then another hand seized Finn.

It belonged to the Shadow Captain.

Together, the two elephants lifted Finn above the water.

Veyra threw them a rope.

They reached the surface just as the burning flagship collapsed into the ocean.

Finn coughed and opened his eyes.

The Shadow Captain stared at him.

A single tear fell from his remaining eye.

"This time..."

He whispered.

"This time I caught you."

KOKI finally understood.

Whatever the stranger had become, somewhere beneath his anger was a captain who had lost the friends he loved.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Prison Beneath the Stars

The surviving black ships began to disappear into the mist.

The Shadow Captain climbed aboard one of the remaining vessels.

Before leaving, he looked back at KOKI.

"Go to the Island of Whispering Bones. Find the keeper of the Second Star."

He paused.

"Ask her what sleeps beyond the twelve locks."

KOKI called after him.

"There has to be another way!"

The stranger stood silently.

"I spent forty years looking for one."

His ship vanished into the darkness.

Then the ancient star map began to tremble.

A voice rose from deep beneath the ocean.

It was neither human nor animal.

It sounded like a thousand voices speaking together.

"Eleven locks remain. Thank you, Captain."

KOKI stared at the water.

Something beneath the ocean was laughing.

The Shadow Captain believed destroying the stars would save the world.

But what if destroying them was exactly what the prisoner wanted?

KOKI gripped the little wooden ship that Milo had given him.

There had to be another way.

And he would find it.

No matter what it cost.
