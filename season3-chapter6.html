CHAPTER 06

The Forge of Broken Histories

Even the greatest weapon can become a place to call home.

The sound of a hammer was destroying the universe.

With every strike, a distant star disappeared.

With every spark, an unwritten civilization vanished.

KOKI stood at the bow of his ship, staring at an enormous fortress surrounded by rivers of molten iron.

Thousands of mechanical towers rose from its walls.

Above them, broken moons floated in a crimson sky.

Milo held his mother's paper boat.

Its silver light pointed toward the heart of the fortress.

"Brann is in there."

KOKI drew his sword.

"Then we're going in."

✦ ✦ ✦

I. The Iron Ocean

Behind KOKI sailed the surviving fleet of Eldoria.

Hundreds of vessels carried families who no longer had a world to call home.

Suddenly, the fortress opened its enormous gates.

Black warships emerged from the rivers of molten iron.

Their cannons burned with violet flames.

Nox raised his telescope.

"Those aren't ordinary weapons."

The first cannon fired.

A royal vessel disappeared.

Not a single piece of wood remained.

Even its reflection vanished from the molten river.

Milo stared in horror.

KOKI immediately raised his sword.

"ALL SHIPS! SCATTER!"

The iron ocean erupted.

KOKI turned his vessel toward the approaching warships.

"Nox, protect the fleet!"

"What about you?"

KOKI smiled.

"I'm going to visit an old friend."

He accelerated directly toward the fortress.

✦ ✦ ✦

II. The Blacksmith Who Forged the End

Deep within the fortress, Brann stood before an enormous anvil.

Golden chains surrounded his wrists.

His hammer was connected to a machine that stretched through the entire mountain.

Every time he struck the anvil, the machine absorbed the energy of a forgotten history.

Director Vaelor watched from a platform above him.

"Again."

Brann refused to move.

Vaelor touched a golden instrument.

Within a glass chamber, a small village appeared.

Children played beside a river.

Families gathered around warm fireplaces.

"Strike the anvil, or their history ends."

Brann closed his eyes.

He raised his hammer.

The impact shook the fortress.

Somewhere beyond the walls, another world disappeared.

Vaelor smiled.

"Even the strongest creatures can be controlled when they have something to protect."

Brann looked at him.

"Then you've never met my captain."

✦ ✦ ✦

III. The Pirate Inside the Mountain

KOKI's ship crashed through a waterfall of molten iron, protected by the silver light of Milo's paper boat.

The vessel entered an enormous underground chamber.

Thousands of unfinished weapons hung from mechanical chains.

Some contained miniature oceans.

Others held entire skies.

KOKI climbed onto a moving platform and leapt toward the central machinery.

Mechanical guards descended from the ceiling.

He drew his sword.

The first guard fell.

A second grabbed his coat.

KOKI swung around a hanging chain and sent the creature crashing into a furnace.

Then the floor began moving.

The entire chamber was part of an enormous machine.

Walls became gears.

Bridges folded into weapons.

A mechanical dragon emerged from the molten river beneath him.

Its wings were formed from burning pages of history.

KOKI jumped onto its back.

The dragon roared and flew through the fortress.

He held tightly to its armor as it smashed through towers and mechanical bridges.

Above him, a familiar voice thundered.

"WHO'S MAKING ALL THAT NOISE?"

KOKI laughed.

"YOUR CAPTAIN!"

For the first time in what felt like a lifetime, Brann smiled.

✦ ✦ ✦

IV. The World Vaelor Lost

The mechanical dragon crashed into the central forge.

KOKI landed beside Brann.

He raised his sword and shattered one of the golden chains.

But before he could destroy the others, Vaelor appeared.

"Do you understand what you are protecting, pirate?"

Vaelor opened his hand.

The forge disappeared around them.

KOKI found himself standing in a beautiful coastal city.

White towers surrounded a peaceful harbor.

A young fox wearing a blue coat ran toward Vaelor.

"Father!"

Vaelor knelt and embraced her.

For a moment, he was no longer the cold director of the Meridian Council.

He was simply a father.

Then the sky fractured.

The ocean rose.

The city disappeared.

The little fox vanished from his arms.

Vaelor stood alone.

"This was my world."

His voice trembled.

"The collision of two histories destroyed everything."

The vision changed.

Thousands of different worlds appeared.

In some, the little fox survived.

In others, she had never been born.

"I searched for a history in which I could save her."

Vaelor looked at KOKI.

"But every new history created another tragedy."

The vision disappeared.

"That is why there must be only one history. One future. One perfect world."

KOKI slowly lowered his sword.

"And who decides which world deserves to survive?"

Vaelor remained silent.

KOKI looked at him.

"You didn't lose your daughter because there were too many worlds."

He pointed toward the enormous forge.

"But you are destroying other families because you cannot accept losing yours."

Vaelor's expression hardened.

"Activate the Final Hammer."

✦ ✦ ✦

V. The Hammer That Built a Ship

The mountain began to open.

An enormous mechanical giant rose from beneath the fortress.

Its body was formed from thousands of stolen histories.

Its right arm carried a hammer larger than an entire city.

With one strike, it could erase a world.

With two, it could erase every memory of that world.

Brann stared at the creature.

"I built that."

KOKI broke the remaining chains.

"No. They forced you to build it."

Brann looked at his hands.

"How many worlds have disappeared because of me?"

KOKI placed his hand upon Brann's shoulder.

"Then let's use those hands to save some."

Outside, the giant raised its hammer above the Eldorian fleet.

Nox desperately turned his ship.

Milo held the paper boat against his chest.

Its light was fading.

Brann looked toward the central furnace.

"The machine has a weakness."

He lifted his enormous hammer.

"Everything that has been destroyed is still inside it."

KOKI understood.

"Can we bring those histories back?"

Brann grinned.

"I'm a blacksmith, Captain."

He walked toward the furnace.

"Breaking things was never my specialty."

Together, they struck the heart of the machine.

Once.

Twice.

On the third strike, the furnace cracked.

Millions of golden lights escaped.

They were not flames.

They were memories.

Lost oceans.

Forgotten mountains.

Cities that had never seen another sunrise.

The lights surrounded the mechanical giant.

Its enormous hammer stopped falling.

Then its armor began to transform.

Weapons became bridges.

Cannons became towers.

Its enormous chest opened into a harbor.

Before KOKI's eyes, the greatest weapon ever created became a magnificent floating island.

Brann lowered his hammer.

"Now that's something worth building."

✦ ✦ ✦

VI. The Harbor Beyond History

The Eldorian fleet approached the floating island.

For the first time since losing their kingdom, the refugees stepped onto solid ground.

Children ran across newly formed bridges.

Families gathered beneath golden trees.

Milo placed his mother's paper boat upon the water of the new harbor.

It floated peacefully.

Nox stood beside KOKI.

"Looks like we've accidentally founded a kingdom."

KOKI smiled.

"Not a kingdom."

He watched the families leaving their ships.

"A home."

Brann approached them.

His hammer rested upon his shoulder.

"Where are the others?"

KOKI looked toward three distant lights.

"Still waiting for us."

Suddenly, a silver compass emerged from the heart of the floating island.

It spun wildly before pointing toward a dark ocean.

Beyond the next doorway, thousands of warships surrounded an enormous prison vessel.

At its center stood Veyra.

But she was not a prisoner.

She wore the uniform of an admiral.

And every warship obeyed her command.

A message appeared across the silver compass.

CAPTAIN KOKI — WANTED FOR THE DESTRUCTION OF HISTORY.

Nox raised an eyebrow.

"Well, that's new."

Brann laughed.

KOKI placed his hand upon the wheel.

"Let's go remind her who the real pirates are."

The black pirate flag rose once more.

Behind them, the new harbor illuminated the darkness like a newborn star.

But far beneath the Meridian Tower, Vaelor stood before a glass chamber.

Inside it slept a little fox.

He placed his hand against the glass.

"Just eighteen more hours, my daughter."

An ancient clock began to turn.

And somewhere in the universe, tomorrow began to disappear.

Eighteen hours remained.

18:00:00
TIME REMAINING
