CHAPTER 12
SEASON FINALE

The Dawn of a Thousand Tomorrows

The greatest adventure is not finding a perfect world, but discovering the courage to sail toward an uncertain tomorrow.

One second was all the universe had left.

But within that second, KOKI could hear a thousand bells.

He could hear the laughter of children.

The songs of sailors.

The voices of friends he had lost along the way.

The universe was not silent.

It was fighting to live.

The Final Tomorrow raised its enormous blade.

"ALL IMPERFECTIONS MUST END."

KOKI raised his sword.

"Then you'll have to start with me."

✦ ✦ ✦

I. The Battle Within a Second

The creature brought its blade down.

KOKI leapt toward it.

Their swords collided.

The frozen ocean shattered into millions of floating fragments.

Within every fragment, an entire world was waiting to disappear.

KOKI's sword began to crack.

The creature pushed him toward the edge of the ship.

"YOU CANNOT DEFEAT ETERNITY."

Suddenly, a silver blade struck the creature's arm.

Nox stood beside KOKI.

"Good thing he isn't fighting alone."

A hammer flew through the golden darkness.

Brann stepped forward.

Finn rang the bell again.

Veyra's sails unfolded.

Lyra's lantern burst into light.

One by one, the frozen ships began to move.

Time had not returned.

But the memories connecting them had become stronger than the machine controlling it.

The Final Tomorrow looked toward the approaching fleet.

"IMPOSSIBLE."

KOKI smiled.

"We hear that a lot."

✦ ✦ ✦

II. The Father Who Chose Tomorrow

Beneath the deck, Vaelor held the crystal containing his daughter's heartbeat.

The golden light was fading.

Lyra knelt beside him.

"The connection is almost complete."

"But the engine must be destroyed before the final second ends."

Vaelor looked at Elara.

He had spent centuries trying to escape the worst moment of his life.

He had sacrificed kingdoms.

Erased civilizations.

And turned the future into a prison.

Elara placed her little hand upon his.

"Father, I'm not afraid."

Vaelor began to cry.

"I am."

Milo placed his paper boat beside them.

"My mother was afraid too."

"But she still taught me how to sail."

Vaelor looked at the little mouse.

Then he looked at his daughter.

For the first time in centuries, he stopped searching for certainty.

He removed the crystal from its golden chamber.

The Meridian Tower began to collapse.

"Forgive me," he whispered.

And he broke the connection.

✦ ✦ ✦

III. The Fall of the Final Tomorrow

The creature screamed.

Its seven golden rings shattered.

Millions of stolen stars erupted from its chest.

But instead of falling, they began returning to their own skies.

The creature reached toward KOKI.

Its enormous body was breaking apart.

"WITHOUT ME, THE FUTURE WILL BE IMPERFECT."

KOKI lowered his sword.

"Of course it will."

"THERE WILL BE PAIN."

"I know."

"THERE WILL BE LOSS."

"I know."

"THEN WHY DO YOU WISH TO CONTINUE?"

KOKI looked at his friends.

At the thousand ships.

At the little paper boats glowing beneath the broken sky.

"Because there will also be things worth living for."

The creature became silent.

Its golden armor fell away.

Within it remained a small, trembling light.

"WHAT WILL HAPPEN TOMORROW?"

KOKI smiled.

"I don't know."

"And that's why I want to see it."

The light dissolved into the sky.

For the first time since its creation, the Meridian Engine stopped.

00:00:00
THE MERIDIAN ENGINE HAS STOPPED
✦ ✦ ✦

IV. The Girl Who Survived Yesterday

The universe became dark.

Vaelor held Elara in his arms.

Her little hands began to disappear.

"No..."

He held her tighter.

Milo placed his paper boat upon her chest.

Lyra raised her lantern.

Across the fleet, thousands of golden threads gathered around the little fox.

Milo began to speak.

He told the story of a girl he had met beside a fountain.

A girl who could not make her paper boat float.

A girl who wanted everyone to have a home.

Finn remembered her laughter.

Brann remembered her little blue coat.

KOKI remembered the courage with which she had questioned her father.

Elara was no longer only a memory belonging to Vaelor.

She had become part of their lives.

Her hands slowly returned.

Her heart began to beat.

She opened her eyes.

"Father?"

Vaelor could not speak.

He simply held her.

And for the first time, he thanked the universe for a tomorrow he had not created.

✦ ✦ ✦

V. The Harbor of a Thousand Flags

Morning came.

The seven golden rings had become seven magnificent bridges across the ocean.

The remains of Meridian Tower formed an enormous island.

Ships from countless worlds gathered around its shores.

Brann began rebuilding the harbor.

Veyra established a fleet to protect travelers between worlds.

Finn helped lost sailors find their way home.

Lyra planted a tree beside the harbor.

Upon its branches hung thousands of lanterns.

Each lantern carried the name of someone whose history had been lost.

Vaelor approached KOKI.

"Can someone like me ever be forgiven?"

KOKI looked toward the lanterns.

"That isn't something I can decide for everyone."

Vaelor lowered his head.

"But you can decide what you do tomorrow."

Vaelor looked toward the harbor.

There were thousands of broken ships.

Thousands of families who needed help.

And for the first time, he had a purpose that did not require controlling the universe.

He picked up a hammer.

Brann smiled.

"About time you made yourself useful."

Nearby, Milo and Elara placed two paper boats upon the ocean.

The little boats sailed side by side.

Neither was perfect.

Both floated.

✦ ✦ ✦

VI. The Captain's Tomorrow

Several weeks later, the pirate ship was ready to sail again.

Nox stood at the wheel.

Finn checked the ropes.

Brann carried the last barrel aboard.

Veyra studied the stars.

Lyra closed her new book.

Milo waved toward Elara and Vaelor upon the harbor.

"Will you come back?" Elara shouted.

KOKI raised his hat.

"Every good pirate knows the way back to his friends!"

The sails unfolded.

The ship moved toward the open ocean.

Nox looked at KOKI.

"Where are we going?"

KOKI took out his compass.

Its needle pointed toward an unfamiliar horizon.

He smiled.

"Somewhere we haven't been."

Above them, thousands of restored stars illuminated the morning sky.

KOKI remembered the little elephant who had once left a quiet island in a broken wooden boat.

He had wanted to discover how large the world was.

Now he understood.

The greatest thing about the world was not its size.

It was the endless possibility of becoming something more than yesterday.

KOKI placed his hand upon the wheel.

The wind carried seven familiar voices across the ocean.

NO ONE LEFT BEHIND.

And the third voyage came to an end.

✦ ✦ ✦

Epilogue — The Ocean Without Stars

Far beyond the borders of the known universe, an ancient ship drifted through a colorless ocean.

There were no stars above it.

No wind.

No sound.

At its center stood a lighthouse made entirely of white stone.

Its light had been extinguished for ten thousand years.

Suddenly, a small golden feather fell from the darkness.

The lighthouse awakened.

Deep beneath the ocean, something enormous opened its eyes.

An ancient voice whispered.

"The seven rings have fallen."

A second voice answered.

"Then the barrier is gone."

Beyond the lighthouse, an ocean that had never appeared on any map began to move.

And upon its shores stood an army of forgotten gods.

One of them looked toward the distant universe.

"Find the little elephant."
