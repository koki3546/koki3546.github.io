THE KOKI CHRONICLES · CHAPTER 09

The Lost Navigator

Some journeys lead us across the sea. Others lead us back to the people we thought we'd lost.

The chain pulled KOKI toward the darkness.

He staggered across Brann's deck as the red links tightened around his wrist.

Finn caught him before he fell.

"KOKI! Stay with me!"

But KOKI could hear another voice.

Morcant.

"Come to the Hollow Lighthouse. Bring me the Last Compass."

KOKI clenched his teeth.

"Get out of my head."

The chain burned brighter.

His hand rose against his will.

Slowly, his fingers reached toward the golden compass hanging from Finn's neck.

Finn froze.

KOKI's eyes widened in horror.

"Finn... move!"

The little fox stepped back.

KOKI slammed his chained arm against the wooden railing.

The impact sent pain through his shoulder.

But his hand stopped moving.

For now.

✦ ✦ ✦

By nightfall, the fleet reached the Black Reefs.

Jagged rocks rose from the ocean like the teeth of a sleeping monster.

At their center stood the Hollow Lighthouse.

Its tower was broken.

Its windows were dark.

Yet a pale beam circled the sky where no flame burned.

Captain Brann lowered his telescope.

"No ship can cross those reefs."

Thalor surfaced beside them.

"Then the ships must wait."

KOKI looked toward the tower.

The Last Compass pointed directly at its highest window.

Veyra stepped forward.

"There is an old causeway beneath the water. Rowan and I used it once."

Finn turned to her.

"You were here with my father?"

Veyra nodded.

She looked away.

"And I left him here."

The words hung between them.

Finn said nothing.

KOKI looked at Veyra.

"Then this time, you don't leave anyone behind."

Veyra met his eyes.

"I won't."

✦ ✦ ✦

Thalor raised the ancient causeway from beneath the waves.

Stone after stone emerged from the black water.

KOKI, Finn, and Veyra crossed toward the lighthouse.

Behind them, Brann's fleet waited beneath a moonless sky.

The lighthouse door opened before they touched it.

Inside, hundreds of broken compasses hung from the ceiling.

Their needles spun in different directions.

Finn held the Last Compass.

Its golden needle remained still.

Pointing upward.

They climbed a winding staircase.

On every landing stood a stone tablet.

Each bore the same warning.

"THE LIGHT REMEMBERS WHAT THE SEA FORGETS."

At the top, they found a circular room filled with silver dust.

In the center stood an empty lantern.

Beneath it lay a familiar blue scarf.

Finn gasped.

"That's my father's."

He ran toward it.

But before he could reach the lantern, the floor lit up with red symbols.

KOKI's chain tightened.

His eyes turned crimson.

✦ ✦ ✦

Morcant's voice filled the chamber.

"Welcome, little captain."

KOKI tried to step back.

His body refused.

His chained arm rose.

The red links twisted into the shape of a blade.

Finn stared at him.

"KOKI?"

KOKI's mouth moved.

But the words were not his.

"Give me the compass."

Veyra drew her silver sword.

"Morcant! Release him!"

KOKI turned toward her.

His arm struck.

Veyra blocked the blow, but the force sent her crashing against the wall.

Finn stumbled backward.

KOKI advanced.

Inside his mind, he screamed.

Stop.

Please stop.

But his feet kept moving.

The red blade rose above Finn.

Finn closed his eyes.

He did not run.

"I know you're still there."

The blade trembled.

"You promised we would stay together."

KOKI's arm shook violently.

Morcant roared inside his mind.

"STRIKE!"

KOKI turned the blade toward himself.

It struck the floor beside his feet.

The stone shattered.

KOKI fell to his knees.

"I... won't..."

The chain pulled tighter.

Blood ran down his wrist.

"I won't hurt him!"

✦ ✦ ✦

Finn dropped beside KOKI.

He pressed the Last Compass against the red chain.

Golden light spread across the floor.

The empty lantern began to glow.

A voice filled the room.

Gentle.

Familiar.

"Finn?"

The little fox froze.

Slowly, he turned.

A figure stood inside the lantern's light.

A silver-furred fox wearing a navigator's coat.

His face was older.

His eyes were tired.

But Finn knew him.

"Father?"

The figure smiled.

"You've grown so much."

Finn ran toward him.

His arms passed through the light.

He stumbled.

The figure flickered.

"I'm sorry, Finn. This is only a memory I left behind."

Finn's face crumpled.

"Where are you?"

Rowan's image looked toward the dark sea.

"Beyond the third gate. Where the Bell cannot be heard."

He looked down at the golden compass.

"If you found this light, then the Last Compass has chosen its captain."

His gaze shifted to KOKI.

Rowan smiled sadly.

"And I see it chose well."

✦ ✦ ✦

Veyra stepped forward.

Her voice trembled.

"Rowan..."

The memory turned.

For a moment, the old friends faced each other.

"Veyra. If you are hearing this, then you came back."

Tears filled her eyes.

"I should never have left you."

Rowan's image softened.

"You cannot change the night you betrayed me."

Veyra lowered her head.

"I know."

"But you can decide what kind of person you become afterward."

Veyra covered her face.

The lantern grew brighter.

Rowan turned toward Finn.

"My son, listen carefully."

The walls of the lighthouse transformed into a map.

Three circles appeared.

One was broken.

Two remained.

"Morcant believes the three seals are locks protecting the Bell."

Rowan paused.

"He is wrong."

The map changed.

Beneath the circles appeared a vast sleeping shape.

Something enormous.

Something alive.

"The seals are not locks."

Rowan's voice grew solemn.

"They are chains holding the Drowned King beneath the sea."

Finn stared at the image.

"The Drowned King?"

"The first ruler of the Forgotten Sea. The one who tried to command every living soul."

Rowan looked at KOKI.

"The Bell does not grant power."

The lighthouse shook.

"It awakens a king who believes freedom is a curse."

✦ ✦ ✦

KOKI struggled to stand.

The chain burned against his wrist.

"Then we have to stop Morcant."

Rowan's memory nodded.

"But first, you must free yourself."

The lantern opened.

A silver flame floated into the air.

"The light of the second seal can weaken the chain."

KOKI reached toward it.

The red links tightened.

Morcant screamed inside his mind.

"DO NOT TOUCH IT!"

KOKI fell forward.

His fingers brushed the silver flame.

Light exploded through the chamber.

The red chain cracked.

One link broke.

Then another.

KOKI screamed.

The chain shattered into burning fragments.

Finn caught him as he collapsed.

For a moment, the room was silent.

Then KOKI opened his eyes.

They were his own again.

Finn threw his arms around him.

"You're back!"

KOKI held him tightly.

"I never left."

But beneath his coat, a faint red mark remained on his wrist.

KOKI saw it.

He said nothing.

✦ ✦ ✦

The lighthouse began to collapse.

Rowan's memory flickered.

Finn turned toward him.

"Father! Wait!"

Rowan looked at his son.

His expression was filled with love.

"I am proud of you, Finn."

Finn began to cry.

"I don't want to lose you again."

Rowan knelt within the fading light.

"Then come find me."

The image vanished.

The lantern went dark.

KOKI caught Finn as the floor split open.

Veyra led them toward the staircase.

Outside, Thalor rose beneath the collapsing causeway.

They leaped onto his back as the lighthouse fell into the sea.

A final beam of silver light shot into the sky.

The second seal remained unbroken.

For now.

✦ ✦ ✦

At dawn, the fleet sailed away from the Black Reefs.

Finn stood at the bow, holding his father's scarf.

KOKI joined him.

For a long time, neither spoke.

Then Finn looked up.

"He's alive."

KOKI nodded.

"Somewhere beyond the third gate."

Finn smiled through his tears.

"Then we'll find him."

KOKI looked toward the distant horizon.

"Together."

Behind them, Veyra stood silently.

She had spent years running from her past.

Now, for the first time, she was sailing toward it.

But far beneath the waves, in the ruins of the Hollow Lighthouse, something moved.

The shattered red links gathered in the darkness.

One by one, they formed a new chain.

And at its center, a single black link began to glow.

Morcant's voice whispered through the deep.

"You have broken my command, little captain."

A dark smile spread beneath his mask.

"But you have not broken our bond."
