THE KOKI CHRONICLES · SEASON TWO

The Captain Who Never Existed

The Twelve Stars — Chapter 07

There are questions more frightening than any monster beneath the sea.

For KOKI, one of them had been written on a single sheet of paper.

"If I reach it, I will remember why I created you."

The words had followed him from the ruined throne room of Auralis to the deck of his ship.

He had read the letter twelve times.

The words never changed.

But every time he read them, something inside his chest seemed to grow colder.

Finn sat beside him.

"Captain, you've been staring at that letter all night."

KOKI folded the paper.

"What if everything I remember isn't real?"

Finn frowned.

"What do you mean?"

"My childhood. My father. The day I first went to sea."

KOKI looked toward the dark water.

"What if someone put those memories inside my head?"

Finn was silent for a moment.

Then he reached into his coat and took out a battered telescope.

"Remember when we first met?"

KOKI nodded.

"You were terrible at steering."

Finn laughed.

"And you nearly got us both killed."

He placed the telescope in KOKI's hands.

"Whatever happened before that, I was there for everything after. And that was real."

KOKI smiled faintly.

Then the telescope began to glow.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Message in the Glass

A thin line of golden light spread across the telescope's lens.

Finn jumped to his feet.

"That's never happened before!"

Veyra approached, carrying the ancient star map.

The map was glowing too.

Its lines twisted into a new shape: a circle divided by a jagged crack.

Beneath it appeared three unfamiliar words.

"The Mirror of Origins."

Brann leaned over the map.

"Sounds like another place that wants to kill us."

"Probably," Veyra replied.

She turned the map toward the moon.

A beam of light escaped its surface and pointed toward the western sea.

There, between two enormous cliffs, stood a narrow island that had not existed on any chart they possessed.

At its center rose a black tower.

The tower had no windows.

Only a giant silver mirror facing the ocean.

KOKI felt the golden mark over his heart grow warm.

He put away the letter.

"Set a course for the tower."

Veyra studied his face.

"Are you sure?"

KOKI gripped the wheel.

"No."

Then he turned toward the island.

"But we're going anyway."

✦ ✦ ✦

The Island of Broken Reflections

They reached the island at dawn.

There were no birds.

No trees.

Not even the sound of waves.

The beach was covered with fragments of polished glass.

Each fragment reflected a different sky.

One showed a burning sunset.

Another showed a thousand stars.

A third showed a black sun hanging above an empty ocean.

Finn bent to examine a shard.

Inside it, he saw himself standing on the deck of a shattered ship.

He dropped it immediately.

"That wasn't my reflection."

Veyra examined the tower.

"These mirrors aren't showing what we are."

"They're showing what we might become."

Suddenly, the glass beneath them began to tremble.

A thousand reflections rose from the ground.

Each took the shape of a member of the crew.

A dozen Finns.

A dozen Veyras.

A dozen Branns.

And twelve KOKIs, each wearing a different expression.

One was laughing.

One was crying.

One wore a crown.

One carried a broken sword.

The final reflection wore the black coat of the Shadow Captain.

It stepped forward.

"Welcome home."

KOKI drew his sword.

The reflection smiled.

"Still using the same weapon."

It drew an identical sword.

"How disappointing."

Then all twelve reflections attacked.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Battle Against Tomorrow

Steel rang against steel.

KOKI blocked the first attack, ducked beneath the second, and struck the third reflection across its chest.

The figure shattered.

But its fragments immediately formed two new copies.

"Captain!" Brann shouted.

"Every time we break one, more appear!"

Veyra fired toward the tower.

The shot struck its silver surface.

For an instant, every reflection froze.

Finn noticed.

"They're connected to the mirror!"

KOKI looked up.

A golden symbol was engraved above the tower entrance.

The Thirteenth Light.

He ran toward it.

The Shadow Captain's reflection blocked his path.

Their swords collided.

The reflection knew every move KOKI attempted.

Every feint.

Every strike.

Every desperate trick he had learned at sea.

"You cannot defeat yourself," the reflection whispered.

KOKI stepped backward.

He glanced at Finn.

Then at Brann.

Then at Veyra, who was circling behind the enemy.

A smile appeared on his face.

"Maybe not."

He lowered his sword.

"But I'm not alone."

"Now, Veyra!"

Veyra struck the reflection from behind.

Brann smashed its sword arm.

Finn hurled a rope around its legs.

KOKI leaped forward and drove his sword through the golden symbol above the entrance.

The entire island flashed.

Every reflection shattered at once.

The tower doors opened.

And a voice spoke from the darkness within.

"At last."

"The captain has returned."

✦ ✦ ✦

The Mirror of Origins

Inside the tower, a single enormous mirror stood beneath a ceiling filled with moving stars.

Its frame was carved from black stone and gold.

Twelve chains hung around it.

Eleven glowed faintly.

One had turned completely dark.

KOKI approached.

His reflection appeared on the silver surface.

Then it changed.

His face grew older.

His coat became black.

A white beard appeared beneath his trunk.

The Shadow Captain stood inside the mirror.

KOKI raised his sword.

"Who are you?"

The older captain looked at him sadly.

"You already know."

"Are you my future?"

"One of them."

"Did you create me?"

The Shadow Captain closed his eye.

"For forty years, I believed I had."

KOKI stepped closer.

"What does that mean?"

The older captain placed his hand against the glass.

A memory appeared between them.

A golden chamber.

Twelve burning stars.

A frightened child holding a lantern.

KOKI recognized the scene from his vision in Auralis.

The Shadow Captain spoke.

"The Hollow King showed me a future in which every star was gathered together."

"I believed I could prevent it by separating the light from the captain who carried it."

He looked toward the child.

"I performed an ancient rite."

"A part of my memory was given a new beginning."

KOKI stared at him.

"Me?"

"That is what I believed."

The mirror trembled.

The older captain's expression changed.

"But the rite did not create a life."

"It only opened a door."

"And someone was already waiting on the other side."

KOKI felt his heart pound.

"Who?"

The Shadow Captain pressed both hands against the glass.

"That is the memory the Hollow King stole from us."
✦ ✦ ✦

The Captain Who Never Existed

The mirror suddenly filled with thousands of images.

KOKI saw his childhood village.

He saw his father.

He saw the first morning he had ever sailed.

Then he saw something impossible.

A record book inside an ancient royal archive.

Its pages contained the names of every captain who had crossed the Forgotten Sea.

The pages turned rapidly.

One name appeared.

KOKI.

Then the letters vanished.

The page became blank.

A voice echoed through the chamber.

"There was never a captain by that name."

Finn shouted.

"That's a lie!"

The mirror showed Finn meeting KOKI for the first time.

Then the image changed.

Finn stood alone on the same beach.

Veyra stepped forward.

"Stop showing us illusions!"

The mirror answered.

"These are not illusions."

"They are histories that were never allowed to happen."

KOKI lowered his sword.

The realization struck him.

The mirror was not showing that he had never existed.

It was showing worlds where he had never been born.

Worlds where Finn had drowned.

Where Veyra had remained cursed.

Where Brann had died defending an empty harbor.

And worlds where the Hollow King had already escaped.

The Shadow Captain spoke softly.

"I searched those histories for forty years."

"In every one, someone was lost."

KOKI looked at him.

"So you decided the only answer was sacrifice."

The older captain said nothing.

KOKI stepped closer.

"You looked at every possible future."

"But did you ever ask the people in those futures what they wanted?"

The Shadow Captain lowered his gaze.

KOKI placed his hand against the glass.

"You can show me a thousand worlds without me.

But I'm standing here in this one."

For the first time, the Shadow Captain smiled.

A small, exhausted smile.

"Perhaps that is why you succeeded where I failed."

Then the mirror turned black.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Prisoner's Reflection

A deep laugh filled the tower.

The golden stars above them began to disappear.

The Shadow Captain's image twisted into something monstrous.

A crown of black fire rose above his head.

His face dissolved into a hollow darkness.

The Hollow King had entered the mirror.

"Such touching words, little captain."

KOKI drew his sword.

"Get out of his reflection!"

The creature laughed.

"You still do not understand."

"Every time you seek the truth, you bring the Thirteenth Light closer to me."

The mirror's chains began snapping.

One.

Two.

Three.

Black hands burst from the silver surface.

They seized Finn and dragged him toward the glass.

"Captain!"

KOKI lunged forward.

Brann caught Finn's arm.

Veyra slashed through the black tendrils.

But more emerged.

The mirror began pulling the entire chamber inward.

KOKI looked at the golden symbol glowing over his heart.

The Thirteenth Light.

The Hollow King wanted it.

Then KOKI remembered the broken chain in Auralis.

He had nearly repaired it when his friends joined him.

The light had grown stronger when they acted together.

He turned to the crew.

"Everyone! Hold on to each other!"

Brann grabbed Finn.

Veyra seized Brann's shoulder.

KOKI took Veyra's hand.

Golden light spread between them.

The Hollow King recoiled.

"No!"

"That light belongs to me!"

KOKI stepped forward.

"It doesn't belong to anyone."

He raised his sword.

The golden light raced along its blade.

With one powerful strike, KOKI shattered the mirror's central black seal.

A deafening explosion shook the tower.

The Hollow King's image vanished into darkness.

The broken chains fell harmlessly to the floor.

And the mirror became still.

✦ ✦ ✦

A Promise Across Time

Only one fragment of the mirror remained.

Inside it, the Shadow Captain appeared again.

His image was fading.

"KOKI..."

The young captain knelt.

"Tell me where to find you."

The older captain shook his head.

"Not yet."

"The Hollow King has begun turning the First Star against its own seal."

KOKI remembered the darkening star he had seen in Auralis.

"Can we stop him?"

"Yes."

"But you must find the one who remembers what happened before the Thirteenth Light was divided."

"Who is that?"

The older captain's voice weakened.

"The Last Witness."

"Where?"

The Shadow Captain looked toward the sea.

"At the place where the first star fell."

KOKI leaned closer.

"One more question."

The older captain waited.

"If you really are a version of me..."

"Why did you save Finn when we fought?"

For a moment, the Shadow Captain looked almost young again.

"Because in my world, I was too late."

His image began to vanish.

"And because..."

He smiled sadly.

"...some promises survive even when memories do not."

The final fragment turned to silver dust.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Name Beneath the Stars

The tower began collapsing.

KOKI and his friends raced toward the entrance.

Brann carried a fallen beam off the staircase.

Veyra guided them through the raining debris.

Finn leaped across a widening crack.

KOKI was the last to escape.

Behind them, the black tower collapsed into the sea.

For a long time, no one spoke.

Then Finn turned toward KOKI.

"Captain?"

"Yes?"

"Are you all right?"

KOKI looked at his friends.

He thought of the blank record book.

The impossible memories.

The Shadow Captain.

And the thousands of worlds where he had never existed.

Then he smiled.

"I don't know everything about where I came from."

He placed a hand over the golden mark on his chest.

"But I know where I'm going."

Brann laughed.

"Into more trouble?"

"Almost certainly."

Veyra shook her head, but she was smiling too.

They returned to the ship.

KOKI unfolded the ancient star map.

A new destination had appeared upon it.

A dark crater in the middle of a silver sea.

Above it, a single star was falling.

Beneath the image were four words.

"The First Falling Star."

KOKI touched the symbol.

The golden mark on his chest pulsed.

Then a voice spoke.

Not from the sea.

Not from the sky.

But from somewhere deep inside his own memory.

It was the voice of a frightened child.

"Please...

Don't let him remember my real name."

KOKI froze.

He recognized the voice.

It was his own.

Far beneath the silver sea, something ancient opened its eyes.

A stone door, sealed for thousands of years, began to crack.

And behind that door, someone whispered:

"At last..."

"The Thirteenth Captain is coming home."
