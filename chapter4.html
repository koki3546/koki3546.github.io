THE KOKI CHRONICLES · CHAPTER 04

The Ghost Ship

Not every monster was born a monster.

The dead were waiting for them.

From the cliffs of the Forgotten Island, KOKI watched the Mourning Crown glide through a fleet of silent wrecks.

Its black sails moved without wind. Blue flames burned behind its windows. And on its highest deck stood a figure wearing a crown of broken silver.

Finn held the brass key so tightly that his paw began to shake.

"That ship was in my father's stories."

Captain Veyra drew her sword.

"Then your father knew more than he told you."

Below them, the ghost ship lowered a narrow wooden gangplank onto the shore.

KOKI frowned.

"It's inviting us aboard."

"It's a trap," Veyra replied.

KOKI looked at Finn. The young fox stared at the distant deck, his eyes filled with fear and hope.

"Then we'll walk into it together," KOKI said.

✦ ✦ ✦

The gangplank creaked beneath their feet.

The moment they stepped aboard, the bridge rose behind them and disappeared into the fog.

A hundred blue eyes opened across the darkness.

Skeleton pirates emerged from beneath the stairs, behind barrels, and between the hanging sails.

Their bones were wrapped in torn uniforms. Rusted swords trembled in their hands.

A towering skeleton wearing a cracked officer's hat stepped forward.

"Living souls are forbidden aboard the Mourning Crown."

Veyra raised her blade.

"Then come and remove us."

The skeleton officer pointed his sword.

"Crew! To arms!"

The deck erupted.

Veyra met the first attackers in a flash of silver. Finn ducked beneath a swinging cutlass. KOKI seized a loose rope and swung across the deck.

He struck two skeletons with his shoulder and sent them tumbling into a pile of barrels.

"Sorry!" he shouted. "But you started it!"

The skeletons rose again.

Broken bones pulled themselves together. Blue fire returned to their eyes.

KOKI stared.

"That's not fair."

✦ ✦ ✦

The battle drove them toward the captain's cabin.

KOKI slammed the door shut and pushed a heavy table against it.

Skeleton fists pounded the wood.

Finn searched the room. Old maps covered the walls. A broken hourglass lay beside a captain's journal.

On the journal's cover was the symbol of a bell.

"KOKI! Look!"

Finn opened the book.

The first pages were filled with unfamiliar names and dates.

Then he found a drawing of a small fox wearing a blue scarf.

Beneath it was written:

"My son Finn. If these pages survive, let him know I did not abandon him."

Finn stopped breathing.

His paws trembled over the words.

"Father..."

Veyra slowly lowered her sword.

For the first time, her voice lost its sharpness.

"His name was Rowan."

Finn looked up.

"You knew him."

Veyra nodded.

"He was my navigator. And my oldest friend."

Before Finn could ask another question, the cabin door exploded inward.

✦ ✦ ✦

The skeleton officer stepped through the shattered doorway.

His sword glowed with blue fire.

"The journal belongs to the dead."

He charged.

KOKI grabbed a chair and blocked the first strike.

The chair shattered.

The officer struck again. KOKI stumbled backward and fell beside an open chest.

Inside were hundreds of small objects: lockets, letters, wooden toys, faded portraits, and wedding rings.

One photograph showed the skeleton officer as a living sailor, standing beside a smiling little girl.

KOKI picked it up.

"Is this your daughter?"

The officer froze.

For a moment, the blue fire in his eyes flickered.

"Her name..."

His sword lowered.

"Her name was Elara."

KOKI held out the photograph.

"She must have been waiting for you to come home."

The officer stared at the picture.

His skeletal fingers reached toward it.

"I promised her... I would return before winter."

The sword slipped from his hand.

Outside the cabin, the other skeletons stopped fighting.

For a brief moment, the ship became silent.

✦ ✦ ✦

KOKI stepped onto the deck, holding the photograph.

Hundreds of cursed sailors watched him through the fog.

He raised his voice.

"You were pirates once. But before that, you were someone's family."

The sailors did not move.

"Someone loved you. Someone waited for you. And I don't believe you wanted to become monsters."

The officer stood beside him.

"We didn't."

He looked toward the highest deck.

"We were ordered to ring the bell. When we refused, the king chained our souls to this ship."

Finn stepped forward.

"What king?"

The officer's jaw trembled.

"The king who drowned an entire sea to escape death."

A terrible sound rolled through the ship.

The enormous bell above the mast began to swing.

Every skeleton fell to its knees.

Blue chains erupted from the deck and wrapped around their bodies.

The officer screamed.

"The bell! Stop the bell!"

KOKI looked up.

The bell hung above the mainmast, far beyond his reach.

He could escape into the sea.

He could leave the cursed sailors to their suffering.

But he remembered the photograph.

He remembered Finn, trapped beneath the wreckage.

He remembered the promise he had made on the Forgotten Island.

"A captain doesn't leave his crew behind."

Veyra stared at him.

"They're not your crew!"

KOKI grabbed a hanging rope.

"Then maybe it's time somebody treated them like one!"

✦ ✦ ✦

KOKI climbed the rigging as blue lightning tore across the sails.

Below, Veyra fought to keep the chains from reaching Finn.

The fox held the brass key against the journal.

Its pages began to glow.

"KOKI!" Finn shouted. "There's a lock above the bell!"

KOKI reached the highest beam.

A black iron box hung above the swinging bell.

Inside the box, something beat like a heart.

KOKI pulled at the lock.

It would not move.

The bell swung toward him.

He had seconds.

Finn threw the brass key upward.

KOKI caught it with his trunk, pushed it into the lock, and turned.

The iron box burst open.

A blue flame rose into the air.

The bell stopped.

Across the deck, the chains fell apart.

For one beautiful moment, the cursed sailors looked human again.

The officer gazed at the photograph in his hands.

"Elara..."

Then the blue light faded, and the sailors became skeletons once more. But their eyes no longer burned.

The officer bowed to KOKI.

"You have given us silence for the first time in a century."

KOKI climbed down, exhausted and shaking.

"Then let's find a way to give you freedom."

✦ ✦ ✦

The officer led them to the captain's journal.

Its final pages had been hidden beneath a false cover.

Finn opened them carefully.

A message from Rowan had been written in fading ink.

"The Bell is not a weapon. It is a prison. And the thing beneath the sea is not its prisoner. It is its guardian."

Veyra went pale.

Finn turned the page.

There was a map.

At its center stood a mountain shaped like a crown.

Beneath it were four words:

"Find the Last Compass."

Suddenly, footsteps echoed from the upper deck.

The crowned figure who had watched them from above descended the staircase.

Its face was hidden behind a silver mask.

In one hand it held a blade made of black glass.

In the other, a compass whose needle pointed straight at Finn.

"Rowan's son," the figure whispered.

Finn stepped backward.

The figure raised its sword.

"At last."
