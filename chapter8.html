THE KOKI CHRONICLES · CHAPTER 08

The Captain's Sacrifice

Courage is not the absence of fear. It is the choice to stay when leaving would be easier.

The fire had nowhere left to go.

It climbed the mast, swallowed the sails, and raced along the deck toward KOKI and the fallen raven captain.

The red chain around Veyra's wrist tightened with every beat of her heart.

"Leave me," she whispered.

KOKI pulled at the chain.

"No."

Sparks scattered over his hat. He stamped them out and pulled again.

"You don't understand. Morcant bound the chain to my life. If it breaks, he takes my soul."

KOKI stopped.

Across the storm, the crimson eye above the fortress watched them.

Morcant's voice drifted through the flames.

"Such loyalty. How touching."

The chain began to glow.

"You may save her, little captain. But the debt must belong to someone."

✦ ✦ ✦

On Brann's ship, Finn watched the burning vessel tilt toward the waves.

"We have to go back!"

Brann gripped the wheel.

"If we move closer, the flames will take us too!"

Finn looked down at the Last Compass.

Its needle pointed toward KOKI.

Then it turned toward the red chain.

A second needle appeared, black as midnight.

Finn remembered Rowan's journal.

He opened the soaked pages and searched desperately.

There, beneath a drawing of three broken circles, he found a warning.

"A life-bound chain cannot be destroyed while its master holds the debt. It may only be transferred by one who accepts its burden freely."

Finn's heart sank.

"KOKI... don't."

✦ ✦ ✦

KOKI heard Morcant laugh.

"Take her place. Wear my chain. Or watch her burn."

Veyra seized KOKI's arm.

"Don't listen to him."

Her voice broke.

"I betrayed Rowan. I betrayed Finn. I betrayed you. This is my punishment."

KOKI looked at her.

He remembered the stolen key, the broken deck, and the long night in the water.

He was still angry.

He did not know if he could forgive her.

But he remembered something else.

Veyra standing between Morcant and Finn's helpless body.

Her silver sword trembling, yet refusing to fall.

"You were wrong," KOKI said.

Veyra closed her eyes.

"I know."

"And saving you doesn't make what you did right."

He reached for the chain.

"But I won't let him decide that you can never become better."

Veyra shook her head.

"KOKI, please!"

The little elephant wrapped the burning chain around his wrist.

"Take me instead."

✦ ✦ ✦

The world turned red.

Fire exploded from the chain.

Veyra screamed as the metal dissolved from her wrist.

The links twisted through the air and locked around KOKI's arm.

Pain tore through him.

He fell to one knee.

The red eye in the sky widened.

"A willing sacrifice," Morcant whispered.

His laughter rolled across the sea.

"How rare."

KOKI tried to stand.

His legs trembled.

Dark markings spread beneath the chain.

Veyra caught him.

"What have you done?"

KOKI managed a weak smile.

"Something stupid, probably."

The deck cracked beneath them.

They were falling.

✦ ✦ ✦

A great wave rose from the darkness.

Thalor burst from the sea and caught the sinking wreck upon his enormous back.

Finn leaped from Brann's vessel onto the guardian's armored scales.

"KOKI!"

He ran across the broken planks.

KOKI lay beside Veyra, his eyes half-closed.

Finn dropped to his knees.

"What happened?"

He saw the chain.

The little fox went silent.

Then he began to shake.

"You promised we would get through this together."

KOKI reached toward him.

"We still will."

Finn pulled away.

"You don't know that!"

Tears streamed down his face.

"You keep saving everyone. But who's supposed to save you?"

KOKI had no answer.

He had never thought about that question before.

✦ ✦ ✦

Thalor carried them beyond the burning ships to a sheltered stretch of water.

Brann's vessel followed, its sails torn but still standing.

Around them, the ghost fleet formed a protective circle.

Veyra knelt beside KOKI.

"The chain is not merely a curse," she said.

She looked at the red links.

"It is a command. When Morcant calls, the bearer must answer."

Brann stared at her.

"You mean he can control KOKI?"

Veyra nodded.

"If the chain reaches his heart, yes."

Finn clutched the Last Compass.

"Then we break it before that happens."

Thalor lowered his great head.

"There may be one way."

The guardian's golden eyes reflected the compass.

"The chain was forged from the second seal. Its power can be undone only where that seal was made."

Veyra looked up sharply.

"The Hollow Lighthouse."

Thalor nodded.

"At the edge of the Drowned Kingdom."

Finn opened Rowan's journal.

A faded map unfolded from its final pages.

At the center stood a lonely lighthouse, surrounded by black reefs.

Beneath the drawing was a name.

Finn whispered it aloud.

"Rowan."

✦ ✦ ✦

Veyra slowly took the journal.

Her hands trembled.

"He was there."

Finn stared at her.

"My father?"

Veyra nodded.

"Before Morcant captured him, Rowan reached the lighthouse. He believed the second seal could be used to close the prison."

She turned the page.

A sentence had been written in Rowan's familiar handwriting.

"If the light goes dark, remember the stars."

Finn pressed the journal against his chest.

For a moment, he was no longer the brave little navigator who had faced the ghost fleet.

He was simply a child missing his father.

"He always said that to me."

KOKI looked at him.

Finn smiled through his tears.

"When I was afraid of the dark, he would take me outside and show me the stars."

His voice grew quiet.

"He said that even when we couldn't see the road, the sky still remembered it."

KOKI reached out and placed his unchained hand on Finn's shoulder.

Neither spoke.

✦ ✦ ✦

At dawn, the surviving ships gathered beneath a pale sky.

The Harrower had vanished into the deep.

The crimson fortress remained on the horizon, pulsing with stolen power.

Brann called the captains together.

Living sailors stood beside silent skeleton officers.

Some had fought each other only hours earlier.

Now they waited for the same young elephant to speak.

KOKI stood at the bow, his chained arm hidden beneath his coat.

He looked across the fleet.

"I can't promise that everyone will survive."

A murmur passed through the gathered crews.

"I can't promise that we will win."

He looked at Finn.

Then at Veyra.

"But I can promise that no one here will be treated as disposable."

The ghost officer from the Mourning Crown stepped forward.

"And what of you, Captain?"

KOKI frowned.

"Me?"

The officer raised his skeletal hand.

"Who will protect you?"

Before KOKI could answer, Finn stepped beside him.

"We will."

Brann placed his telescope against his chest.

"Every ship here."

The ghost officer lowered his head.

"Every soul."

One by one, the captains raised their swords, lanterns, and weathered flags.

KOKI stared at them.

He had sailed away from home dreaming of becoming a great pirate.

He had imagined treasure.

Battles.

Songs sung in his honor.

He had never imagined that the greatest treasure he would find was a crew willing to stand beside him.

✦ ✦ ✦

Far away, inside the crimson fortress, Morcant stood before a black stone altar.

The Shadow Compass floated above it.

Its needle trembled.

Then turned toward KOKI.

Morcant smiled.

"So the little captain has accepted my chain."

Behind him, a figure stirred within a prison of blue glass.

A fox.

Older than Finn.

His fur was streaked with silver, and his eyes were closed.

Morcant approached the glass.

"Your son has grown, Navigator."

The prisoner did not move.

Morcant raised the Shadow Compass.

"And now his captain will bring me the second seal."

Inside the glass, Rowan's eyes opened.

His voice was faint, but steady.

"You still don't understand what makes a captain."

Morcant leaned closer.

"Then enlighten me."

Rowan looked toward the distant sea.

"A captain isn't the one who commands everyone to follow."

He smiled sadly.

"A captain is the one they choose to follow when they are free to leave."

Morcant's smile vanished.

He struck the glass with his armored fist.

The chamber shook.

Rowan closed his eyes.

And far away, aboard Brann's ship, the red chain around KOKI's wrist began to tighten.

KOKI staggered.

Finn caught him.

"KOKI!"

The Last Compass flashed gold.

Then its needle turned toward the Hollow Lighthouse.

From the darkness inside KOKI's mind, Morcant whispered:

"Come to me, little captain."
