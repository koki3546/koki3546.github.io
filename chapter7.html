THE KOKI CHRONICLES · CHAPTER 07

The Broken Seal

When the sea rises against the world, even the smallest captain must stand.

The third bell did not fade.

Its sound spread through the ocean, through the ruins of the City of Tides, and into places where sunlight had never reached.

KOKI stood in the ancient temple, the Last Compass trembling between his hands.

A crack of red light tore across the ceiling.

"Thalor! What's happening?"

The guardian's enormous golden eyes turned toward the darkness.

"The first seal has fallen."

Finn pressed his paws against the shaking floor.

"How many seals are there?"

Thalor hesitated.

"Three."

The answer was followed by a distant scream.

It came from the sea itself.

✦ ✦ ✦

Thalor carried them upward through the collapsing ruins.

A great sphere of air surrounded KOKI and Finn as the ocean rushed past.

When they broke the surface, the world had changed.

The sky was crimson.

Lightning crawled between clouds shaped like enormous hands.

Across the horizon, ships were rising from the deep.

Dozens.

Then hundreds.

Their sails were torn, their hulls covered in coral, and their decks crowded with pale figures.

Finn stared in horror.

"The lost fleet..."

Thalor nodded.

"The first seal held the drowned ships beneath the Forgotten Sea. Now the curse has called them home."

KOKI looked toward the approaching vessels.

"Can we stop them?"

Thalor's voice was heavy.

"Not by destroying them. Their souls are prisoners, not enemies."

KOKI remembered the officer aboard the Mourning Crown.

He remembered the photograph of a little girl named Elara.

Those sailors had once been fathers, mothers, brothers, and daughters.

He tightened his grip on the compass.

"Then we'll save them."

✦ ✦ ✦

A thunderous roar split the sky.

Something enormous rose between the ships.

It had six twisting arms, a body covered in black armor, and a mouth filled with glowing red teeth.

Its tentacles crushed two ghost ships together.

Splintered wood rained across the waves.

Finn stumbled backward.

"What is that?"

Thalor lowered his head.

"A Harrower. A creature born from the prison's oldest nightmares."

The monster turned toward a smaller vessel struggling to escape the storm.

Its deck was crowded with frightened sailors.

Living sailors.

KOKI pointed.

"That ship! We have to help!"

Thalor surged forward.

The sea rose around him like a moving mountain.

KOKI and Finn held tight as the guardian struck the monster's nearest arm.

The impact shook the ocean.

The Harrower screamed.

But its other arms wrapped around Thalor.

Red chains spread across the guardian's golden markings.

"KOKI!" Thalor thundered. "I cannot hold it forever!"

KOKI looked at the trapped ship.

Its mast was burning.

Its sailors were screaming.

He could not reach them from Thalor's back.

Unless...

He spotted a loose length of wreckage floating nearby.

"Finn! The compass!"

Finn held out the golden instrument.

Its needle spun wildly.

Then it pointed toward the burning vessel.

KOKI smiled grimly.

"That's where we're going."

✦ ✦ ✦

KOKI leaped from Thalor's back onto the drifting wreckage.

Finn followed, landing beside him.

A wave lifted their makeshift raft toward the burning ship.

KOKI caught a hanging rope and climbed aboard.

The vessel's captain, an old sea turtle with a cracked brass telescope, stared at him.

"Who in the seven seas are you?"

KOKI straightened his hat.

"Captain KOKI."

The turtle looked at the little elephant.

Then at the burning mast.

"Captain of what?"

KOKI hesitated.

He had no ship.

No crew beyond Finn.

Not even a proper sword.

But the sailors were waiting for an answer.

"Right now?"

He grabbed a bucket and threw water over the burning ropes.

"Captain of anyone who wants to survive!"

The turtle laughed once.

Then he grabbed another bucket.

"Name's Brann. And you've got yourself a ship, Captain."

✦ ✦ ✦

Together they fought the fire.

Finn climbed the rigging and cut away the burning sail.

KOKI helped the wounded below deck.

Captain Brann steered between the rising wrecks.

But the danger was growing.

Ghost ships surrounded them.

Their cannons turned toward the living vessel.

Blue flames gathered inside the barrels.

"They're going to fire!" Brann shouted.

KOKI raised the Last Compass.

The needle spun toward the nearest ghost ship.

He remembered Thalor's words.

The sailors were prisoners.

Not enemies.

He climbed onto the railing.

"Listen to me!"

His voice disappeared into the storm.

The cannons continued turning.

KOKI shouted again.

"You don't have to fight us!"

Nothing changed.

Then Finn stepped beside him.

He raised the journal they had recovered from the Mourning Crown.

"We know about the Bell!"

Several ghost sailors stopped moving.

Finn opened the journal to Rowan's final message.

"The Bell is a prison. And we know how to find its heart!"

One by one, the ghost ships lowered their cannons.

A voice rose from the nearest deck.

"Can you truly free us?"

KOKI looked at the sailors.

He wanted to promise them that he could.

But he remembered how easily promises had been broken.

"I don't know yet."

The sailors fell silent.

"But I will try. And I won't use you as weapons while I do."

For a moment, nothing moved.

Then the nearest ghost ship turned its cannons away from Brann's vessel.

It fired at the Harrower.

✦ ✦ ✦

Blue cannon fire struck the monster's armored back.

The Harrower released one of Thalor's fins.

Another ghost ship fired.

Then another.

The entire fleet began to turn.

Brann stared in disbelief.

"You've just convinced a fleet of ghosts to fight beside us."

KOKI gripped the railing.

"Then let's give them a reason to believe."

He climbed beside Brann at the wheel.

"Can your ship signal the others?"

Brann nodded.

"With lanterns. If the storm doesn't swallow the light."

KOKI looked at Finn.

"We have a golden compass. Let's make some light."

Finn raised the instrument.

Its eight points glowed.

A column of gold rose above the ship.

The ghost fleet answered with blue lanterns.

For the first time, hundreds of lost vessels moved together under a single command.

"All ships!" KOKI shouted.

"Protect the living! Keep the monster away from the smaller vessels!"

Brann repeated the orders through his speaking trumpet.

The fleet divided.

Some ships formed a barrier around the wounded.

Others circled the Harrower, firing at its exposed arms.

Thalor tore free of the remaining chains.

The guardian surged upward and struck the creature with all his strength.

The Harrower fell backward.

The ocean swallowed it.

For one heartbeat, the storm grew quiet.

✦ ✦ ✦

Then the sea exploded.

The Harrower rose again, larger than before.

Its shattered armor had begun to repair itself.

A red mark burned across its forehead.

Thalor recoiled.

"Morcant is feeding it the power of the broken seal!"

KOKI looked toward the crimson horizon.

Far beyond the fleet, a jagged fortress rose from the sea.

Red lightning circled its highest tower.

The Last Compass pointed directly toward it.

"That's where the power is coming from!"

Finn grabbed the railing.

"The first seal..."

KOKI nodded.

"If we can't stop the power feeding it, we'll never defeat that thing."

Brann turned the wheel.

"Then we sail for the fortress!"

But a shadow crossed the deck.

A massive tentacle rose above them.

KOKI looked up.

There was no time to turn the ship.

The tentacle fell.

And a silver flash cut through the rain.

✦ ✦ ✦

The monster's arm split against a blade.

Black blood scattered across the waves.

A dark ship swept between Brann's vessel and the Harrower.

Its sails were crimson.

A black raven stood at the prow, her silver sword raised toward the storm.

Finn gasped.

"Veyra!"

KOKI could not speak.

The raven captain looked older than before.

One wing was wrapped in bloodstained bandages.

A dark chain circled her wrist.

But she was alive.

Veyra met KOKI's eyes across the crashing water.

"You really are impossible to kill, little elephant."

KOKI felt anger rise in his chest.

He remembered her betrayal.

The stolen key.

Finn's terrified cries.

And their long night alone in the sea.

"Why are you here?"

Veyra lowered her sword.

"Because I made a terrible mistake."

She looked at Finn.

"And because Rowan trusted me with something I should have protected."

Finn stepped forward.

"My father?"

Veyra nodded.

"I know where the second seal is."

She raised her chained wrist.

The metal glowed red.

"But Morcant knows where I am now."

✦ ✦ ✦

The chain tightened.

Veyra cried out.

A red symbol appeared across her chest.

Above the distant fortress, a great eye opened in the clouds.

Morcant's voice rolled across the fleet.

"My faithful raven."

Veyra fell to one knee.

"You were warned what would happen if you disobeyed me."

The crimson sails began to burn.

KOKI stared at the approaching flames.

Brann seized his shoulder.

"Captain! We have to move! That ship is going down!"

Finn looked at KOKI.

The little fox said nothing.

He did not ask KOKI to save her.

He did not ask him to leave her.

KOKI watched Veyra struggle beneath the chain.

He could still remember the moment she handed his friend's key to Morcant.

He could still feel the cold of the sea after their ship was lost.

Forgiveness was not something he could offer with a single word.

But the flames were climbing higher.

KOKI turned to Brann.

"Bring us alongside."

Brann stared at him.

"She betrayed you!"

KOKI nodded.

"I know."

He looked toward the burning ship.

"But I won't let Morcant decide who deserves to live."

Finn stepped beside him.

"Then I'm coming too."

KOKI shook his head.

"Stay with Brann. Keep the compass safe."

He grabbed a rope and climbed onto the railing.

The burning ship rocked violently.

KOKI looked once toward the crimson fortress.

Then he jumped.

✦ ✦ ✦

He landed hard on Veyra's deck.

Fire surrounded them.

The raven captain struggled to stand.

"You shouldn't have come."

KOKI grabbed the chain around her wrist.

"You can tell me that later."

He pulled.

The chain did not move.

Veyra looked at him with exhausted eyes.

"It's bound to my life. If you break it..."

She stopped.

KOKI looked up.

"What happens?"

Veyra's voice was barely a whisper.

"Morcant takes what remains of me."

Above them, the red eye widened.

The ship lurched.

A burning mast collapsed across their only escape.

KOKI wrapped his trunk around Veyra.

The sea waited below.

The flames closed in.

And somewhere beneath the waves, the second bell began to stir.
