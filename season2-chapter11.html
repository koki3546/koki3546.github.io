THE KOKI CHRONICLES · SEASON TWO
CHAPTER 11

The Thirteenth Door

Some doors are opened by keys. Others are opened by the things we cannot bear to lose.

The first scream came from the sky.

It was not the cry of a bird, nor the howl of a storm. It was the sound of a thousand forgotten voices speaking at once, each calling for something the world had taken away.

Above KOKI's ship, the Thirteenth Door stretched across the heavens.

Its edges burned with ancient golden symbols. Beyond its black center, enormous shapes moved like mountains beneath a dark ocean.

Then a hand pressed against the opening.

Five fingers, each longer than a warship, curled around the edge of the door.

The Hollow King was trying to enter the world.

Finn clung to the wheel.

"Captain! The wind's changing direction every few seconds!"

"Keep us moving!" KOKI shouted.

"There is nowhere to move!"

Finn pointed toward the horizon.

The sea had risen into a wall.

Not a wave.

A wall of water stretching from one end of the world to the other.

At its center stood a fleet of ships made from broken memories.

Their sails were woven from shadows. Their hulls carried the faces of people no one remembered.

And their cannons were turning toward KOKI's ship.

✦ ✦ ✦

I. The Fleet of Forgotten Names

The first cannon fired.

A black sphere crossed the sea without making a sound.

It struck the water beside the ship.

Nothing exploded.

Instead, every sailor on deck forgot what they had been doing.

Finn released the wheel.

Brann lowered his sword.

Veyra stared at the horizon, confused.

Milo looked up at KOKI.

"Who are you?"

The question struck KOKI harder than the cannon.

He remembered the First Star.

He remembered choosing to surrender the memory of his true name forever.

But he still remembered his friends.

And he refused to let the Hollow King take that from them.

KOKI grabbed Milo's shoulders.

"I'm KOKI."

The boy blinked.

"I'm your captain."

Another cannon fired.

KOKI lifted Milo's wooden ship high above his head.

"And you made this for me!"

A golden light burst from the toy.

The black cannonball shattered before reaching the deck.

Milo gasped.

"Captain KOKI!"

He threw his arms around him.

Across the deck, the crew's memories returned.

Lyra stared at the wooden ship.

"That's it," she whispered.

"What is?" KOKI asked.

"The King can steal memories. But he cannot easily destroy the bonds those memories created."

Veyra drew her sword.

"Then let's remind him who he's fighting."

Brann grinned.

"Finally. Something I understand."

He loaded the ship's largest cannon.

Finn seized the wheel again.

The ship turned sharply between two black projectiles.

"Ready!" Brann shouted.

KOKI raised his sword.

"FIRE!"

A golden cannonball tore through the nearest shadow ship.

Its hull split apart.

For a moment, hundreds of faces appeared in the air.

Then the faces smiled.

And vanished into starlight.

The crew cheered.

But beyond the destroyed vessel, hundreds more were approaching.

✦ ✦ ✦

II. The Woman Beyond the Door

Deep beneath the Forgotten Kingdoms, Nox ran through a corridor that had not existed an hour earlier.

The golden door stood ahead.

Beyond it, a woman waited between two small cradles.

Her face remained hidden beneath a veil of light.

"Mother?" Nox called.

She did not answer.

Instead, she sang the same lullaby he had heard in the broken palace.

Nox stepped through the doorway.

The world changed.

He stood in a garden beneath a sky filled with thirteen stars.

Two children played beside a fountain.

Both were young gray elephants.

One laughed as he pushed a little wooden boat through the water.

The other watched from the shadows.

Nox approached them.

The laughing child looked up.

For a moment, Nox thought he was seeing KOKI.

Then the garden split apart.

Two different skies appeared.

In one, the child sailed toward a bright horizon.

In the other, the harbor burned.

The woman finally spoke.

"They were never one child divided into two."

Nox turned toward her.

"They were two real lives, born in two possible histories."

"Then why do we remember the same lullaby?"

"Because some things survive even when histories are separated."

She lifted her veil.

Her face was gentle, but translucent.

Nox understood.

She was not truly standing there.

She was an echo.

A preserved memory.

"Are you our mother?"

"In one history, I raised you. In another, I raised him. Those lives were real, though they were not the same life."

Nox lowered his head.

"Can you come back?"

The woman smiled sadly.

"No."

He closed his eyes.

Forty years of searching.

Forty years of obedience to the Hollow King.

All for a reunion that could never truly happen.

"Then why show me this?"

"Because you have mistaken remembering someone for bringing them back."

She placed a hand against his cheek.

"You cannot undo every loss. But you can decide what your love becomes."

Nox began to cry.

The woman pointed toward the thirteen stars.

"The King has lied about the Door."

Nox looked up.

"It does not lead to a kingdom where the lost are waiting."

"Then where does it lead?"

Her expression darkened.

"To the place where all erased histories are held before they disappear forever."

The stars flickered.

"If the King reaches that place, he will choose which histories are allowed to exist."

Nox stepped backward.

"How do we stop him?"

The woman looked toward the two cradles.

"The Door cannot be closed by one history alone."

Nox understood.

"KOKI."

She nodded.

"And you."

✦ ✦ ✦

III. The Battle Above the Sea

The shadow fleet surrounded KOKI's ship.

Brann fired cannon after cannon.

Veyra swung from the rigging onto an enemy vessel, cutting through the chains that bound its forgotten sailors.

Finn steered between burning wrecks.

Lyra stood at the bow, holding the star map against the violent wind.

Eleven pale stars trembled on the parchment.

Only the First Star shone gold.

"The seals are failing!" she cried.

KOKI looked toward the sky.

The Hollow King's second hand had appeared through the Door.

The darkness surrounding him was taking the shape of a colossal crowned body.

A voice thundered over the ocean.

"KOKI."

The crew froze.

"YOU WHO THREW AWAY YOUR TRUE NAME."

KOKI raised his sword.

"I didn't throw it away."

"YOU GAVE IT UP FOR NOTHING."

KOKI looked at Milo.

The boy was helping Brann reload the cannons.

Then he looked at Finn, Veyra and Lyra.

"No," KOKI said. "I gave it up for them."

The Hollow King laughed.

"THEN WATCH THEM DISAPPEAR."

A vast black wave rose behind the shadow fleet.

Within the wave, thousands of glowing windows appeared.

Each showed a different life.

Finn saw a harbor where his family had never left him.

Brann saw his brother alive, standing beside a warm fire.

Veyra saw a world where she had never betrayed anyone.

Lyra saw a peaceful city where no child ever grew old.

And Milo saw himself standing beside his parents, safe from every storm.

The crew stopped fighting.

The Hollow King whispered.

"ALL OF IT CAN BE YOURS."

KOKI felt the temptation too.

He saw a small house beside a quiet sea.

A family waiting at the door.

A childhood without fear.

And a face he almost recognized.

His heart ached.

For a moment, he wanted to step into that vision more than anything in the world.

Then he heard Milo's voice.

"Captain?"

KOKI looked down.

The boy was crying.

"Is that really my family?"

KOKI knelt beside him.

"I don't know."

"Could we go there?"

KOKI looked toward the glowing windows.

"Maybe."

Milo waited.

"But the King isn't offering us a place to visit," KOKI continued. "He's asking us to abandon everyone who is here."

Milo wiped his eyes.

"Even you?"

"Even me."

The boy took KOKI's hand.

"Then I don't want to go."

The nearest window shattered.

One by one, the crew turned away from the visions.

Brann wiped his face.

"My brother wouldn't want me to leave my friends behind."

Veyra raised her sword.

"I can't erase what I've done. But I can still choose what I do next."

Finn seized the wheel.

"And I choose to keep sailing!"

The black wave faltered.

For the first time, the Hollow King sounded angry.

"THEN YOU SHALL ALL BE FORGOTTEN."

✦ ✦ ✦

IV. The Return of Nox

A silver blade cut through the sky.

The black wave split in two.

A figure fell from the Thirteenth Door, wrapped in silver fire.

He landed on the deck with enough force to crack the wood.

Brann raised his sword.

Then he stopped.

"Nox?"

The other captain slowly stood.

His coat was torn.

His black sword glowed silver.

One eye remained crimson.

The other burned gold.

KOKI stared at him.

"You're alive."

Nox smiled faintly.

"You sound disappointed."

KOKI laughed despite the danger.

"Not even a little."

Nox looked toward the sky.

"We have a problem."

"Only one?" Finn shouted.

"The King isn't trying to enter our world."

Lyra lowered the star map.

"Then what is he doing?"

"He's trying to reach the Archive of Lost Histories."

Nox pointed toward the Door.

"Every history erased by the Hollow King is held beyond that opening. If he takes control of the Archive, he can decide which worlds are remembered and which disappear."

KOKI looked toward the thousands of glowing windows.

"Then all those people..."

"Are real possibilities. Real lives that once existed or might have existed in other histories."

Nox's voice broke.

"My world is there."

KOKI placed a hand on his shoulder.

"Then we'll save it."

Nox shook his head.

"We can't restore every history without tearing the world apart."

"Then what can we do?"

Nox looked at the golden First Star.

"We can stop the King from erasing them forever."

Lyra studied the map.

"The twelve stars are seals. But the Thirteenth Light was never a seal."

"What was it?" KOKI asked.

"A bridge."

She turned toward Nox.

"A bridge between histories."

Nox nodded.

"And the King needs both of us to control it."

KOKI frowned.

"Why?"

"Because we came from two different histories through the same broken passage."

Nox raised his sword.

"We can open the way into the Archive."

He looked at KOKI.

"And perhaps close it from the other side."

✦ ✦ ✦

V. The Bridge of Thirteen Stars

Lyra spread the star map across the deck.

Its ancient symbols rearranged themselves.

The eleven pale stars formed a circle around the First.

At the center, the black Thirteenth Point pulsed.

"The First Seal is restored," Lyra said. "The others are damaged, but not completely destroyed."

She traced the lines with her fingers.

"If we can connect them for even a few moments, we may be able to form a path through the Door."

Brann looked upward.

"Through that?"

"Yes."

"Of course."

He sighed.

"Why is it never a nice ordinary bridge?"

The crew prepared the ship.

Finn secured the wheel.

Veyra gathered the remaining silver lanterns.

Brann reinforced the damaged mast.

Milo tied his strongest knot around the main sail.

KOKI and Nox stood at the bow.

Two captains.

Two swords.

Two histories that should never have met.

Nox looked at KOKI.

"Are you afraid?"

KOKI smiled.

"Terrified."

"Good."

Nox raised his sword.

"So am I."

They struck their blades together.

Gold and silver light rose into the sky.

The First Star blazed.

Then a second star flickered.

A third.

A fourth.

One by one, the eleven pale stars answered the call.

They did not become gold.

But thin threads of light connected them.

A bridge appeared between the ship and the Thirteenth Door.

It was made of starlight.

And it was breaking almost as quickly as it formed.

Finn looked at KOKI.

"Captain?"

KOKI raised his sword.

"Full speed ahead!"

The ship surged forward.

Its bow lifted from the water.

For one impossible moment, the vessel sailed into the sky.

The shadow fleet fired everything it had.

Black cannonballs raced upward.

Veyra and Brann fought to keep them away.

Lyra held the star map high.

Milo clung to the mast.

And Finn steered the ship along a bridge made from twelve trembling stars.

✦ ✦ ✦

VI. Inside the Thirteenth Door

The moment the ship crossed the threshold, the ocean vanished.

There was no sky.

No horizon.

No wind.

Only an endless darkness filled with floating islands of light.

Each island contained a world.

KOKI saw cities built on clouds.

Forests where trees grew upside down.

Oceans filled with golden ships.

And a thousand unfamiliar stars.

Some worlds were peaceful.

Others were burning.

Some contained people who looked almost exactly like his own crew.

But their lives were different.

In one world, Finn was a king.

In another, Brann was a farmer with three children.

In another, Veyra was a teacher surrounded by laughing students.

Milo pointed toward a small floating island.

There, a young elephant stood on the deck of a beautiful white ship.

He wore no eyepatch.

He was smiling.

KOKI watched him for a long moment.

"Is that me?"

Nox shook his head.

"Another possible life."

KOKI nodded slowly.

The sight filled him with a strange sadness.

But also wonder.

There were countless ways a life might unfold.

And he had only lived one.

Then the islands began to shake.

A black crown appeared at the center of the Archive.

The Hollow King was waiting.

His body was enormous, formed from shadows and broken fragments of forgotten worlds.

In his chest, the Second Heart beat like a black sun.

"YOU HAVE BROUGHT ME THE FINAL KEYS."

KOKI raised his sword.

"We came to stop you."

The King laughed.

"YOU CAME BECAUSE YOU COULD NOT ACCEPT LOSS."

He extended one hand.

The floating islands moved.

Thousands of worlds began falling toward his chest.

"AND NOW YOU WILL WATCH EVERY HISTORY BECOME MINE."

✦ ✦ ✦

VII. The King of Nothing

KOKI leapt from the bow.

His sword flashed gold as he struck the King's hand.

The blade passed through shadow.

The King barely noticed.

Nox attacked from the opposite side.

His silver sword cut through the darkness, leaving a bright wound.

The King roared.

For a moment, the Second Heart faltered.

Then the wound closed.

"YOU CANNOT DESTROY ME."

KOKI landed on a floating island.

"Then we'll find another way."

The King struck.

A wave of black fire swept across the Archive.

Finn steered the ship between two falling worlds.

Brann fired a cannon directly into the King's chest.

The blast exposed the black crystal Heart.

Lyra shouted.

"That's his weakness!"

Nox looked toward the crystal.

"No."

KOKI turned.

"What?"

"The Heart isn't his weakness."

Nox's face was pale.

"It's his prison."

The King laughed.

"AT LAST, YOU UNDERSTAND."

The black crystal opened.

Inside it, a small figure sat alone.

A child.

Not an elephant.

Not a pirate.

A child made of fading stars.

He looked frightened.

KOKI lowered his sword.

"Who is that?"

The Hollow King's voice became strangely quiet.

"THE FIRST ONE FORGOTTEN."

The child looked up.

His eyes were filled with tears.

"I only wanted someone to remember me."

KOKI stared.

The enormous King was not merely a monster born from darkness.

He was a creature who had learned to erase others because he could not bear being erased himself.

But understanding his pain did not excuse what he had done.

KOKI stepped forward.

"You don't have to destroy worlds to be remembered."

The King's crown trembled.

"YOU KNOW NOTHING OF BEING FORGOTTEN."

KOKI looked down at Milo's wooden ship.

"I gave up the memory of my true name."

The King fell silent.

"I'll never know it again."

KOKI raised his head.

"But I'm still here. And I still have people who know who I am."

The child inside the Heart began to cry.

The King roared.

"ENOUGH!"

His crown shattered into twelve black spears.

They flew toward KOKI.

Nox threw himself into their path.

His silver sword broke.

A black spear pierced his shoulder.

He fell.

KOKI caught him before he disappeared into the darkness.

"Nox!"

Nox coughed.

"Still... not very good at staying out of trouble."

KOKI held him tightly.

"Don't you dare die."

Nox smiled weakly.

"You're becoming predictable."

✦ ✦ ✦

VIII. The Choice of Two Captains

The Archive began collapsing.

Entire histories fell toward the Second Heart.

Lyra shouted from the ship.

"The Door is closing!"

Finn turned the wheel.

"We have to leave!"

KOKI looked at the Hollow King.

Then at the frightened child inside the crystal.

He understood what had to be done.

The Heart could not simply be destroyed.

It contained the memories of countless lost histories.

Destroying it might erase everything the King had stolen.

But leaving it intact would allow the King to continue his conquest.

Nox struggled to stand.

"There is one way."

KOKI looked at him.

"The two of us can separate the King from the Archive."

"And then?"

Nox hesitated.

"One of us must remain to guard the Door."

KOKI shook his head.

"No."

Nox smiled sadly.

"There it is again."

KOKI looked toward his crew.

They were waiting for him.

Milo stood at the railing, holding out his hand.

"Captain! Come home!"

KOKI closed his eyes.

He remembered the first day he had sailed beyond the horizon.

He remembered every storm, every battle, every promise.

He remembered the people who had chosen to follow him.

Then he looked at Nox.

"No one left behind."

Nox sighed.

"You really mean that, don't you?"

"Every word."

KOKI held out his hand.

"Then let's find a third choice."

Nox stared at him.

For a moment, the Archive was silent.

Then the small child inside the Second Heart spoke.

"There is another way."

Both captains turned.

The child pressed his hand against the crystal.

"Someone must remember us."

KOKI stepped closer.

"All of you?"

The child nodded.

"Not every name. Not every face. But that we existed."

Nox looked toward the thousands of floating worlds.

"The Archive doesn't need a king."

KOKI understood.

"It needs witnesses."

The child smiled.

"Yes."

KOKI turned toward his crew.

"Everyone! I need your help!"

Finn, Veyra, Brann, Lyra and Milo gathered at the bow.

KOKI raised Morcant's silver ring.

The engraved paper boat began to shine.

He thought of the forgotten sailors.

Of Elara.

Of the people of the drowned kingdom.

Of Nox's lost world.

And of every life the Hollow King had tried to erase.

"We remember you," KOKI said.

The crew repeated the words.

"We remember you."

The ring blazed gold.

Across the Archive, the floating islands stopped falling.

A thousand lights answered.

Then a million.

The Hollow King screamed.

"NO!"

The child inside the Heart stood up.

For the first time, he was no longer fading.

The King's crown cracked.

His enormous body began to unravel.

"YOU CANNOT TAKE MY KINGDOM!"

KOKI raised his sword.

"It was never yours."

The final golden light struck the black crown.

And the Hollow King disappeared.

✦ ✦ ✦

IX. The Door That Would Not Close

For one beautiful moment, the Archive was peaceful.

The floating worlds glowed with gentle light.

The child inside the Heart smiled at KOKI.

"Thank you."

KOKI smiled back.

"You're welcome."

Then the stars began to fall.

Lyra looked at the map.

Her face turned white.

"The King is gone..."

KOKI turned.

"But the Door is still open."

The Thirteenth Light had become unstable.

Without the King's power holding the stolen histories together, the Archive was beginning to collapse into the world.

Nox looked toward the opening.

"If it breaks, every history will collide."

Finn shouted from the wheel.

"Captain! We have seconds!"

KOKI looked at the crew.

Then at Nox.

The other captain understood.

"No," Nox said.

KOKI smiled.

"You sound like me."

He handed Morcant's ring to Milo.

"Keep this safe."

Milo stared at him.

"Why?"

KOKI knelt.

"Because I made a promise."

The boy's eyes filled with tears.

"You're coming back, right?"

KOKI held him close.

"I'll do everything I can."

He stood and faced the Door.

Nox stepped beside him.

"You're not doing this alone."

KOKI looked at him.

"You don't have to."

Nox smiled.

"Neither do you."

Together, the two captains raised their swords.

Gold and silver light surrounded them.

The Thirteenth Door began to close.

But something moved inside the darkness.

A small black crown appeared at the center of the collapsing Archive.

The Hollow King's voice returned.

Faint.

Broken.

But alive.

"YOU HAVE FORGOTTEN..."

KOKI froze.

"...THERE WERE ALWAYS THIRTEEN."

The black crown shattered.

Twelve pieces flew toward the twelve stars.

The thirteenth flew toward KOKI.

It struck his chest.

A black mark appeared beneath his coat.

KOKI gasped.

Nox caught him.

"KOKI!"

The Thirteenth Door slammed shut.

A blinding flash filled the sky.

And both captains vanished.

✦ ✦ ✦

X. The Captain's Empty Chair

The ship fell from the sky.

Finn fought the wheel as the vessel crashed through the clouds.

Brann grabbed Milo and shielded him with his own body.

Veyra clung to the mast.

Lyra held the star map against her chest.

The ship struck the sea with a tremendous roar.

For a moment, everything went black.

Then the waves settled.

The morning sun rose over a quiet ocean.

The shadow fleet was gone.

The Thirteenth Door had disappeared.

The sky was clear.

But KOKI was missing.

So was Nox.

Milo searched every cabin.

He looked beneath the stairs.

Behind the barrels.

Inside the captain's room.

Then he returned to the deck.

"He's not here."

No one answered.

Milo looked toward KOKI's empty chair.

The chair rocked gently with the movement of the ship.

He placed Morcant's silver ring on the seat.

Then he sat beside it.

"Captain KOKI promised he'd come home."

Finn lowered his head.

Veyra closed her eyes.

Brann turned away to hide his tears.

Lyra looked at the star map.

The First Star still burned gold.

The other eleven were pale and silent.

But at the center, the Thirteenth Point had changed.

It was no longer black.

It was silver.

And beneath it, a single line appeared.

Lyra read the words aloud.

"The last voyage begins where the first promise was made."

Finn looked up.

"The first promise?"

Milo stood.

He remembered the day KOKI had promised that no one would be left behind.

He remembered the wooden ship.

He remembered the First Star.

And he remembered the captain's words.

Milo wiped his tears.

"Then we have to find him."

Brann looked at the boy.

"Where?"

Milo pointed toward the horizon.

"Where the promise began."

Finn slowly smiled.

He took the wheel.

"Then that's where we're going."

The crew raised the sails.

And KOKI's ship began its final voyage.

EPILOGUE — THE NAMELESS SHORE

KOKI opened his eye.

He was lying on a beach beneath a sky filled with unfamiliar stars.

The sand was silver.

The sea was perfectly still.

There was no ship.

No crew.

No sound except the gentle movement of the waves.

KOKI sat up.

His sword lay beside him.

The black mark on his chest was glowing faintly.

He looked around.

A little wooden boat floated near the shore.

He recognized it immediately.

Milo's unfinished boat.

KOKI stood.

"Nox?"

No answer.

Then footsteps sounded behind him.

KOKI turned.

A woman stood at the edge of the forest.

Her face was familiar, though he could not remember why.

She wore a simple blue cloak.

In her hands, she held two small wooden cradles.

KOKI stared.

"Who are you?"

The woman smiled sadly.

"Someone who has been waiting a very long time."

KOKI took a step forward.

"Where am I?"

She looked toward the silver sea.

"At the shore between forgotten histories."

KOKI looked down at the black mark.

"Why am I here?"

The woman approached.

"Because the Hollow King left something inside you."

KOKI felt a chill.

"What?"

She looked directly into his remaining eye.

"His final memory."

The silver sea began to turn black.

And far beyond the horizon, a familiar voice whispered.

"KOKI..."

He turned toward the sound.

It was Nox.

But his voice was different.

Terrified.

Desperate.

"Don't let her open the last cradle."

KOKI turned back toward the woman.

She was no longer smiling.

One of the wooden cradles had begun to move.

And something inside it was waking up.
