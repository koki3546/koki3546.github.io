Beyond the Edge of Time
CHAPTER 04

The Ship That Never Sailed

What if a life without pain
was also a life without the ones you loved?

The ocean was disappearing.

KOKI reached the harbor just as the mast of his beloved ship dissolved into golden dust.

"BRANN! THE ROPES!"

Brann jumped onto the pier and grabbed the mooring line.

But the rope vanished between his fingers.

The sails disappeared.

The cannons disappeared.

The wooden deck upon which they had shared countless adventures faded into nothingness.

Then Finn looked at KOKI.

"Excuse me..."

The little sailor stepped backward.

"Who are you?"

KOKI froze.

The Council was not simply destroying his ship.

It was destroying the journey that had brought them together.

✦ ✦ ✦

I. The Island He Never Left

A golden wave swept across the harbor.

KOKI closed his eyes.

When he opened them, he was standing beneath a familiar tree.

The ocean was calm.

Fishing boats rested beside a wooden pier.

Children laughed along the beach.

He recognized every house, every path, and every distant mountain.

It was the island where his adventure had begun.

But something was different.

His reflection appeared in the water.

There was no scar upon his cheek.

No eyepatch.

No pirate hat.

He was wearing the simple clothes of a fisherman.

"KOKI!"

An elderly neighbor waved from the village.

"You're going to be late for dinner!"

KOKI stared toward the horizon.

"Where is my ship?"

The neighbor laughed.

"What ship? You've never left this island."

KOKI felt a terrible emptiness.

He ran toward the beach where his first wooden boat had once waited.

It was still there.

Its sails were torn.

Its compass was broken.

But its hull had never touched the ocean.

✦ ✦ ✦

II. The Happiness He Could Have Had

As the sun descended, KOKI walked through the village.

Everyone knew him.

Everyone welcomed him.

There were no enemies.

No terrible battles.

No nights spent wondering whether his friends would survive until morning.

A warm meal waited inside his little house.

Photographs covered the walls.

In every photograph, KOKI was smiling.

For a moment, he wondered what his life might have been if he had never sailed beyond the horizon.

Then a voice spoke behind him.

"Beautiful, isn't it?"

Director Vaelor stood beside the doorway.

"You could have lived a peaceful life."

KOKI turned.

"Where are my friends?"

"Living their own lives. Far away from you."

Vaelor approached the photographs.

"No scars. No sacrifices. No suffering."

He smiled.

"Isn't this what everyone ultimately wants?"

KOKI looked at the smiling elephant in the photographs.

Then he noticed something.

There was not a single photograph of Finn.

Not one of Brann, Veyra, Lyra, Nox, or Milo.

He turned toward Vaelor.

"You removed my pain."

"Yes."

"But you also removed everything that made my life worth living."

Vaelor's smile disappeared.

KOKI walked past him.

"I'm leaving."

✦ ✦ ✦

III. The Ocean Without a Horizon

KOKI pushed his little wooden boat into the water.

The villagers gathered along the beach.

"Don't go!"

"There is nothing beyond the horizon!"

"You have everything you need here!"

KOKI raised the torn sail.

"I've heard that before."

The wind carried his boat away from the island.

But as he approached the horizon, the ocean suddenly rose into the sky.

An enormous wall of golden water surrounded the world.

Within it, thousands of clocks turned backward.

Vaelor's voice thundered above the waves.

"You cannot escape a history that has already been rewritten!"

The golden ocean transformed.

Enormous mechanical sea creatures emerged from the waves.

Their bodies were covered in clocks.

Their eyes burned with stolen sunlight.

KOKI reached for his sword.

It was gone.

He had never become a pirate.

He had never learned to fight.

But somewhere inside him, the courage remained.

KOKI seized a wooden oar.

"Then I'll write it again!"

The first creature attacked.

He turned his boat sharply, narrowly escaping its enormous jaws.

A second creature shattered the mast.

A third rose beneath the hull.

The little boat was breaking apart.

Then, from somewhere beyond the golden wall, a familiar voice shouted.

"CAPTAIN!"

✦ ✦ ✦

IV. The Sailor Who Remembered

A silver blade pierced the golden ocean.

The wall cracked.

Nox emerged aboard a small black vessel.

His coat was torn.

His face was covered in scratches.

But he was smiling.

"Did you really think I'd let you have another adventure without me?"

KOKI laughed.

"Nox!"

The two vessels collided as another mechanical creature attacked.

Nox threw his silver sword toward KOKI.

"Catch!"

KOKI caught the blade with his trunk before gripping its handle.

The moment he touched it, his scar returned.

His pirate coat appeared.

His eyepatch covered his right eye.

The false history began to fracture.

Nox pointed toward the golden wall.

"The Council can erase our meetings. But it can't completely erase the choices we've already made."

Together, the two captains sailed directly toward the largest creature.

It opened its enormous jaws.

KOKI climbed onto the broken mast.

"NOW!"

Nox turned the vessel.

KOKI leapt through the air and drove the silver blade into the creature's golden clock.

The entire ocean trembled.

Thousands of cracks spread across the sky.

Beyond them, KOKI saw fragments of another world.

Brann working alone in a distant shipyard.

Veyra imprisoned aboard a forgotten warship.

Lyra wandering through a library of endless books.

Finn standing beside a wrecked boat.

And Milo, sitting alone beneath a tree.

KOKI reached toward them.

The golden wall shattered.

✦ ✦ ✦

V. A Ship Built from Memories

KOKI and Nox fell into a vast ocean of darkness.

Pieces of broken ships floated among thousands of glowing memories.

A wooden plank drifted toward KOKI.

He recognized it immediately.

It was part of his ship.

Upon its surface, someone had carved four words.

NO ONE LEFT BEHIND.

KOKI held the plank against his chest.

Then another piece of wood appeared.

And another.

A familiar sail descended from the darkness.

Brann's hammer emerged from a golden light.

Finn's old rope wrapped itself around the mast.

Veyra's compass appeared beside the wheel.

Lyra's lantern illuminated the deck.

Every memory became a piece of the ship.

Every friendship became something the Council could not completely destroy.

The vessel slowly rebuilt itself beneath KOKI's feet.

Nox watched in astonishment.

"How is this possible?"

KOKI placed his hand upon the wheel.

"Because a ship isn't just made of wood."

He looked toward the distant lights.

"It's made of everyone who sailed aboard it."

The sails opened.

The cannons returned.

The pirate flag rose into the darkness.

And for the first time in a world without wind, the ship began to move.

✦ ✦ ✦

VI. The Captain Against Tomorrow

Far above Aevora, Director Vaelor watched his enormous clock begin to crack.

"Impossible."

One of the Meridian officers approached him.

"Director, the pirate has escaped the corrected timeline."

Vaelor stared at the instrument.

"Where is he going?"

The officer hesitated.

"Into the discarded histories."

Vaelor's face turned pale.

Beneath the Meridian Tower, an ancient machine began to awaken.

It had remained silent for a thousand years.

Seven enormous doors opened.

Behind each door waited a different past.

Meanwhile, KOKI stood upon the deck of his restored ship.

Five distant lights appeared across the endless darkness.

One for each of his missing companions.

Nox stood beside him.

"Which one do we follow first?"

KOKI looked at the smallest light.

It was fading faster than the others.

"Milo."

He turned the wheel.

The ship accelerated toward the unknown.

But as they approached the first doorway, a terrifying figure emerged from the darkness.

It wore a golden crown.

Its enormous body was formed from broken clocks and forgotten centuries.

It carried a sword upon which entire civilizations appeared and disappeared.

Nox slowly drew his weapon.

"What is that?"

The creature opened its eyes.

"I am the guardian of everything that never happened."

KOKI stepped forward.

The pirate flag rose behind him.

"Then you're about to witness something new."

The creature raised its sword.

And the battle for yesterday began.

Twenty hours remained.
