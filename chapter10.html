THE KOKI CHRONICLES · CHAPTER 10

The Drowned Kingdom

Beneath every legend lies a truth. Beneath every kingdom lies a grave.

The ocean opened like a forgotten door.

Beneath Thalor's enormous fins, the waters of the Forgotten Sea began to spiral downward.

KOKI stood upon the guardian's back, watching the darkness deepen.

Finn held the Last Compass.

Veyra stood beside them, her silver sword resting against her wounded wing.

Above, Captain Brann and the fleet disappeared behind a curtain of shimmering blue light.

"How far down are we going?" Finn asked.

Thalor's voice echoed through the water.

"Farther than any living sailor has traveled in a thousand years."

The darkness broke.

And beneath them lay a kingdom.

✦ ✦ ✦

Towers of white stone stretched across the ocean floor.

Golden bridges connected enormous palaces.

Statues of forgotten rulers stood among forests of coral.

At the center rose a magnificent castle, its highest tower wrapped in three great chains.

One chain had snapped.

The second trembled.

The third remained still.

Finn stared in wonder.

"The Drowned Kingdom..."

Thalor nodded.

"Once called Aurelion. The greatest kingdom the seas had ever known."

KOKI looked at the silent streets.

"What happened to everyone?"

Thalor lowered his head.

"Their king feared losing them. So he tried to make their loyalty eternal."

The guardian paused.

"And in doing so, he took away their freedom."

✦ ✦ ✦

Thalor carried them through a shattered gateway into a vast courtyard.

A sphere of air surrounded the ancient stones.

KOKI stepped onto the ground.

For the first time, he stood in a city that had been buried beneath the sea for centuries.

Along the walls, faded paintings showed a king wearing a golden crown.

In the first painting, the king stood beside his people.

In the second, he held a magnificent bell.

In the third, his subjects knelt before him with empty eyes.

Finn studied the images.

"He used the Bell to control them."

Thalor nodded.

"He believed obedience would end suffering. No rebellion. No betrayal. No one leaving him."

Veyra looked away.

"A kingdom without choices is only a prison."

KOKI looked at the final painting.

The king stood alone among thousands of silent figures.

His golden crown had become black.

"And he became the Drowned King."

✦ ✦ ✦

A slow clap echoed across the courtyard.

KOKI turned.

A figure stepped from beneath a broken archway.

Black armor.

A crimson cloak.

The Shadow Compass hanging from his hand.

Morcant.

"Very good, little captain."

Veyra drew her sword.

Finn raised the Last Compass.

KOKI stepped between them and the armored figure.

"Stay back."

Morcant laughed quietly.

"You came all this way to stop me."

He gestured toward the palace.

"But you still don't know why I began."

KOKI tightened his fists.

"You want to wake the Drowned King."

Morcant's smile vanished.

"I want to bring someone back."

✦ ✦ ✦

The Shadow Compass projected a circle of light.

Inside it appeared a small seaside village.

Children ran along the docks.

Fishing boats returned beneath the evening sun.

A younger Morcant stood beside a little girl.

She wore a blue ribbon around her hair.

She laughed as she chased a paper boat through the water.

Morcant watched the memory without speaking.

Then the sky darkened.

A terrible storm swallowed the village.

Houses vanished beneath enormous waves.

The little girl disappeared into the sea.

The image faded.

Morcant's voice was almost a whisper.

"Her name was Lyra."

He looked at KOKI.

"My daughter."

Finn lowered the compass.

Even Veyra's sword trembled slightly.

Morcant continued.

"I searched every sea. I begged every guardian. I offered everything I had."

He looked toward the chained palace.

"Only the Bell answered me."

✦ ✦ ✦

KOKI looked at the lonely figure.

For a moment, he saw no monster.

Only a father who had lost his child.

"I'm sorry about Lyra."

Morcant stood motionless.

KOKI took a step forward.

"But waking the King won't bring her back."

Morcant's voice sharpened.

"You cannot know that!"

The Shadow Compass began to glow.

"The Bell can command the souls of the dead. It can call them home."

Thalor shook his head.

"It can bind a soul, Morcant. It cannot restore the life that soul once lived."

Morcant turned on him.

"You guardians told me to accept her death."

His voice broke.

"How could I accept a world that continued without her?"

KOKI felt the faint mark beneath his coat grow warm.

He remembered Finn's tears when he thought his father was gone.

He remembered the lost sailors aboard the ghost fleet.

He understood the pain.

But not the choice.

"You lost someone you loved."

KOKI looked directly into Morcant's eyes.

"That doesn't give you the right to take everyone else's freedom."

✦ ✦ ✦

Morcant lowered his head.

For one brief moment, the courtyard was silent.

Then he raised the Shadow Compass.

"Freedom is what allowed the world to abandon her."

A crimson beam struck the palace.

The second chain began to crack.

Thalor roared.

"Morcant! Stop!"

The ground shook.

Stone statues stepped from their pedestals.

Their eyes burned red.

Finn stumbled backward.

Veyra raised her sword.

KOKI grabbed the Last Compass.

Its needle spun toward the palace.

Then toward the second chain.

Then toward KOKI himself.

A terrible realization crossed his face.

"The chain..."

Beneath his coat, the red mark began to spread.

Morcant smiled.

"Did you really believe breaking a few links could free you from me?"

✦ ✦ ✦

KOKI fell to his knees.

Red light burst from his wrist.

The mark crawled across his arm.

Finn rushed toward him.

"KOKI!"

Morcant raised his hand.

KOKI's body rose against his will.

His eyes flashed crimson.

Veyra ran forward, but a stone guardian struck the ground between them.

Thalor battled the awakening statues.

Finn held the Last Compass against his chest.

KOKI struggled inside his own mind.

He heard Morcant whisper.

"You will open the second seal for me."

KOKI tried to resist.

But his legs carried him toward the palace.

The golden doors opened.

Inside stood an enormous throne.

Upon it rested a figure wrapped in black chains.

The Drowned King.

His eyes were closed.

His crown was buried in darkness.

KOKI approached the second seal.

Morcant followed.

"Touch it."

KOKI's hand rose.

The Last Compass burned against his chest.

He remembered Finn standing before him in the lighthouse.

He remembered the fleet raising its flags.

He remembered the promise he had made.

No one would be disposable.

Not even himself.

KOKI clenched his fist.

"No."

Morcant's eyes narrowed.

"What?"

KOKI turned toward him.

"You don't get to decide who I am."

The golden compass erupted with light.

✦ ✦ ✦

A wave of gold swept across the throne room.

The red mark vanished from KOKI's arm.

Morcant staggered backward.

The Shadow Compass cracked down its center.

For a moment, KOKI stood free.

Then the second chain snapped.

A sound like thunder rolled through the kingdom.

KOKI turned.

The second seal had broken.

Morcant stared at the shattered chain.

Even he looked surprised.

From the throne came a slow, terrible breath.

The Drowned King's fingers began to move.

His enormous head lifted slightly.

And beneath the crown, two ancient eyes opened.

They were not red.

They were not gold.

They were empty.

The King spoke a single word.

"Freedom."

The entire kingdom trembled.

Then he smiled.

"How I have hated that word."

✦ ✦ ✦

Above the sea, Brann's fleet was thrown into chaos.

Waves rose higher than the tallest masts.

The ghost sailors fell to their knees.

Their blue flames turned black.

Far beneath the waves, Thalor cried out.

"The second seal has fallen!"

Finn looked toward the palace.

Veyra gripped his shoulder.

"We have to reach KOKI!"

But the palace doors slammed shut.

A wall of darkness spread across the kingdom.

Inside the throne room, KOKI stood alone between Morcant and the awakening King.

The Drowned King looked down at him.

"A little captain."

His voice shook the ancient stones.

"And a very large heart."

KOKI raised the Last Compass.

The King leaned forward.

"Tell me, little elephant."

His empty eyes fixed upon KOKI.

"Would you surrender your freedom to save everyone you love?"

KOKI hesitated.

The King smiled.

"I thought so."

Above the throne, the final chain began to glow.
