THE KOKI CHRONICLES · SEASON TWO

The Island of Whispering Bones

The Twelve Stars — Chapter 05

For seven nights, the ocean whispered KOKI's name.

At first, he thought it was the wind.

Then he heard the voices clearly.

"Captain..."

"Turn back..."

"Before the bones remember..."

KOKI stood at the bow of his ship, his black eyepatch shining beneath the moon.

The Shadow Captain's final warning still echoed in his mind.

"Find the keeper of the Second Star."

Finn approached, carrying his telescope.

"Captain, there's something ahead."

KOKI looked toward the horizon.

At first, he saw only pale mountains.

Then lightning illuminated the sky.

They were not mountains.

They were bones.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Island That Should Not Exist

An enormous skull rose above the clouds.

Its jaws formed the entrance to a forgotten temple. Its ribs stretched across the island like the arches of a ruined cathedral.

Waterfalls poured through its empty eye sockets.

Brann stared in disbelief.

"What kind of creature was that?"

Veyra examined the ancient star map.

"Something older than the Forgotten Sea."

As their ship approached, the ocean suddenly turned white.

Thousands of small bones floated beneath the surface.

They began to move.

The bones gathered into enormous hands, rising from the water to seize the ship.

"Hard to port!" KOKI shouted.

The first hand smashed against the hull.

Wood shattered.

Finn was thrown across the deck.

Brann caught him before he fell overboard.

Veyra fired the forward cannon.

The skeletal hand exploded, but the fragments immediately reassembled.

KOKI noticed something strange.

The bones moved whenever the ship's bell rang.

"Brann! Silence the bell!"

Brann wrapped the bell in a heavy sailcloth.

The skeletal hands stopped.

For one precious moment, the sea became still.

KOKI seized the opportunity.

"Full sail! Straight through!"

Their ship raced between the enormous ribs and crashed onto a narrow beach.

Behind them, the bones began moving again.

There would be no easy escape.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Keeper of the Second Star

Deep inside the island, the crew discovered a staircase carved into the spine of the ancient creature.

At its summit stood a woman wearing a white cloak embroidered with gold.

A radiant star floated above her hand.

KOKI stepped forward.

"We're looking for the keeper of the Second Star."

The woman studied his face.

Then she saw his eyepatch.

Her expression hardened.

"Another KOKI."

She raised her hand.

The temple doors slammed shut.

Golden chains burst from the ground and wrapped around the crew.

Finn struggled to break free.

"We're not your enemies!"

The woman ignored him.

"My name is Seraphine. I have guarded the Second Star since the first ocean was born."

She approached KOKI.

"Forty years ago, a captain wearing your face came to this island."

KOKI felt his heart tighten.

"The Shadow Captain."

Seraphine nodded.

"He begged me to destroy the star. When I refused, he tried to take it by force."

She pointed toward a broken tower.

"He failed. But before he left, he told me another version of himself would come one day."

Her voice became cold.

"He said you would make the same terrible mistake."

KOKI looked directly at her.

"Then let me prove him wrong."

Seraphine lifted the star higher.

"Very well."

The floor disappeared beneath KOKI's feet.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Trial of a Thousand Voices

KOKI fell into darkness.

When he opened his eye, he was standing in his childhood village.

The streets were empty.

A small wooden house stood before him.

Inside, a familiar voice called his name.

"Come home, KOKI."

He froze.

It was his father's voice.

KOKI opened the door.

His father sat beside a warm fireplace, smiling as though no time had passed.

"You've traveled far enough, son."

KOKI felt tears rising.

For a moment, he wanted nothing more than to sit beside him.

Then the room began to shake.

Outside the window, Finn was drowning in a black ocean.

Veyra was trapped beneath a burning mast.

Brann was calling for help.

His father held out his hand.

"Stay with me. You have already given enough."

KOKI looked at the face he had missed for so long.

"I wish I could."

He stepped backward.

"But they're waiting for me."

The house vanished.

Suddenly, KOKI stood before twelve enormous doors.

Behind each door, someone was crying for help.

A voice thundered through the darkness.

"Choose one."

KOKI gripped his sword.

"Why?"

"Because no captain can save everyone."

KOKI looked at the doors.

He remembered the Shadow Captain's words.

Some lives must be sacrificed to save the rest.

Then KOKI lowered his sword.

Instead of opening a door, he examined the walls between them.

There were cracks.

Tiny cracks connecting every chamber.

KOKI smiled.

"Who said I had to choose a door?"

He drove his sword into the largest crack.

The wall shattered.

All twelve doors collapsed together.

Light flooded the darkness.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Secret of the Twelve Stars

KOKI awakened inside the temple.

The golden chains had disappeared.

Seraphine stood before him, her eyes filled with astonishment.

"No one has ever broken the trial."

KOKI rose slowly.

"Then perhaps the trial was wrong."

For the first time, Seraphine smiled.

She lowered the Second Star.

"You are different from the captain who came before you."

KOKI stepped closer.

"Tell me the truth. What are the Twelve Stars?"

Seraphine led them into a hidden chamber.

Across its walls, ancient paintings showed twelve stars surrounding a black sun.

"Long before the oceans existed, a being known as the Hollow King attempted to consume the light of creation."

"To stop him, the first guardians divided his prison into twelve living seals."

KOKI studied the paintings.

"Living seals?"

Seraphine nodded.

"The stars do not simply lock his prison. They keep its walls alive."

Veyra frowned.

"Then destroying them would release him."

"Yes."

Finn pointed to the final painting.

"But what happens if someone gathers all twelve stars together?"

Seraphine's face darkened.

"Their power can be joined. But if the Hollow King controls that power, he can open the prison himself."

Brann folded his arms.

"So destroying the stars is dangerous. And gathering them is dangerous."

KOKI looked at the star map.

"There must be another way."

Seraphine hesitated.

"There is an old legend."

She touched the painting at the center of the chamber.

A thirteenth symbol appeared.

It was not a star.

It was a small circle, surrounded by twelve open hands.

"The Twelve Stars can be bound without being broken. But only by the one who carries the Thirteenth Light."

KOKI stared at the symbol.

"What is the Thirteenth Light?"

Seraphine shook her head.

"That knowledge was erased from every kingdom."

She paused.

"Except one."

"The kingdom beyond the northern storm."

✦ ✦ ✦

The Bones Awaken

Before KOKI could ask another question, the entire island trembled.

A deafening roar rose from beneath the temple.

The walls cracked.

The giant skull outside began to move.

Seraphine turned pale.

"Impossible."

The enormous creature was awakening.

Its ancient bones rose from the ocean, forming a monstrous body that towered above the island.

A voice echoed through the temple.

"Keeper..."

"Give me the star."

KOKI recognized the voice.

It was the same voice he had heard beneath the sea after the battle with the Shadow Captain.

The Hollow King.

Seraphine gripped the Second Star.

"He is reaching through the prison."

The creature's enormous jaws began closing around the temple.

KOKI ran toward the collapsing staircase.

"Everyone, move!"

Brann lifted a fallen stone pillar, clearing the passage.

Veyra cut the ropes holding the ancient bridge.

Finn guided the crew toward a narrow opening between the creature's ribs.

But Seraphine remained behind.

"The Second Star is bound to this island. If I leave, the seal will weaken."

KOKI looked at the approaching jaws.

"Then we don't leave you."

He examined the ancient bridge.

The same golden markings covered its stones and the star map in his hand.

An idea struck him.

"Finn! The map!"

Finn threw it across the gap.

KOKI pressed the map against the bridge's central stone.

Golden light spread through the entire structure.

The bridge transformed into a circle of glowing chains that wrapped around the creature's jaws.

For one moment, the enormous beast stopped moving.

"Now!" KOKI shouted.

Seraphine raised the Second Star.

A beam of golden light struck the creature's skull.

The ancient bones shattered, falling harmlessly into the ocean.

Silence returned to the island.

Seraphine fell to her knees.

The Second Star still glowed.

Its seal had survived.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Thirteenth Light

At sunrise, KOKI prepared to leave.

Seraphine approached the ship, carrying a small golden fragment.

"This belongs to the Second Star. It will guide you toward the northern kingdom."

She placed it beside the ancient star map.

The fragment glowed.

A new route appeared.

Far beyond the known sea, a mountain rose above a wall of storms.

At its summit stood a forgotten castle.

KOKI studied the image.

"The kingdom beyond the northern storm."

Seraphine nodded.

"Find the Thirteenth Light before the Shadow Captain finds the remaining stars."

KOKI looked back at his friends.

Finn was repairing his telescope. Veyra was checking the sails. Brann was laughing as he carried three enormous barrels at once.

KOKI smiled.

He had once believed that courage meant facing danger without fear.

Now he understood something else.

Courage also meant refusing to accept a terrible choice when another answer might exist.

He raised his sword.

"Set sail!"

Their ship moved toward the northern horizon.

But deep beneath the Island of Whispering Bones, something remained awake.

A single red eye opened inside the darkness.

The Hollow King's voice whispered:

"The Thirteenth Light..."

"So the little captain has discovered my greatest fear."

Far away, aboard a black warship, the Shadow Captain heard the same whisper.

He opened an old journal.

On its final page was a drawing of the thirteenth symbol.

Beneath it, written in his own handwriting, were five words.

"Do not let KOKI remember."

The Shadow Captain stared at the page.

For the first time in forty years, he looked afraid.
