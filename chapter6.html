THE KOKI CHRONICLES · CHAPTER 06

The Last Compass

A true captain is guided not by treasure, but by what he refuses to abandon.

The sea had spoken.

KOKI held Finn tightly as the enormous creature rose beneath their broken timber.

Its skin was dark as midnight, marked with ancient golden patterns. Two luminous eyes watched them with a sadness older than the stars.

"Who are you?" KOKI whispered.

The creature's voice rolled through the water like distant thunder.

"I am Thalor, the last guardian of the Forgotten Sea."

Finn trembled.

"The guardian from my father's stories?"

Thalor lowered his great head.

"Rowan remembered the old promises. Few others did."

KOKI stepped forward.

"Then you knew his father?"

"I knew the man who chose to lose everything rather than betray those he loved."

Finn's eyes filled with tears.

✦ ✦ ✦

The ocean began to glow.

A dome of shimmering air formed around KOKI and Finn as Thalor descended beneath the waves.

Fish moved like silver stars through the deep blue darkness.

Far below them, a city appeared.

Broken towers rose from the seabed. Golden bridges connected silent temples. At the center stood a colossal instrument shaped like a compass.

Its eight points were made of light.

"The City of Tides," Thalor said.

Finn pressed his paw against the transparent dome.

"My father said this place was only a legend."

"Legends are often memories the world has forgotten."

Thalor carried them to a broad stone platform beneath the city.

Water rolled away from the entrance, leaving a dry path between two enormous statues.

KOKI stepped down.

For the first time since losing his boat, he felt solid ground beneath his feet.

✦ ✦ ✦

Inside the temple, eight stone doors surrounded a circular chamber.

At its center floated a small golden compass.

Unlike Morcant's black instrument, it had no needle.

KOKI frowned.

"How can a compass work without a needle?"

Thalor's great voice echoed through the chamber.

"The Last Compass does not show the road you wish to travel. It reveals the road you are willing to suffer for."

Finn approached the golden light.

"Can it lead us to my father?"

The compass flickered.

"Perhaps," Thalor replied. "But first, someone must awaken it."

The floor began to shake.

Eight stone doors opened.

Darkness poured into the chamber.

"Only one may enter the trial."

KOKI looked at Finn.

The little fox was still weak from their night at sea.

KOKI stepped forward.

"I'll go."

✦ ✦ ✦

The doors slammed shut behind him.

KOKI stood alone in a dark stone corridor.

A warm light appeared ahead.

He followed it.

Suddenly, the corridor vanished.

He was standing on his home island.

The sea was calm. The trees swayed gently. Familiar voices called his name.

His old wooden boat rested safely beside the harbor.

Everything was exactly as he remembered.

A voice whispered behind him.

"You can stay."

KOKI turned.

A figure made of golden mist stood beneath the trees.

"No storms. No enemies. No pain."

KOKI looked toward his home.

He had dreamed of returning since the night he nearly drowned.

He could hear laughter through the open windows.

His heart ached.

"Is this real?"

"It can be."

The golden figure extended a hand.

"Leave the Forgotten Sea. Forget Morcant. Forget the Bell. Your adventure can end here."

KOKI slowly reached forward.

Then he heard a distant voice.

"KOKI!"

Finn.

The sound came from somewhere beyond the beautiful illusion.

KOKI lowered his hand.

"What happens to Finn if I stay?"

The golden figure said nothing.

KOKI understood.

He looked at the warm lights of his childhood home.

A tear rolled down his cheek.

"I want to go home."

He closed his eyes.

"But not like this."

The illusion shattered.

✦ ✦ ✦

KOKI fell to his knees on the temple floor.

The eight doors had vanished.

In their place stood a single enormous mirror.

Inside it, he saw Finn trapped beneath the waves.

Blue chains pulled the fox toward the ocean floor.

Above the mirror appeared two glowing words:

"ONE LIFE."

A second image appeared.

Hundreds of cursed sailors aboard the Mourning Crown.

Their faces twisted in pain.

The words changed.

"OR HUNDREDS."

KOKI stared at the images.

He understood the choice.

Save Finn.

Or free the cursed fleet.

He clenched his fists.

"No."

The mirror trembled.

"I won't choose who deserves to be abandoned."

He stepped toward the glass.

"If there's a way to save them, I'll find it."

The mirror cracked.

Water exploded through the chamber.

KOKI was thrown backward.

The sea rushed around him.

He could barely breathe.

Through the darkness, he saw a small shape sinking.

Finn.

KOKI swam toward him.

His injured shoulder screamed.

He caught the fox's paw.

But the blue chains tightened.

The golden compass appeared between them.

Its light grew brighter.

KOKI wrapped his trunk around the chains.

He pulled with all his strength.

The metal began to crack.

And then the entire ocean turned to gold.

✦ ✦ ✦

KOKI opened his eyes.

He was back in the temple.

Finn stood beside him, alive and unharmed.

The golden compass floated between them.

Its eight points spun slowly.

Thalor watched in silence.

"You refused the choice."

KOKI lowered his head.

"I couldn't leave them."

Thalor's golden eyes softened.

"That was the trial."

The compass descended into KOKI's open hands.

For the first time, a golden needle appeared.

It did not point north.

It pointed toward Finn.

Finn smiled through his tears.

"My father was right."

The needle turned again.

This time it pointed toward the dark horizon beyond the Forgotten Sea.

Thalor's voice grew solemn.

"Morcant possesses the Shadow Compass. It can find the prison of the Bell. But only the Last Compass can show the path that frees it."

KOKI looked down at the instrument.

"Then we'll get there first."

Thalor shook his enormous head.

"There is something else you must know."

The temple walls began to glow.

An ancient image appeared: Rowan standing before a gate of black stone.

Finn gasped.

"Father!"

In the image, Rowan placed both hands against the gate.

Blue chains wrapped around him.

He did not run.

Instead, he smiled sadly.

And stepped into the darkness.

Finn began to shake.

"Is he dead?"

Thalor lowered his head.

"I do not know what remains of Rowan beyond that gate. But his final act kept the prison closed."

KOKI placed a hand on Finn's shoulder.

"Then that's where we're going."

Finn looked up.

"Even if he's gone?"

KOKI nodded.

"We deserve to know the truth. And the people trapped by that Bell deserve a chance."

✦ ✦ ✦

Above the temple, a distant thunder rolled.

The golden compass shook in KOKI's hands.

Its needle suddenly turned black.

Thalor raised his great head.

"Morcant has found the first seal."

A crack appeared across the temple floor.

Blue fire rose from the depths.

Far away, beyond the city, a colossal bell began to ring.

Once.

Twice.

Then a third time.

Thalor's voice thundered through the ancient halls.

"The prison is beginning to open."

KOKI gripped the Last Compass.

He looked at Finn.

Then toward the distant darkness.

"Then we don't have a moment to lose."
