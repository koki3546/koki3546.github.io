A Little Elephant. A Big Adventure.

Chapter 1 — Beyond the Horizon

KOKI was no ordinary elephant. While others dreamed of peaceful days, he dreamed of oceans, distant islands, and adventures beyond the horizon.

One day, carrying nothing but courage and an impossible dream, KOKI decided to leave his familiar world.

He did not know what awaited him. Storms, mysterious lands, unexpected friends, and dangerous enemies would soon change his destiny forever.

But every great adventure begins with a single brave step.
