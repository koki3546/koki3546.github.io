CHAPTER 08

The Library of Unwritten Tomorrows

The future is not a prison. It is an adventure waiting to begin.

The book knew how KOKI would die.

It knew the place.

It knew the hour.

It even knew the final words he would speak.

But there was one thing the book did not know.

KOKI had never been particularly good at following instructions.

He closed the book.

"Terrible ending."

Nox laughed.

Veyra did not.

Beyond the silver table stretched a library without walls, without ceilings, and without an end.

Millions of books floated between distant stars.

Some were larger than mountains.

Others were small enough to fit inside Milo's hands.

And somewhere among them, a familiar lantern was burning.

"Lyra," KOKI whispered.

✦ ✦ ✦

I. The Books That Had Never Been Written

As the pirate ship entered the library, its wooden hull began sailing across an ocean of golden letters.

Brann reached over the railing and caught a floating book.

He opened it.

Inside, he saw himself building a little house beside a peaceful river.

Children gathered around his workshop.

An elderly Brann sat beneath a tree, smiling.

He quietly closed the book.

"Not a bad future."

Milo found another book.

On its first page stood his mother.

She was waiting beside the river where she had taught him to fold paper boats.

His hands began to tremble.

KOKI gently closed the book for him.

"Some dreams are beautiful, Milo."

He looked toward the endless shelves.

"But that doesn't mean we should become prisoners of them."

Suddenly, every book in the library opened.

Millions of voices whispered at once.

THE FUTURE HAS BEEN DECIDED.

The golden letters beneath their ship turned black.

And the library began to move.

✦ ✦ ✦

II. The Keeper of Tomorrow

At the center of the library stood an enormous tree.

Its branches carried thousands of glowing lanterns.

Beneath it sat Lyra.

A silver crown floated above her head.

Her eyes reflected countless futures.

When KOKI approached, she did not look surprised.

"You arrived seven seconds earlier than expected."

KOKI smiled.

"Good to see you too."

Lyra raised her hand.

A book appeared between them.

"You will ask me to return to your ship."

Another book appeared.

"Nox will attempt to destroy the crown."

A third book opened.

"Brann will break the eastern pillar."

She looked at KOKI.

"And you will die trying to save me."

Silence filled the chamber.

Veyra stepped forward.

"Then tell us how to prevent it."

Lyra shook her head.

"I have already searched."

"How many futures?"

"All of them."

KOKI studied her face.

Behind her calm expression, he saw something familiar.

Fear.

"How long have you been looking, Lyra?"

For the first time, her voice trembled.

"Nine hundred years."

✦ ✦ ✦

III. The Beast Made of Endings

The silver crown suddenly began to glow.

An enormous shadow descended from the branches of the tree.

Thousands of books flew together.

Their pages became scales.

Their covers became armor.

Their golden letters became burning eyes.

A colossal serpent wrapped itself around the library.

Its body stretched across entire constellations.

Lyra stood.

"The Final Author."

The creature opened its mouth.

A storm of black letters swept toward the pirate ship.

Nox turned the wheel.

Veyra fired the cannons.

Brann shattered a falling tower with his hammer.

KOKI grabbed a rope and swung toward the serpent.

His sword struck its golden scales.

The creature roared.

Then an enormous book opened before him.

KOKI saw himself falling into darkness.

A moment later, the serpent struck.

Exactly as the book had predicted.

He barely caught a floating staircase with his trunk.

Another book opened.

Another attack followed.

Every movement had already been written.

Every escape had already been anticipated.

KOKI looked toward the endless pages.

"Well, that's cheating."

The serpent opened its jaws.

And swallowed him.

✦ ✦ ✦

IV. The Last Page

KOKI awakened upon a quiet beach.

The ocean was calm.

There were no monsters.

No broken clocks.

No distant battles.

An old wooden boat rested beside him.

A book lay upon its deck.

He opened it.

Every adventure he had ever experienced appeared within its pages.

The Forgotten Sea.

The Twelve Stars.

The friends he had rescued.

The promises he had made.

The name he had sacrificed.

And finally, the library.

On the last page, he saw his own death.

A voice spoke behind him.

"You cannot escape your ending."

Director Vaelor stood beside the water.

"Every life is a story, Captain. Every story must end."

KOKI looked at the book.

"Of course."

Vaelor appeared surprised.

"Then you accept your fate?"

KOKI shook his head.

"I accept that my life will end someday."

He tore the final page from the book.

"But I don't accept your right to decide what I do before then."

The beach began to fracture.

Vaelor stepped backward.

"Without a written future, there is only uncertainty!"

KOKI smiled.

"That's what makes it an adventure."

He raised his sword.

And cut through the horizon.

✦ ✦ ✦

V. The First Unwritten Future

The Final Author screamed.

A golden blade emerged from its chest.

KOKI burst through a storm of burning pages.

He landed upon a floating bookshelf.

"LYRA!"

She looked upward.

"You were supposed to die."

"Sorry to disappoint you!"

KOKI leapt toward the enormous tree.

The serpent followed.

Lyra searched the books surrounding her.

For the first time in nine hundred years, their pages were changing faster than she could read them.

She saw thousands of new possibilities.

Then millions.

Then something she had never seen before.

An empty page.

KOKI landed beside her.

"Do you remember why you joined my crew?"

Lyra stared at him.

"To discover the secrets of the world."

KOKI nodded.

"Not to know everything before it happens."

He offered his hand.

"Come discover tomorrow with us."

Tears filled her eyes.

She removed the silver crown.

The Final Author lunged toward them.

Lyra lifted her lantern.

Its light spread across the library.

Every book opened to a blank page.

The serpent began to disintegrate.

Its scales became white feathers.

Its enormous body dissolved into millions of golden stars.

For the first time, the library fell silent.

Lyra looked at the empty pages.

Then she laughed.

"I have absolutely no idea what happens next."

KOKI grinned.

"Neither do I."

✦ ✦ ✦

VI. The Boy at the End of the Ocean

As the crew prepared to leave, Lyra carried a small black book aboard the pirate ship.

Unlike the others, its pages had not become empty.

"This isn't a prediction," she explained.

"It's a record."

She opened the book.

An ancient machine appeared above its pages.

Seven enormous rings surrounded a single golden star.

At the center of the machine stood the Meridian Tower.

Lyra's expression darkened.

"Vaelor isn't simply trying to create one perfect history."

She turned another page.

"He is preparing to restart time itself."

Every ocean.

Every civilization.

Every living creature.

All would return to the beginning.

Only one history would be allowed to continue.

Milo held his paper boat tightly.

"What happens to everyone else?"

Lyra looked at him.

"They will never have existed."

Nobody spoke.

Then a small rope fell from the sky.

KOKI recognized its knots immediately.

Finn.

Beyond the final doorway appeared an endless black ocean.

A broken wooden boat drifted beneath a single star.

Inside it sat Finn.

He was holding a rope connected to thousands of sinking ships.

Every time he pulled one ship to safety, another began to sink.

And beneath his little boat, something larger than the ocean itself opened its eyes.

KOKI drew his sword.

"One more friend."

Nox took the wheel.

Brann lifted his hammer.

Veyra raised the sails.

Lyra lit her lantern.

Milo placed his paper boat beside the compass.

The pirate flag rose.

Far beneath the Meridian Tower, Vaelor watched his daughter take her first steps outside the glass chamber.

She looked toward the enormous machine.

"Father, what is that?"

Vaelor knelt beside her.

"Our second chance."

The first of the seven golden rings began to turn.

And across the universe, newborn stars began to grow old.

Sixteen hours remained.
