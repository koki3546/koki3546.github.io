Beyond the Edge of Time
CHAPTER 11

The Last Second of Eternity

When eternity comes to an end, even a single second can change everything.

For the first time in his life, Vaelor was afraid of tomorrow.

The creature standing before him was his own creation.

A being designed to eliminate every imperfection.

And now it had decided that even its creator was imperfect.

KOKI stepped forward.

The creature raised its hand.

The golden star shattered.

Across the universe, millions of clocks stopped.

✦ ✦ ✦

I. The End of Tomorrow

The creature lifted its arms.

The fourth, fifth, and sixth golden rings began to turn simultaneously.

Lyra stared at the burning remains of her black book.

"The countdown is collapsing!"

Outside the tower, entire oceans began rising into the sky.

Mountains transformed into dust.

Stars disappeared before their light could reach the world.

Vaelor looked at the creature.

"What are you?"

Its voice shook the tower.

"I AM THE FINAL TOMORROW."

KOKI tightened his grip upon his sword.

"Tomorrow belongs to everyone."

The creature turned toward him.

"THEN EVERYONE MUST BE CORRECTED."

A wave of golden light exploded from its body.

KOKI grabbed Milo.

Vaelor protected Elara.

The central chamber collapsed beneath their feet.

✦ ✦ ✦

II. The Seven Hearts

Nox crashed through the collapsing wall aboard the pirate ship.

"Need a ride, Captain?"

KOKI laughed.

"You're getting better at arriving late."

Finn threw down the ropes.

Brann pulled Vaelor aboard.

Veyra ordered the fleet to surround the tower.

Lyra looked toward the six rotating rings.

"The engine cannot erase a history while its memories remain connected."

KOKI understood.

"Then let's connect them all."

Finn rang the ship's bell.

Across the ocean, a thousand vessels answered.

Every sailor began telling a story.

Stories of childhood.

Stories of friendship.

Stories of mistakes and forgiveness.

Stories of those who had been loved and lost.

Golden threads emerged from every ship.

They stretched across the sky, connecting thousands of disappearing worlds.

The creature screamed.

For the first time, the Meridian Engine began to lose control.

✦ ✦ ✦

III. The War Against Eternity

The Final Tomorrow transformed.

Its body expanded beyond the tower.

Six enormous arms emerged from its golden armor.

Its crown pierced the clouds.

Within its chest burned the remains of countless stolen stars.

Veyra raised her sword.

"ALL SHIPS, FIRE!"

The ocean erupted with light.

Brann drove his hammer into the tower's foundation.

Finn guided the fleet between falling fragments of time.

Lyra gathered the golden threads into an enormous shield.

Nox steered the pirate ship directly toward the creature's heart.

KOKI climbed onto the mast.

The creature reached toward him.

"YOUR EXISTENCE IS AN ERROR."

KOKI smiled.

"I've been called worse."

He jumped.

His sword struck the creature's crown.

An explosion illuminated the universe.

But beneath the golden armor, another ring began to move.

The seventh ring.

Vaelor's face became pale.

"The final reset."

✦ ✦ ✦

IV. A Father's Impossible Choice

Vaelor opened a hidden passage beneath the deck.

Inside was the original core of the Meridian Engine.

A small crystal containing a single heartbeat.

Elara's heartbeat.

KOKI looked at him.

Vaelor fell to his knees.

"Her original history was destroyed."

"I rebuilt her existence using the engine."

His voice broke.

"If the core is destroyed, she may disappear."

Elara stood behind him.

She had heard everything.

"Father?"

Vaelor turned toward her.

Tears filled his eyes.

"I only wanted you back."

Elara embraced him.

"I know."

She looked toward the thousands of worlds disappearing beyond the ship.

"But I don't want everyone else to go away."

Vaelor held his daughter tightly.

For years, he had believed that love meant refusing to let go.

Now his daughter was teaching him something different.

Love also meant allowing others to live.

✦ ✦ ✦

V. The Smallest Ship in the Universe

Milo placed his paper boat beside the crystal.

Its golden light began to spread.

Lyra watched in astonishment.

The boat contained the memory of Milo's mother.

A memory that had survived the destruction of an entire kingdom.

"Perhaps a history doesn't need the engine to survive," she whispered.

Milo looked at Elara.

"Would you like to sail with us?"

Elara nodded.

Together, they placed their little paper boats upon the crystal.

Two golden lights became one.

The crystal began to change.

Its heartbeat grew stronger.

Vaelor stared at the children.

For centuries, he had searched for a perfect solution.

Two children had discovered a possibility he had never considered.

Not a perfect world.

A shared one.

But before Lyra could complete the connection, the Final Tomorrow tore open the sky.

Its enormous hand descended toward the pirate ship.

KOKI stood between the creature and the children.

"Nobody touches my crew."

✦ ✦ ✦

VI. The Last Second

The seventh ring completed its rotation.

Everything stopped.

The waves.

The ships.

The falling stars.

Even the golden threads connecting the worlds.

Only KOKI remained standing.

The Final Tomorrow appeared before him.

"ONE HISTORY MUST SURVIVE."

Thousands of doorways opened around the little elephant.

Behind one stood his mother.

Behind another waited the island of his childhood.

Behind another was a peaceful world where none of his friends had ever suffered.

The creature extended its hand.

"CHOOSE YOUR PERFECT WORLD."

KOKI looked at every doorway.

Then he looked at his frozen friends.

At Finn's wounded hands.

At Brann's broken hammer.

At Veyra's torn sails.

At Lyra's extinguished lantern.

At Nox's shattered sword.

At Milo and Elara holding their paper boats.

KOKI lowered his sword.

"I don't want a perfect world."

He placed his hand upon the frozen deck.

"I want this one."

A tiny golden light appeared beneath his hand.

Then another.

And another.

From beyond the frozen universe came the sound of a familiar bell.

A thousand voices answered.

NO ONE LEFT BEHIND.

The creature stepped backward.

For the first time, it had encountered something it could not calculate.

KOKI raised his sword one final time.

The last second of eternity began.
