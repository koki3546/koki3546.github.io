KOKI Roadmap

Every great adventure begins with a single brave step.

Phase 1 — The Beginning

Create the KOKI character and identity. Deploy the KOKI token on Base. Launch the official website and establish community channels.

Phase 2 — The Adventure

Expand KOKI's original adventure stories. Introduce new characters, islands, and exciting challenges. Build an engaged global community.

Phase 3 — The New World

Explore illustrated stories, animations, and digital collectibles. Develop KOKI's creative universe through community participation.

Phase 4 — Beyond the Horizon

Explore merchandise, creative partnerships, and long-term opportunities for the KOKI character brand.

This roadmap represents current intentions, not guaranteed commitments. Plans may change as the project develops.
