THE KOKI CHRONICLES · SEASON TWO

The City Without Time

The Twelve Stars — Chapter 03

"I have been waiting for you for a thousand years."

The voice of the black statue shook the towers of Aetheria.

KOKI drew his sword.

"Then I suppose I'm a little late."

Behind him, Brann sighed.

"One day, Captain, I'd like to visit a city where nobody tries to kill us."

The statue stepped down from its pedestal. Golden cracks appeared across its enormous body.

"I am Chronos, guardian of the First Star. You must surrender the child."

KOKI stepped between the giant and Lyra.

"That's not going to happen."

Chronos raised his stone fist.

"Then your voyage ends here."

✦ ✦ ✦

The Battle of the Frozen City

The guardian struck.

KOKI rolled beneath its enormous fist as the marble bridge shattered behind him.

Veyra fired a grappling rope into a nearby tower. Finn grabbed Lyra and swung across the collapsing bridge.

Brann charged forward, bringing his heavy weapon against the guardian's knee.

The impact rang like a thousand bells.

Chronos barely moved.

With a wave of his hand, he stopped time around them.

Pieces of broken stone hung in the air. Veyra froze in mid-swing. Brann became motionless. Even the wind disappeared.

Only KOKI could still move.

A golden spark emerged from the ancient star map inside his coat.

Chronos stared at him.

"Impossible. You carry the light of a star that has not yet awakened."

KOKI tightened his grip on his sword.

"I don't know what you're talking about. But those are my friends."

He leaped onto the floating pieces of rubble, climbing through the motionless air until he reached the guardian's chest.

His sword struck a glowing crack.

A tremendous burst of golden light ripped through the city.

Time began to move again.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Boy at the Window

While the guardian struggled to rise, Lyra ran through the streets.

KOKI followed her into a small house beneath the enormous clock tower.

Inside, a young white fox sat beside a window.

His hands held a little wooden ship.

He was smiling.

But he did not move.

"That is my brother," Lyra whispered. "His name is Milo."

She knelt beside him.

"The last thing I told him was that I would be home tomorrow."

She reached for his hand.

"He has been waiting for that tomorrow ever since."

KOKI looked around the room.

Two unfinished cups of tea. A drawing of a family. A birthday cake with unlit candles.

An entire life interrupted in a single moment.

Outside, Chronos rose again.

"The First Star was stolen," the guardian declared. "Without its light, this city will remain between one second and the next."

Lyra held up her crystal.

"Then take what remains of it."

Chronos shook his head.

"The fragment is too weak. Someone must give the city their own remaining time."

Lyra closed her eyes.

"Take mine."

KOKI caught her hand.

"No."

"But my brother needs me!"

"And when he opens his eyes, don't you think he'll want to see you?"

Lyra began to cry.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Captain's Clock

KOKI climbed the great clock tower.

At its center stood an ancient mechanism surrounding an empty golden chamber.

The resting place of the First Star.

Chronos appeared behind him.

"Do you understand the price, Captain?"

KOKI nodded.

"Take one year from my life."

"One year will not be enough."

"Then take another."

Finn rushed into the chamber.

"Captain, stop!"

Veyra and Brann followed.

Finn placed his hand against the ancient mechanism.

"Take some of mine."

Veyra stepped forward.

"And mine."

Brann folded his arms.

"I was planning to live forever, but I suppose I can spare a little."

KOKI looked at his crew.

One by one, they placed their hands against the golden chamber.

Lyra joined them, holding the crystal between her palms.

Something extraordinary happened.

The crystal began to shine. Not with the light of a single life, but with the warmth of five hearts willing to give something for others.

Chronos lowered his head.

"For a thousand years, I believed time could only be taken."

The guardian opened his enormous hands.

"Perhaps it can also be given."

He placed his own hands against the ancient mechanism.

His stone body began to crumble into golden dust.

The hands of the great clock moved.

Tick.

Then another.

Tick.

Across Aetheria, waterfalls began to flow. Birds spread their wings. Bells rang from every tower.

And in a small house beneath the clock, a little fox opened his eyes.

"Lyra?"

She ran to him and held him tightly.

"I'm home."

Milo smiled.

"You're early."

Lyra laughed through her tears.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Captain Who Destroyed the Stars

That evening, the people of Aetheria filled the streets with music.

For the first time since the disappearance of the First Star, their city was alive.

But KOKI found himself alone beside the remains of Chronos.

Among the broken stones lay a fragment of the guardian's memory.

When KOKI touched it, the world around him disappeared.

He saw twelve stars falling from a burning sky.

He saw oceans turning black. Cities collapsing. Ships swallowed by darkness.

At the center of it all stood a pirate captain.

A black coat. A golden sword. A familiar scar.

And a single eye beneath a pirate hat.

KOKI stepped backward in horror.

The captain was an elephant.

An older elephant.

It was him.

The last voice of Chronos echoed inside his mind.

"Beware the captain wearing your face. He is the one who will destroy the Twelve Stars."

The vision disappeared.

Far beyond Aetheria, a second star began to flicker.

KOKI looked down at his trembling hands.

What if the greatest enemy of his second voyage was not a monster, a king, or an ancient curse?

What if it was himself?
