THE KOKI CHRONICLES · SEASON TWO

The Missing Star

The Twelve Stars — Chapter 02

On the seventh night of their second voyage, a star disappeared.

There was no explosion. No thunder. No warning.

One moment, twelve stars formed a perfect circle above the ocean. The next, only eleven remained.

Finn lowered his telescope.

"Captain... stars aren't supposed to do that."

KOKI unfolded the ancient star map. A single golden point on the parchment was slowly turning black.

Then the map began to bleed light.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Falling Ship

A terrible sound tore through the sky.

Veyra looked up.

"Something's falling!"

An ancient sailing ship descended from the clouds, its sails burning with blue fire. Its broken hull spun helplessly toward the ocean.

Through his telescope, Finn spotted a small figure clinging to the shattered mast.

"There's someone aboard!"

KOKI seized the wheel.

"Full sail! Bring us underneath it!"

Brann stared at him.

"Underneath a falling ship? That's the worst idea you've ever had!"

KOKI grinned.

"Then let's make it a good one."

The crew rushed into action. Veyra fired a grappling rope. Brann secured it around the mainmast, while Finn climbed the rigging.

The burning ship was only seconds from impact.

KOKI climbed onto the railing, wrapped a rope around his waist, and leaped into the storm.

For one terrible moment, he hung between the ocean and the falling wreck.

Then his trunk caught a tiny hand.

"Got you!"

Brann pulled the rope with all his strength. KOKI and the stranger crashed onto the deck just as the ancient ship struck the sea.

A mountainous wave swallowed the horizon.

But their ship survived.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Girl Who Remembered Tomorrow

The rescued stranger was a young white fox wearing a silver cloak.

Her name was Lyra. Around her neck hung a crystal that contained a faint golden light.

"Where did you come from?" Finn asked.

Lyra pointed toward the clouds.

"From a city that no longer exists."

Brann scratched his head.

"Well, that's a cheerful answer."

Lyra did not smile.

"My people were the guardians of the First Star. For a thousand years, its light kept our city alive."

She held up the crystal.

"But something is hunting the stars. Something that existed before the ocean and before the sky."

KOKI studied the fading light.

"What happened to your people?"

Lyra's hands began to tremble.

"They're still there. My little brother is waiting for me."

Tears filled her eyes.

"I promised I would come back. But I don't know if tomorrow will ever come for him."

KOKI remembered the promises he had made during his first voyage. He remembered how it felt to lose someone without knowing whether he would ever see them again.

He knelt beside her.

"A promise doesn't disappear just because the road home is gone. We'll find another road."
✦ ✦ ✦

The City Above the World

At dawn, Lyra placed her crystal in the center of KOKI's star map.

Eleven golden lights appeared. The empty twelfth point began to pulse.

Suddenly, the ocean beneath the ship started flowing upward.

"Captain!" Veyra shouted.

Water rose into the heavens, forming an enormous spiral around their ship. The sails filled with a wind that seemed to come from beneath the world itself.

Slowly, impossibly, the ship lifted from the ocean.

Finn laughed with astonishment.

"We're flying!"

Brann grabbed the mast.

"I was perfectly happy being a pirate when the water stayed beneath us!"

They broke through the clouds.

Before them floated hundreds of islands, connected by ancient bridges. Waterfalls poured endlessly into the sky. Golden towers surrounded an enormous clock whose hands had stopped moving.

It was the most beautiful city KOKI had ever seen.

And the most silent.

No birds flew. No bells rang. Even the waterfalls hung motionless in the air.

Lyra whispered a single word.

"Aetheria."

At the center of the city stood a gigantic black statue. Its stone fingers held an empty, broken crown.

As KOKI's ship approached, the statue opened its eyes.

A voice echoed across the frozen city.

"Welcome, Captain KOKI. I have been waiting for you for a thousand years."

KOKI slowly reached for his sword.

He had never been here before.

So how did it know his name?
