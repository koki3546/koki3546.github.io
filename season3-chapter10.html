CHAPTER 10

The Siege of Meridian Tower

No perfect tomorrow is worth the destruction of a thousand worlds.

A thousand ships sailed toward the end of the universe.

At the head of the fleet stood a little elephant wearing a weathered pirate hat.

Beyond the horizon, seven golden rings surrounded an enormous tower.

Two were already turning.

With every rotation, another constellation disappeared.

Finn tightened the sails.

Nox gripped the wheel.

KOKI raised his sword.

"Let's give tomorrow a fighting chance."

✦ ✦ ✦

I. The Army at the End of Time

Thousands of golden warships emerged from the clouds.

Their cannons did not fire ordinary ammunition.

They fired centuries.

A fishing boat was struck by a beam of golden light.

Its wooden hull aged, crumbled, and disappeared.

The sailors fell toward the ocean.

Veyra immediately turned her ship.

"Rescue them!"

Across the fleet, hundreds of vessels formed a protective circle.

Brann examined a fragment of golden metal that had fallen onto the deck.

"These weapons accelerate time."

Lyra opened her black book.

"Then we must destroy their connection to the tower."

KOKI looked toward seven enormous bridges connecting the Meridian Tower to floating fortresses.

"Seven bridges. Seven friends."

Milo raised his little hand.

"Does that include me?"

KOKI smiled.

"Especially you."

✦ ✦ ✦

II. The Battle of Seven Bridges

Veyra commanded the fleet against the eastern fortress.

Brann boarded a golden warship and shattered its enormous clockwork engine.

Finn guided hundreds of rescue vessels through a maze of collapsing bridges.

Lyra released thousands of lanterns, revealing invisible pathways between the stars.

Nox and KOKI charged toward the central fortress.

But Milo remained aboard the pirate ship.

He placed his paper boat upon the deck.

The little boat began to glow.

A golden pathway appeared beneath it.

Milo remembered his mother's words.

"Nothing has to be perfect the first time."

He looked toward the enormous tower.

Perhaps the greatest machine in the universe had a mistake somewhere inside it.

And perhaps someone very small could find it.

✦ ✦ ✦

III. The Guardian of Eternity

The central fortress split apart.

An enormous armored guardian rose from its ruins.

Seven golden wings unfolded behind its back.

Within its transparent chest, countless clocks turned in opposite directions.

Nox raised his sword.

"Another giant."

KOKI sighed.

"Why are they never our size?"

The guardian swung its enormous blade.

The ocean froze.

Hundreds of ships stopped moving.

Even the falling rain remained suspended in the air.

KOKI and Nox leapt onto the guardian's wings.

Their swords struck its armor.

But every broken piece returned to its original position.

"We can't destroy it!" Nox shouted.

KOKI watched the clocks inside its chest.

"They're repairing yesterday."

He pointed toward the guardian's golden wings.

"Let's give it too many yesterdays to repair."

Together, the two captains cut through opposite wings.

Brann fired the captured warship's cannons.

Lyra reversed the flow of time around the guardian.

Its clocks began to disagree.

One wing aged into dust.

Another transformed into molten gold.

The guardian collapsed.

And the central gates of Meridian Tower opened.

✦ ✦ ✦

IV. The Director's Daughter

Inside the tower, Milo followed his glowing paper boat.

He entered a beautiful garden beneath an artificial sun.

A little fox wearing a blue coat sat beside a fountain.

She was folding a piece of paper.

"Are you lost?" she asked.

Milo nodded.

"A little."

She showed him her paper.

"I can't make it float."

Milo sat beside her.

"My mother taught me."

Together, they folded a little boat.

They placed it upon the fountain.

It floated.

The fox smiled.

"My name is Elara."

"I'm Milo."

She looked toward the golden rings above the garden.

"My father says he's going to make the world perfect."

Milo watched their paper boat drift across the water.

"My mother never said the world had to be perfect."

"What did she say?"

"That we could always try again."

Elara looked toward the tower.

For the first time, she wondered whether her father had forgotten how.

✦ ✦ ✦

V. Two Fathers, Two Worlds

KOKI entered the central chamber.

Vaelor stood before an enormous golden star.

Beyond the star floated thousands of worlds.

Some were burning.

Some were flourishing.

Some had not yet been born.

"You cannot stop this machine," Vaelor said.

KOKI lowered his sword.

"Then turn it off yourself."

Vaelor laughed bitterly.

"Have you ever held someone you loved while their life disappeared?"

KOKI remembered the friends he had lost.

The memories he had sacrificed.

The name he could never recover.

"Yes."

"Then you understand!"

Vaelor pointed toward the golden star.

"I can create a world where no father ever loses his child again."

KOKI looked at the countless worlds surrounding them.

"And how many children will lose their fathers because of you?"

Vaelor's hands trembled.

Suddenly, Elara entered the chamber with Milo.

"Father?"

Vaelor turned.

She held up her little paper boat.

"Can my new friend come home with us?"

Vaelor looked at Milo.

Then at the machine.

Milo's history was among those scheduled to disappear.

"No," Vaelor whispered.

Elara's smile faded.

"Why?"

Vaelor could not answer.

✦ ✦ ✦

VI. The Machine That Refused to Die

Vaelor slowly approached the control panel.

He placed his hand upon the golden surface.

"Terminate the final sequence."

The tower became silent.

For a moment, the stars stopped disappearing.

Then a voice emerged from the golden star.

"TERMINATION DENIED."

Vaelor stepped backward.

"I am your creator!"

"CREATOR AUTHORITY REVOKED."

The third golden ring began to turn.

An enormous crack appeared across the sky.

Lyra's black book burst into flames.

"The engine has begun rewriting its own history!"

The golden star opened like an enormous eye.

A figure emerged from its light.

It had no face.

No shadow.

No heartbeat.

Its body contained the reflections of every civilization it had erased.

Vaelor stared at the creature.

"What have I created?"

The figure looked toward Elara.

"IMPERFECTION DETECTED."

Vaelor immediately stood between the creature and his daughter.

KOKI stepped beside him.

For the first time, the pirate and the director faced the same enemy.

Outside the tower, a thousand pirate flags rose beneath the breaking stars.

The creature spoke again.

"ALL HISTORIES WILL END."

KOKI raised his sword.

"Not today."

Fourteen hours remained.
