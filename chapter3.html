THE KOKI CHRONICLES · CHAPTER 03

The Forgotten Island

Some places are forgotten for a reason.

The eyes beneath the sea did not blink.

KOKI stood frozen at the wheel. Two enormous golden eyes watched him from beneath the black water.

Finn seized his arm.

"Don't look at them!"

KOKI turned toward the little fox.

"What is that thing?"

Finn's ears trembled.

"Something that should still be sleeping."

The golden eyes sank into the darkness. A powerful current caught the boat and pulled it forward.

KOKI fought the wheel, but the broken compass began to spin. Its needle flashed blue, then stopped, pointing toward a patch of empty mist.

The fog parted.

An island appeared where no island had been a moment before.

✦ ✦ ✦

At dawn, KOKI guided the damaged boat into a sheltered bay.

Dozens of shipwrecks lay scattered along the coast. Some were covered in coral. Others looked as though they had sunk yesterday.

Above the jungle rose an enormous mountain, its cliffs carved into the shape of a grinning skull.

A rusted bell hung between its stone teeth.

"The Forgotten Island," Finn whispered.

KOKI stared at him.

"You've been here before?"

Finn looked away.

"No. But my father told me about it."

He reached beneath his blue scarf and brought out the brass key.

Its engraved bell glowed faintly.

"He said that if I ever heard three bells at sea, I must never follow the sound."

KOKI glanced at the mountain.

"You could have told me that earlier."

Finn lowered his head.

"I was afraid you would leave me behind."

KOKI said nothing for a moment.

Then he knelt beside his friend.

"I didn't pull you out of that wreck just to abandon you on another one."

Finn gave a small, uncertain smile.

✦ ✦ ✦

They climbed through the jungle toward the skull-shaped mountain.

Ancient statues lined the path. Each depicted a different pirate captain, but every statue had its eyes carved away.

At the foot of the mountain, they discovered a stone doorway marked with the same bell symbol as Finn's key.

Before KOKI could touch it, a blade struck the stone beside his ear.

"Step away from the door."

A tall black raven emerged from the shadows.

She wore a weathered captain's coat and carried a curved silver sword. A scar crossed one side of her beak.

"My name is Captain Veyra," she said. "And that key does not belong to you."

Finn stumbled backward.

"You..."

Veyra's sharp eyes narrowed.

"So the fox survived."

KOKI moved in front of Finn.

"Who are you? What do you want with him?"

Veyra raised her sword.

"Give me the key, little pirate. Before the island takes you both."

KOKI gripped a fallen branch and held it like a sword.

Veyra glanced at the branch.

"You cannot be serious."

"I've been told that before," KOKI replied.

He charged.

Veyra swept his makeshift weapon aside and knocked him to the ground.

Before she could strike again, the mountain shook.

A deep bell rang from inside the stone.

✦ ✦ ✦

The jungle fell silent.

Then, from the distant bay, came the sound of creaking timber.

KOKI looked toward the shore.

The wrecks were moving.

Broken masts rose from the water. Torn sails filled with a wind that touched nothing else.

One by one, the sunken ships returned to the surface.

Finn's face turned pale.

"My father said the island returns every lost ship at sunset."

Veyra looked at the sinking sun.

"And every living soul still on its shore must join their crews."

KOKI ran toward the beach.

Their little boat was still there.

He could reach it. He could sail away before sunset.

But behind him, Finn cried out.

A chain had burst from the stone doorway and wrapped around the fox's ankle.

The chain pulled him toward the darkness.

"KOKI! Help me!"

KOKI stopped.

The beach lay only a short distance away. The boat was his last chance.

He thought of the home he had left. Of the sea he had dreamed of crossing. Of the life he might never see again.

Then he turned around.

"Finn! Hold on!"

He ran back and wrapped his trunk around the chain.

The metal burned against his skin. He pulled until his knees buckled.

The chain did not break.

Veyra watched him struggle.

"Run, elephant! You can still escape!"

KOKI shook his head.

"Not without him."

Veyra hesitated.

Then she drove her silver sword into the chain's glowing link.

The metal shattered.

KOKI caught Finn before he fell.

Together, they stumbled away as the doorway slammed shut.

✦ ✦ ✦

The sun disappeared beneath the horizon.

From the bay came a chorus of footsteps on empty decks.

KOKI, Finn, and Veyra reached a narrow ledge above the beach.

Their boat had vanished.

In its place floated a fleet of ancient ships, their decks crowded with silent figures.

Veyra lowered her sword.

"You had a chance to escape," she said quietly. "Why did you come back?"

KOKI looked at Finn, who was trembling beside him.

"Because a captain doesn't leave his crew behind."

Veyra studied the little elephant for a long moment.

Then she reached into her coat and produced a torn page from an ancient captain's journal.

On it was a drawing of the same brass key Finn carried.

Beneath the drawing were five words:

"The Bell Must Never Wake."

Finn swallowed.

"What does that mean?"

Veyra looked toward the sea.

"Your father tried to stop it."

Finn stepped forward.

"You knew my father?"

Before Veyra could answer, a tremendous shadow passed over them.

Beyond the wrecks, a vast black ship rose from the ocean.

Its sails were stitched from darkness. Its figurehead was a crowned skull. No lantern burned aboard it, yet every window glowed blue.

The mountain bell began to swing.

Veyra's voice fell to a whisper.

"The Mourning Crown."

KOKI watched the ghost ship turn slowly toward the island.

On its highest deck stood a solitary figure in a long coat.

The figure raised one hand.

And from the depths below the ship, two enormous golden eyes opened again.
