THE KOKI CHRONICLES · SEASON TWO

The Kingdom Beyond the Storm

The Twelve Stars — Chapter 06

The storm had been waiting for them.

For three days, KOKI's ship sailed north, following the golden fragment given to them by Seraphine, the keeper of the Second Star.

The sea grew colder. The sun disappeared. And the stars above them began to vanish, one by one.

On the fourth night, the ocean rose like a wall.

A ring of black clouds stretched from horizon to horizon. Lightning crawled across it like the veins of a sleeping giant.

Finn lowered his telescope.

"Captain... that's not a storm."

Veyra studied the rotating clouds.

"It's a fortress."

Brann tightened the ropes around the mainmast.

"Then how do we get through?"

KOKI unfolded the ancient star map.

The golden fragment glowed.

A narrow passage appeared between the lightning strikes.

KOKI gripped the ship's wheel.

"We didn't sail this far to turn back at the door."

"All hands!" he shouted.

"Into the storm!"

✦ ✦ ✦

The Sea of Thunder

The first wave struck like a falling mountain.

The ship climbed its towering face, then plunged into darkness.

Lightning shattered the water beside the hull.

A hundred silver shapes rose from the waves.

They were ships.

Ancient warships made of rain and lightning, their sails torn, their crews nothing but shadows.

One ghostly vessel charged toward them.

"Incoming!" Finn screamed.

Veyra swung the forward cannon around.

"Fire!"

The cannonball passed straight through the approaching ship.

The ghost vessel struck their hull.

The impact sent Brann sliding across the deck.

KOKI turned the wheel sharply.

The ghost ship vanished behind them.

Then three more appeared.

Finn looked at the star map.

"Captain! They're not chasing us."

"What?"

"They're repeating the same movements. Every thirty seconds!"

Veyra stared into the storm.

"An ancient battle. Trapped inside a single moment."

KOKI remembered Aetheria, the city that had been frozen in time.

"Then we don't fight the ships."

He watched the lightning.

"Finn! Count the flashes!"

"Seven... eight... nine!"

KOKI waited.

"Now!"

He threw the wheel to starboard.

Their ship slipped between two ghostly warships just as they collided.

A wall of white fire erupted behind them.

Ahead, the clouds opened.

And beyond them...

stood a kingdom.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Kingdom That Forgot the World

Golden towers rose from enormous white cliffs.

Waterfalls poured into a blue sea that glowed beneath the darkness.

Bridges of crystal connected floating gardens and ancient castles.

At the center of the kingdom stood a magnificent palace, surrounded by twelve stone rings.

But something was wrong.

The streets were empty.

The gardens were overgrown.

And every clock in the kingdom showed the same time.

Twelve minutes before midnight.

KOKI and his companions stepped onto the silent harbor.

A voice called from above.

"Leave your weapons."

Armored figures emerged from the ruined watchtowers.

They were not ghosts.

They were living guardians, their armor marked with a silver sun.

Their commander, an elderly silver wolf, stepped forward.

"I am Commander Edrin, last guardian of the Kingdom of Auralis."

His gaze settled on KOKI's face.

Then his expression changed.

He dropped his sword.

"Impossible..."

KOKI frowned.

"What's wrong?"

Edrin took a step backward.

"That face."

"That scar."

"That eyepatch."

His voice trembled.

"The captain who destroyed our kingdom has returned."
✦ ✦ ✦

The Captain in the Portrait

The guardians surrounded KOKI.

Brann stepped between them.

"Careful."

Veyra raised one wing.

"We didn't come here to fight."

Edrin studied the young captain.

"Then come with me. There is something you must see."

He led them through the deserted palace.

Its walls were covered with enormous paintings.

The first showed the kingdom beneath a brilliant sun.

The second showed a black star falling from the heavens.

The third showed a pirate captain standing before the royal throne.

KOKI stopped.

The captain in the painting was an older version of himself.

The Shadow Captain.

But beside him stood another figure.

A small elephant.

No older than a child.

The child was holding a golden lantern.

KOKI stared at the painting.

A strange pain pierced his head.

He heard laughter.

A bell ringing.

And a voice whispering:

"Keep the light safe, little captain."

KOKI stumbled.

Finn caught his arm.

"Captain!"

KOKI looked at the painted child.

"Who is that?"

Edrin answered quietly.

"We never knew his name."

"The older captain brought him here forty years ago."

KOKI shook his head.

"Forty years?"

"Yes."

Edrin pointed toward the painting.

"The child disappeared on the night the storm was born."

KOKI looked again.

The child's face was unmistakably his own.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Clock Beneath the Palace

Before anyone could speak, a deep metallic sound echoed through the palace.

Dong.

Every clock moved forward by one minute.

Eleven minutes before midnight.

Edrin turned pale.

"The clock has moved."

Veyra looked toward the ceiling.

"What happens at midnight?"

Edrin whispered:

"The storm opens."

"And the kingdom disappears."

The floor shook.

Far below them, an enormous mechanism began turning.

KOKI drew his sword.

"Show us."

Edrin led the crew into a vast chamber beneath the palace.

There stood an enormous clock, larger than any ship KOKI had ever seen.

Twelve golden chains stretched from its center into the darkness.

Eleven were still intact.

One had been broken.

At the heart of the mechanism floated a black crystal.

A red light pulsed inside it.

KOKI recognized the rhythm.

It was the same pulse he had felt beneath the Island of Whispering Bones.

"The Hollow King."

Edrin nodded.

"This kingdom was built to hide the oldest memory of his prison."

"Forty years ago, the Shadow Captain tried to destroy that memory."

"He failed. But the attempt trapped Auralis inside an eternal storm."

Another bell rang.

Ten minutes before midnight.

Suddenly, the black crystal cracked.

A gigantic armored hand emerged from within.

Then another.

A towering creature of black metal pulled itself into the chamber.

Its head was shaped like a broken clock.

Its chest contained a spinning red star.

Edrin drew his sword.

"The Midnight Warden."

"It has awakened!"

✦ ✦ ✦

Ten Minutes to Midnight

The Warden swung its enormous arm.

Stone pillars exploded.

Brann charged forward and struck its knee with a heavy iron hammer.

The blow barely slowed it.

Veyra leaped onto a broken column and fired at the red crystal inside its chest.

The crystal flashed.

The Warden staggered.

"That's its weakness!" she shouted.

But the creature immediately covered its chest with both arms.

Finn climbed the enormous clock, searching for a way to stop the mechanism.

"Captain! The Warden is connected to the broken chain!"

KOKI looked up.

The severed chain was wrapped around the creature's back.

He understood.

"The first lock."

"The same one that was broken when the First Star was taken."

Another bell rang.

Nine minutes.

The Warden seized Edrin and lifted him into the air.

KOKI ran.

He jumped onto the creature's arm, climbed across its shoulder, and drove his sword into a joint beneath its neck.

The Warden roared.

Edrin fell.

Brann caught him.

But KOKI was thrown against the giant clock.

His sword slipped from his hand.

The Warden advanced.

A voice spoke inside his mind.

"Do you remember me, Captain?"

KOKI froze.

The voice was not the Warden's.

It was the Hollow King's.

"You have come so far to recover what you lost."

"Would you like to see the night you were born?"

The black crystal flared.

The chamber vanished.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Memory That Was Stolen

KOKI stood in a field of golden light.

Before him was a great hall filled with twelve burning stars.

A small elephant stood at the center of the hall.

The child from the painting.

Beside him knelt an older captain with a black eyepatch.

The Shadow Captain.

He held a golden lantern against the child's chest.

"Listen carefully," he whispered.

"The light cannot stay with me."

The child began to cry.

"Will I forget you?"

The older captain closed his eye.

"Yes."

"Will I forget everything?"

"Almost everything."

The child reached toward him.

"Then how will I know what I'm supposed to do?"

The Shadow Captain placed his hand over the child's heart.

"You won't remember the promise. But you will remember how to keep it."

The golden lantern shattered.

Its light entered the child's chest.

KOKI felt a burning pain beneath his own coat.

He looked down.

A tiny golden symbol was glowing over his heart.

The same symbol Seraphine had shown him.

The Thirteenth Light.

Then the vision changed.

The Shadow Captain stood alone, surrounded by fire.

He whispered:

"Forgive me."

And the memory shattered.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Light That Refused to Die

KOKI opened his eye.

The Midnight Warden stood directly above him.

Its enormous fist descended.

"Captain!" Finn screamed.

KOKI rolled aside.

The fist struck the floor, shattering the stone.

He looked toward the clock.

Seven minutes remained.

KOKI pressed a hand to his chest.

The golden symbol was still glowing.

He remembered Seraphine's words.

The Twelve Stars could be bound without being broken.

KOKI looked at the severed chain.

It was not attached to the clock anymore.

But its broken ends still reached toward each other.

Waiting.

"Finn! Bring me the map!"

Finn leaped down from the gears and threw it toward him.

KOKI caught it.

The golden fragment of the Second Star flared inside its folds.

A circle of light formed around the broken chain.

The Warden lunged.

"Brann! Hold it!"

Brann wrapped an anchor chain around the creature's legs.

Veyra fired her final shot into the red crystal.

The Warden fell to one knee.

KOKI raced forward.

He did not strike the crystal.

Instead, he pressed his glowing hand against the broken chain.

A burst of golden light filled the chamber.

The chain began to reconnect.

But the light flickered.

The First Star was still missing.

KOKI gritted his teeth.

"Come on..."

The Warden rose again.

Its enormous hand reached for Finn.

KOKI released the chain and pulled his friend aside.

The golden light weakened.

The Hollow King laughed.

"Choose, little captain."

"The seal..."

"Or your friend."

KOKI looked at Finn.

Then at Veyra and Brann.

And finally at the golden chains.

He remembered the trial on the Island of Whispering Bones.

There had never been only two choices.

"Everyone!" he shouted.

"Grab the chain!"

Brann seized the first link.

Veyra grabbed the next.

Finn wrapped both arms around the final link.

Edrin and his guardians joined them.

Together, they pulled.

The golden symbol on KOKI's chest shone brighter than the sun.

For one extraordinary moment, the broken ends met.

The Warden screamed.

Its red crystal shattered.

The enormous creature collapsed into a thousand fragments.

The clock stopped.

One minute before midnight.

Silence.

Then the golden chain slowly separated again.

KOKI stared in disbelief.

Edrin approached.

"You held the prison together."

"But only for a moment."

KOKI looked at his friends.

"A moment is enough to prove it can be done."

✦ ✦ ✦

The Door Beneath the Throne

With the Warden destroyed, the storm above Auralis began to weaken.

For the first time in forty years, sunlight touched the palace towers.

The people of the kingdom emerged from hidden shelters, staring at the brightening sky.

Edrin led KOKI to the ancient royal throne.

Behind it stood a door marked with the thirteenth symbol.

"No key has ever opened this door."

KOKI placed his hand against the golden mark.

The door opened immediately.

Inside was a small chamber.

There were no treasures.

No weapons.

Only a wooden table and a single sealed letter.

On its envelope were three words.

"For the Captain."

KOKI broke the seal.

The handwriting was familiar.

It belonged to the Shadow Captain.

The letter read:

"If you are reading this, then the Thirteenth Light has awakened inside you.

Do not trust the memories the Hollow King shows you.

And whatever happens, do not let me reach the final star."

KOKI turned the page.

There was one final sentence.

"If I reach it, I will remember why I created you."

KOKI's hands trembled.

"Created... me?"

Behind him, Finn stared at the letter.

Veyra said nothing.

Brann slowly lowered his head.

Far beyond the kingdom, a black warship emerged from the thinning storm.

At its bow stood the Shadow Captain.

He watched the sunlight fall across Auralis.

Then he whispered:

"So you found the letter."

A terrible sadness crossed his face.

He opened his hand.

Above his palm floated the stolen First Star.

Its golden light had begun turning black.

And from somewhere deep within it, a voice whispered:

"Six locks remain asleep.

Wake them for me."

The Shadow Captain closed his fist around the star.

"No."

He looked toward KOKI's ship.

"Not this time."
