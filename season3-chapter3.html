CHAPTER 03

The Market of Stolen Hours

Some things are worth more than time.

The first thing Milo forgot was his mother's face.

He sat beneath the broken telescope, staring at the little wooden ship in his hands.

"Captain?"

KOKI sat beside him.

"What is it?"

"I know I had a mother."

Milo's fingers trembled.

"But I can't remember what she looked like."

KOKI felt his heart sink.

Yan examined the device around Milo's wrist.

Another golden light had disappeared.

"Twenty-two hours," she whispered.

KOKI stood.

"Where do they keep the stolen hours?"

Yan looked toward the dark streets below.

"There is a place the Council pretends does not exist."

She opened an ancient map.

"The Market of Stolen Hours."

✦ ✦ ✦

I. The Price of a Lifetime

Beneath the beautiful streets of Aevora lay another city.

A city without sunlight.

Thousands of lanterns illuminated narrow passages filled with merchants, mechanical creatures, and travelers wearing silver masks.

But nobody sold food.

Nobody sold gold.

They sold time.

"Ten years of youth!"

"One perfect summer!"

"Forget your greatest regret!"

KOKI watched an elderly merchant exchange a golden coin for a small glass bottle.

The merchant drank its contents.

His wrinkles disappeared.

His hands became young again.

Beside him, a poor sailor suddenly collapsed.

His hair turned white.

KOKI stopped walking.

"What happened to him?"

Yan answered quietly.

"The merchant bought ten years of his life."

Brann clenched his fists.

"You can buy someone else's years?"

"If they agree to sell them."

KOKI looked at the exhausted sailor.

"And if they have no other way to feed their families?"

Yan said nothing.

KOKI pulled his hat lower.

"This isn't a market."

His eye narrowed.

"It's a prison."

✦ ✦ ✦

II. The Merchant of Tomorrow

At the end of the underground street stood a shop filled with enormous clocks.

Behind its counter sat an ancient tortoise wearing a dark purple coat.

His name was Orven.

One of his eyes had been replaced by a mechanical lens.

"Captain KOKI," he said.

"I have been expecting you."

KOKI placed his sword on the counter.

"I'm looking for a stolen hour."

Orven smiled.

"Everyone is."

"An hour belonging to a boy named Milo."

The merchant's smile disappeared.

He closed the shop door.

"That hour is not for sale."

"Then where is it?"

Orven opened a golden ledger.

Thousands of names appeared across its pages.

Some were glowing.

Others were slowly fading.

He turned to a page near the end.

Milo's name appeared in silver.

Beneath it were twenty-three golden circles.

One had already turned black.

"The Council has identified twenty-three moments that made the boy's existence possible."

KOKI stared at the circles.

"Can we recover them?"

"Perhaps."

Orven pointed toward the deepest part of the underground city.

"The first stolen hour is being transported to the Meridian Tower tonight."

KOKI picked up his sword.

"Then we'll take it back."

✦ ✦ ✦

III. The Train Beneath the Ocean

A mechanical railway stretched beneath the island.

Its tracks disappeared into a tunnel surrounded by seawater.

An enormous golden train waited at the platform.

Inside its armored carriages were thousands of stolen hours.

Meridian soldiers guarded every entrance.

Finn studied the railway.

"That's going to be difficult."

Veyra smiled.

"When has that ever stopped us?"

The train began to move.

KOKI climbed onto a nearby tower.

"Nox! With me!"

The two captains jumped.

They landed on the roof of the final carriage.

Brann and Veyra followed aboard a stolen maintenance vehicle.

Lyra and Yan hurried toward the railway control station.

Finn remained with Milo.

The golden train accelerated.

KOKI drew his sword.

The tunnel opened into an enormous transparent passage beneath the ocean.

Outside, ancient sea creatures swam through the darkness.

Inside, the Meridian Guard emerged from the carriages.

Their golden spears began to glow.

Nox grinned.

"Just like old times?"

KOKI smiled.

"Let's make some new ones."

The soldiers charged.

✦ ✦ ✦

IV. The Battle of the Golden Railway

KOKI blocked the first spear.

Sparks scattered across the roof.

Nox struck a second soldier, sending its mechanical weapon spinning into the darkness.

The train raced through the underwater tunnel.

Suddenly, enormous metal arms unfolded from its engine.

The entire locomotive began transforming.

Brann stared from the maintenance vehicle.

"That is definitely not a normal train!"

The machine rose above the railway.

Its engine became the head of a golden serpent.

Its carriages twisted into a mechanical body.

KOKI grabbed a railing as the creature turned upside down.

Nox nearly fell.

KOKI caught his arm.

"Not today!"

Together, they climbed toward the engine.

The serpent opened its enormous jaws.

Golden light gathered inside its mouth.

At the control station, Lyra discovered a lever connected to the railway's power supply.

"Yan! What happens if we reverse this?"

Yan stared at the mechanism.

"The train will lose its borrowed time."

"Good."

Lyra pulled the lever.

The golden serpent froze.

Its mechanical armor began falling apart.

KOKI leapt through the collapsing machinery.

His sword shattered the lock of the final armored carriage.

Inside, thousands of glass spheres illuminated the darkness.

And among them, one small sphere shone with a familiar silver light.

✦ ✦ ✦

V. The Hour of a Paper Boat

KOKI reached toward the silver sphere.

The world disappeared.

He stood beside a quiet river.

A young mouse was sitting beneath a tree.

Beside him sat an older mouse wearing a simple blue dress.

She was folding a piece of paper.

"Like this," she said gently.

The little mouse tried to copy her.

His paper boat collapsed.

He began to cry.

The woman laughed softly.

"Don't worry, Milo."

She placed another piece of paper in his hands.

"Nothing has to be perfect the first time."

Together, they folded a new boat.

They placed it upon the river.

The little boat floated away.

Milo smiled.

KOKI watched in silence.

This was not an hour before Milo's birth.

It was an hour that had taught him how to hope.

The Council was not merely erasing the events that created his life.

It was dismantling everything that made him who he was.

KOKI closed his hand around the sphere.

"I'm bringing this home."

✦ ✦ ✦

VI. Something Worth More Than Time

The railway collapsed behind them.

KOKI and Nox escaped aboard the maintenance vehicle.

Brann laughed as they raced toward the city.

"Next time, Captain, let's steal an ordinary ship!"

When they returned to the observatory, Milo was asleep.

KOKI placed the silver sphere beside him.

Its light flowed into the boy's chest.

Milo opened his eyes.

For a moment, he stared at the ceiling.

Then tears began rolling down his cheeks.

"I remember."

KOKI knelt beside him.

"What do you remember?"

"My mother."

Milo smiled.

"She taught me how to make paper boats."

KOKI embraced him.

Across the room, Yan examined the golden device.

One of its missing lights had returned.

But the countdown had not stopped.

Twenty-one hours remained.

"We recovered one hour," Yan said.

"But the Council still has the others."

KOKI looked toward the Meridian Tower.

"Then we keep going."

Suddenly, Orven's golden ledger opened by itself.

A new name appeared beneath Milo's.

Finn.

Then another.

Veyra.

Brann.

Lyra.

Nox.

Finally, a seventh name appeared.

KOKI.

Yan stepped backward.

"No..."

Every name began to glow crimson.

Far above the city, Director Vaelor stood before an enormous clock.

"Begin the correction."

Seven golden needles moved simultaneously.

And somewhere in the harbor, KOKI's ship began to disappear.

The Council was no longer hunting one boy.

It was erasing the entire crew.
