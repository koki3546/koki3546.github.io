THE KOKI CHRONICLES · SEASON TWO

The First Falling Star

The Twelve Stars — Chapter 08

The sea had turned silver.

Not from moonlight.
Not from the dawn.

Something beneath its surface was shining upward, as though an enormous eye had opened on the ocean floor.

KOKI stood at the bow, staring at the dark crater marked on the ancient star map.

The words from the Mirror of Origins would not leave him.

"Please... don't let him remember my real name."

It had been his own voice.
A child's voice.
A voice from a memory he did not possess.

Finn joined him with the battered telescope.

"Captain, the water ahead is moving against the wind."

KOKI raised his head.

Far across the Silver Sea, the horizon bent into a perfect circle.

Above it hung a single star, burning blue.

Then the star began to fall.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Sky That Broke

At first there was no sound.

The star descended through the clouds, dragging a long ribbon of blue fire behind it.

Veyra unfolded the star map. Its twelve golden symbols were shaking.

"The First Seal," she whispered. "He's turning it against itself."

Brann looked up.

"How do you fight a falling star?"

"We don't," KOKI answered. "We save everyone beneath it."

On the far side of the crater, a small fishing vessel was trapped inside the rising current.

Someone stood on its deck, waving both arms.

Milo.

He and Lyra had gone ahead at dawn to search for the Last Witness.

Now their little boat was being pulled toward the center of the silver whirlpool.

"MILO!" KOKI shouted.

Lyra tried to steer away, but the current snapped their rudder.

KOKI seized a rope.

"Finn, bring us alongside! Brann, secure the mast! Veyra, keep your eyes on the star!"

Then the heavens screamed.

The falling star struck the sea.

A wall of blue fire erupted from the crater. Water climbed into the sky, carrying broken ships and ancient stones with it.

KOKI jumped before anyone could stop him.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Hand Beneath the Waves

He landed on the fishing boat just as its deck split apart.

Lyra caught a rope thrown from Finn's ship.

"Take Milo!" she cried.

But Milo had already fallen.

For one terrible heartbeat, KOKI saw the boy's hand disappear beneath the black water.

He dove after him.

The sea was colder than ice. Broken timber spun around him.

Far below, a vast blue light pulsed inside the crater.

KOKI found Milo caught beneath a shattered beam.

He pushed against the wood.

It would not move.

His lungs burned.

He remembered the little wooden ship Milo had given him, the one he still carried inside his coat.

He remembered the boy saying that every captain needed something worth coming home for.

KOKI planted his feet against the wreckage and pushed again.

The beam broke.

He wrapped one arm around Milo and kicked toward the surface.

But the current reversed.

Something enormous was breathing beneath them.

And the sea pulled them down.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Prison Under the World

Light vanished.

Then twelve chains appeared in the darkness, each forged from the radiance of a different star.

Eleven stretched across a colossal stone gate.

The twelfth lay broken on the ocean floor.

Beyond the gate, a figure wearing a crown of black fire slowly opened its eyes.

The Hollow King.

"At last," the creature whispered. "The light has returned to its beginning."

KOKI held Milo tightly.

"You won't touch him."

The Hollow King laughed.

"I do not want the child. I want what you have forgotten."

Beside the broken chain stood an older elephant in a black captain's coat.

The Shadow Captain.

His sword was buried in the stone, holding back the spreading darkness.

"KOKI!" he shouted. "The gate is opening. Get the boy out!"

"Tell me my name!" KOKI demanded.

The old captain's expression changed.

"Not here. Not where he can hear it."

The Hollow King leaned forward.

"Then I shall remember it for you."

Black fire rushed through the broken chain.

The Shadow Captain pulled his sword free and struck the gate with all his strength.

A corridor of golden light opened above KOKI.

"GO!"

"What about you?"

The old captain smiled sadly.

"I have spent forty years trying to save a future.

You still have a life to live."

The golden current seized KOKI and Milo and hurled them toward the surface.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Last Witness

They burst into the air beneath a sky filled with falling ash.

Finn caught Milo. Lyra pulled him into her arms, trembling as she heard him cough.

Brann hauled KOKI aboard the remains of the larger ship.

"You always choose the hardest way to swim," Brann muttered.

KOKI managed a tired smile.

But the star's impact had revealed something impossible.

At the center of the crater stood a staircase of white stone, rising from the sea toward a door suspended above the water.

An old woman waited upon its highest step.

She was a silver sea turtle, her shell covered in constellations that moved as she breathed.

Veyra stared at her.

"The Last Witness."

The woman nodded.

"I watched the stars become chains. I watched the Thirteenth Light divided. And I watched the first captain make a promise he could not keep."

KOKI climbed the staircase alone.

"Did the Shadow Captain create me?"

"No," she replied.

"Then who am I?"

The Last Witness looked past him toward his friends.

"You are the one life the Hollow King could never predict."

She touched the golden mark above his heart.

Images flashed before KOKI's eyes.

A child standing in a golden chamber.

Twelve stars bound in a circle.

An older captain kneeling before a door.

And a voice, speaking a name that vanished the moment KOKI tried to hear it.

"The ancient rite did not make you," the Witness said.

"It gave you a choice that the older captain had already lost."

KOKI clenched his fists.

"Why would anyone hide my name?"

"Because names are doors. And the Hollow King has been waiting for someone to open yours."

✦ ✦ ✦

The Choice of a Captain

The staircase shook.

From the crater rose a hand made of black water and broken starlight.

The Hollow King's voice thundered across the sea.

"GIVE ME THE THIRTEENTH LIGHT!"

The Last Witness stepped back.

"There is one way to restore the First Seal. But you must surrender the memory of the name you seek."

KOKI stared at her.

"If I do, will I ever know who I was?"

"Perhaps not."

Below him, Finn struggled to keep the damaged ship afloat.

Brann held a snapped mast in place. Veyra fought the storm. Lyra cradled Milo against her chest.

KOKI looked at the people who had followed him beyond the edge of every map.

He remembered Finn's words on the night before they reached the mirror.

Whatever happened before, everything after was real.

KOKI took the wooden ship from his coat.

Its tiny sails glowed gold.

"Then take the memory," he said.

The Witness raised her head.

"Are you certain?"

"I don't need to know who I used to be
to know who I choose to become."

He pressed the wooden ship against the golden mark on his chest.

A brilliant light burst outward.

It traveled across the sea to Finn, Brann, Veyra, Lyra, and Milo, then returned to KOKI like a thousand hands reaching back.

The broken chain rose from the depths.

Its scattered links joined in the sky.

For one breath, the First Star shone brighter than the sun.

Then its chain struck the stone gate beneath the ocean.

The Hollow King screamed.

The black hand shattered into rain.

And the Silver Sea fell silent.

✦ ✦ ✦

Eleven Stars in the Dark

By sunset, the storm had passed.

Milo sat wrapped in a blanket beside Lyra.

He looked up as KOKI approached.

"Captain... did you save my ship?"

KOKI looked at the small wooden toy, now cracked along its hull.

"Not the big one," he said. "But I kept this one."

Milo smiled.

Finn handed KOKI the battered telescope.

"So. Did you find your answer?"

KOKI watched the first stars appear.

"Not the answer I expected."

Veyra unfolded the ancient map.

Its First Star was glowing again.

But the other eleven symbols had begun to move.

They were no longer scattered across the world.

They were drawing together.

One by one.

As though summoned to war.

Deep beneath the sea, the Shadow Captain knelt beside the restored chain.

A voice spoke behind him.

"You let the boy choose."

The old captain turned.

In the darkness stood a figure with a crown of twelve extinguished stars.

"And now," the figure whispered, "the other eleven will fall together."

Above the Silver Sea, eleven stars suddenly burned red.

KOKI raised his eyes.

The sky looked like a battlefield waiting for its first soldier.

And somewhere beyond the horizon, an army of forgotten ships began to awaken.
