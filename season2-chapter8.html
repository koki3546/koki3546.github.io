THE KOKI CHRONICLES · SEASON TWO

The First Falling Star

The Twelve Stars — Chapter 08

For three nights, KOKI had dreamed of a door.

It stood at the bottom of an endless black ocean.

Twelve chains held it shut.

Behind it, something enormous was breathing.

And every night, a frightened child stood before the door.

The child looked exactly like him.

"Please," the child whispered.

"Don't let him remember my real name."

KOKI always tried to reach the child.

But before he could, the water turned red.

And a voice from behind the door spoke a single word.

A name.

A name KOKI could never remember when he woke.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Sea of Silver

On the fourth morning, the ocean changed.

The waves became strangely smooth, as though an invisible hand had pressed them flat.

A silver light spread beneath the surface.

Finn stood beside the wheel, holding his battered telescope.

"Captain, I've sailed these waters for half my life."

He lowered the telescope.

"I've never seen the sea shine from underneath."

Veyra approached with the ancient star map.

The twelve symbols on its surface were moving.

Eleven remained golden.

The First Star was turning blue.

"The Hollow King is doing something to the seal," she said.

Brann examined the horizon.

"How far to the crater?"

"Less than an hour," Veyra replied.

KOKI studied the map.

At its center, a new inscription had appeared.

The Place of the First Falling Star.

Below it were three words.

The Last Witness.

KOKI remembered the Shadow Captain's warning.

He had to find someone who remembered the world before the Thirteenth Light was divided.

Someone who knew what had happened forty years ago.

Perhaps someone who knew who he truly was.

Finn watched him.

"You're thinking about that name again."

KOKI nodded.

"What if the Hollow King knows something about me that I don't?"

Finn shrugged.

"Then we'll find out."

"And if the truth is something terrible?"

Finn placed the telescope in KOKI's hands.

"Then we'll face it together.

A captain doesn't become someone else just because he learns where he came from."

KOKI looked toward the horizon.

For a moment, he felt less afraid.

Then the sky began to darken.

✦ ✦ ✦

The First Star Falls

At noon, the wind stopped.

Every sail hung motionless.

Even the seabirds fell silent.

Above the distant crater, a blue star appeared.

It was far too bright for the daytime sky.

Veyra looked up.

"That isn't possible."

The star began to grow.

Brann narrowed his eyes.

"Is it getting closer?"

Veyra's face turned pale.

"It's falling."

A low vibration passed through the ship.

The water around them began rising in enormous circles.

Then the star screamed.

A pillar of blue fire tore through the clouds.

The sky split open.

KOKI saw something impossible inside the falling light.

A small child holding a lantern.

The child turned toward him.

It was the boy from his dreams.

The boy raised one hand.

And the star struck the sea.

A mountain of water exploded upward.

Blue flames raced across the ocean.

The shockwave tore through the ship's sails.

The mainmast cracked.

Finn was thrown against the wheel.

Brann grabbed a rope and wrapped it around the broken mast.

"Captain!"

"The ship won't survive another wave!"

KOKI seized the wheel.

"Turn her into the current!"

Finn struggled to his feet.

"That's toward the crater!"

"I know!"

KOKI pointed through the rising spray.

Beyond the shattered waves, a small fishing boat was being dragged toward the center.

Two figures clung to its broken railing.

Lyra.

And Milo.

They had sailed ahead at dawn to scout the approach to the crater.

Now their boat was trapped in the current.

KOKI tightened his grip.

"Finn, get us closer!"

Brann stared at him.

"Closer?"

"We'll be torn apart!"

KOKI looked at the little boat.

"Then we'll have to be faster than the sea."
✦ ✦ ✦

The Rescue

The ship surged forward.

Veyra fired a rope across the raging water.

It struck the fishing boat, but the current snapped it immediately.

"Again!" KOKI shouted.

Brann loaded a heavier grappling hook.

The second rope caught.

Lyra seized it and tied it around the railing.

Milo was crying.

"Captain KOKI!"

KOKI climbed onto the railing of his own ship.

Finn grabbed his coat.

"What are you doing?"

"Getting them out."

"That gap is too wide!"

KOKI smiled.

"Then don't look down."

He jumped.

For one breath, he flew above the boiling silver water.

Then he crashed onto the fishing boat.

The deck splintered beneath his boots.

Lyra grabbed his arm.

"We can't hold on!"

KOKI pushed Milo toward the rescue rope.

"Climb!"

The boy shook his head.

"I'm scared!"

KOKI knelt before him.

"So am I."

Milo stared at him.

"Captains get scared?"

"All the time."

"Then how do you do it?"

KOKI took the boy's hand.

"Being brave doesn't mean you're not afraid.

It means someone needs you more than you need to run away."

Milo nodded.

He grabbed the rope.

Lyra climbed behind him.

Then the fishing boat struck a hidden rock.

Its hull split open.

The rope snapped.

Lyra was thrown toward the larger ship.

Brann caught her.

But Milo fell backward.

His hands reached for the empty air.

"CAPTAIN!"

KOKI lunged forward.

His fingers brushed the boy's coat.

Then Milo vanished beneath the waves.

KOKI jumped after him.

✦ ✦ ✦

Into the Black Sea

The water swallowed every sound.

Above him, the surface glowed with blue fire.

Below him, the ocean opened into an endless darkness.

KOKI saw Milo sinking beside a broken beam.

The boy's coat was caught beneath it.

KOKI swam downward.

His lungs burned.

He pulled at the beam.

It would not move.

He tried again.

Nothing.

Milo's eyes were beginning to close.

KOKI remembered a small wooden ship the boy had made for him.

Its crooked sails.

Its uneven hull.

And the words Milo had spoken when he gave it to him.

"Every captain needs a reason to come home."

KOKI pressed both feet against the wreckage.

He pulled with all his strength.

The beam broke.

He caught Milo and kicked upward.

But the current changed.

Instead of rising, they began to sink faster.

The light above them grew smaller.

The sea grew colder.

Then KOKI saw something far below.

A gate.

An enormous stone gate buried in the ocean floor.

Twelve chains stretched across its surface.

One chain was broken.

And behind the gate, something opened its eyes.

✦ ✦ ✦

The King Beneath the World

A deep voice rolled through the water.

"Welcome home, Thirteenth Captain."

KOKI held Milo tightly.

The darkness beyond the gate began to take shape.

A crown of black fire.

Two enormous hands.

And a face that seemed to contain nothing at all.

The Hollow King.

"You have traveled far," the creature whispered.

"Searching for answers."

KOKI tried to swim upward.

Invisible chains wrapped around his legs.

"Let us go!"

The Hollow King laughed.

"I do not want the little sailor."

"I want what sleeps inside your memory."

The creature raised one hand.

A vision appeared between them.

A golden chamber.

Twelve burning stars.

A frightened child.

And an older elephant kneeling beside him.

The Shadow Captain.

The child held a lantern.

The older captain placed one hand against a glowing door.

Then someone spoke from the other side.

"Give me his name."

KOKI felt his heart begin to race.

The child turned.

His lips moved.

But no sound emerged.

The Hollow King leaned closer.

"Do you understand now?"

"Your name is the key."

"And the key was hidden inside you."

KOKI looked at Milo.

The boy was motionless.

He had no time for the creature's riddles.

"Take your answers," KOKI whispered.

"Just let him live."

The Hollow King smiled.

"Then remember."

Black fire rushed toward KOKI's chest.

Suddenly, a golden blade cut through the darkness.

The chains around his legs shattered.

An older captain stood between him and the gate.

The Shadow Captain.

"Don't listen to him!"

KOKI stared.

"You!"

The older captain raised his sword.

"Get the boy to the surface!"

KOKI shook his head.

"Not until you tell me what happened!"

The Shadow Captain looked toward the Hollow King.

"I tried to divide the Thirteenth Light."

"I thought I could separate its power from its memories."

"But I was wrong."

KOKI stepped closer.

"Then what am I?"

The old captain lowered his voice.

"Someone I was supposed to protect."

The Hollow King roared.

Black hands burst through the broken seal.

The Shadow Captain drove his sword into the ocean floor.

Golden light erupted from the remaining chains.

A powerful current began lifting KOKI and Milo.

KOKI reached toward him.

"Come with us!"

The older captain smiled.

"I spent forty years trying to change the past.

You still have a future worth protecting."

The golden current carried KOKI upward.

The Shadow Captain disappeared into the darkness.

And the Hollow King's voice followed him.

"Eleven stars remain."

"Eleven chances to remember your name."

✦ ✦ ✦

A Breath Worth Fighting For

KOKI burst through the surface.

"FINN!"

A rope struck the water beside him.

Brann and Finn pulled him toward the shattered ship.

Lyra seized Milo from his arms.

The boy was not breathing.

For one terrible moment, no one moved.

Then Lyra began trying to revive him.

Veyra knelt beside her.

Finn looked away.

Brann removed his hat.

KOKI stood trembling.

He had crossed the Forgotten Sea.

He had fought ghosts and kings.

He had survived storms that destroyed fleets.

But none of those battles had frightened him as much as this silence.

"Please," he whispered.

"Not him."

Lyra tried again.

A second passed.

Then another.

Suddenly, Milo coughed.

Water spilled from his mouth.

He gasped for air.

Lyra held him against her chest.

Finn laughed through tears.

Brann sat heavily against the broken mast.

KOKI sank to his knees.

Milo opened his eyes.

"Captain?"

KOKI leaned closer.

"Yes?"

"Did we win?"

KOKI looked at the ruined ship.

The broken sails.

The burning horizon.

The enormous crater still glowing beneath the sea.

Then he smiled.

"You're here."

"That's enough of a victory for me."

✦ ✦ ✦

The Stairway of the Dead Star

The sea began to grow quiet.

The blue flames faded.

At the center of the enormous crater, a white staircase rose from the water.

Each step was carved with a different constellation.

At the top stood a silver door.

An ancient figure waited before it.

She was an old sea turtle, her shell marked with moving stars.

Veyra bowed her head.

"The Last Witness."

The woman looked toward the damaged ship.

"You survived the First Star's fall."

Brann crossed his arms.

"Barely."

The Witness smiled.

"That is how most important stories begin."

KOKI stepped onto the staircase.

"I came for answers."

The Witness studied him.

"I know."

"Then tell me who I am."

She looked toward the sea.

"Forty years ago, the Shadow Captain came to this place."

"He carried the Thirteenth Light."

"He believed the Hollow King could be defeated by dividing that light."

KOKI remembered the vision in the mirror.

"The ancient rite."

The Witness nodded.

"But the rite did not create you."

"It opened a passage between possible histories."

"And through that passage, someone arrived."

KOKI's heart pounded.

"Me?"

"Yes."

"But from where?"

The Witness hesitated.

"From a history the Hollow King tried to erase."

KOKI stepped closer.

"Then my childhood..."

"Your memories are not simply lies."

"They are pieces of a life that was real, even if its history was stolen."

KOKI closed his eye.

He remembered his father.

His village.

The first day he sailed away.

For the first time, those memories did not feel like a trap.

They felt like something worth defending.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Thirteenth Captain

The Witness opened the silver door.

Inside was a chamber filled with floating lights.

Twelve lights formed a great circle.

At its center burned a thirteenth.

But the central flame had no color.

It changed constantly.

Gold.

Blue.

White.

Black.

KOKI approached it.

"What is that?"

"The Thirteenth Light."

"Is it mine?"

The Witness shook her head.

"It belongs to no one."

"Then why does the Hollow King want it?"

"Because the twelve stars were made into seals."

"Each holds a part of his prison."

"But the Thirteenth Light can join what was divided."

"It can restore the chains."

"Or break them all."

KOKI stared at the colorless flame.

"And my name?"

The Witness lowered her voice.

"Your forgotten name is tied to the first time that light was used."

"The Hollow King cannot speak it himself."

"He needs you to remember it willingly."

KOKI understood.

Every vision.

Every broken memory.

Every question that had led him here.

The Hollow King had been using his search for the truth.

The Witness touched the central flame.

A final memory appeared.

A frightened child stood beside the Shadow Captain.

The older captain knelt before him.

"Listen carefully," he said.

"You are not my second chance."

"You are not a weapon."

"You are not the Hollow King's key."

The child looked up.

"Then what am I?"

The Shadow Captain placed a hand on his shoulder.

"You are someone who deserves to live his own life."

The memory vanished.

KOKI stood silently.

For years, he had searched for a place to belong.

Now he realized someone had once fought to give him the freedom to choose one.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Price of the First Seal

The chamber suddenly shook.

A black crack spread across the ceiling.

The Hollow King's voice filled the air.

"Enough stories!"

The twelve lights began to flicker.

Outside, the crater erupted again.

A colossal hand rose from the ocean.

Its fingers were made of black water and broken stars.

The hand reached toward KOKI's ship.

Finn shouted from the deck.

"CAPTAIN!"

Brann raised a damaged cannon.

He fired.

The shot passed through the creature.

Veyra fired three glowing arrows.

They exploded against its wrist.

The hand barely moved.

Lyra shielded Milo behind the broken mast.

KOKI turned to the Witness.

"How do we stop it?"

"The First Seal can still be restored."

"How?"

The Witness looked toward the thirteenth flame.

"You must surrender the memory that the Hollow King is trying to awaken."

KOKI froze.

"My real name?"

"Yes."

"If you release it, you may never recover it again."

KOKI looked through the open door.

His friends were fighting a battle they could not win.

Milo stood beside Lyra.

The boy held his little wooden ship.

He raised it toward KOKI.

Even from far away, KOKI understood.

Come home.

The Witness waited.

"Your choice, Captain."

KOKI remembered the thousand worlds shown in the Mirror of Origins.

Worlds where he had never existed.

Worlds where his friends had died.

And the one world in which they were still standing together.

He placed his hand over the golden mark on his chest.

"I may never know who I was before.

But I know who needs me now."

The Witness nodded.

"Then make your choice."

KOKI reached into the flame.

A voice spoke inside his memory.

The frightened child.

"Are you going to forget me?"

KOKI closed his eye.

"No."

"I'll remember that you were afraid."

"And I'll remember that someone tried to protect you."

"But I won't let your name destroy the people I love."

The child smiled.

For the first time, he looked peaceful.

Then the memory became golden light.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Light of the Living

The Thirteenth Light burst from the chamber.

It crossed the staircase and swept across the Silver Sea.

Finn's telescope began to glow.

Veyra's star map opened in the wind.

Brann's broken sword shone like fire.

Milo's wooden ship filled with golden light.

The Witness smiled.

"The light is answering them."

KOKI raised his head.

He could feel his friends' determination.

Their fear.

Their hope.

Their refusal to abandon one another.

The golden light formed a great chain above the ocean.

The Hollow King roared.

"THAT LIGHT IS MINE!"

KOKI stepped forward.

"No."

He drew his sword.

"It belongs to no king."

The enormous hand struck toward the ship.

Finn turned the wheel.

Brann caught the falling mast.

Veyra released her final arrow.

KOKI leaped from the staircase.

For one brilliant moment, the golden chain carried him above the sea.

He raised his sword.

The Thirteenth Light raced along its blade.

Then he struck the center of the black hand.

A thunderous explosion shook the ocean.

The creature shattered.

Far beneath the waves, the broken First Chain began to move.

Its scattered links rose from the seabed.

One by one, they joined together.

The chain wrapped around the ancient gate.

The Hollow King screamed.

The gate closed.

And the First Star shone gold once more.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Promise of the Shadow Captain

Deep beneath the sea, the Shadow Captain knelt beside the restored chain.

His black coat was torn.

His sword had shattered.

But the gate was closed.

For the first time in forty years, he allowed himself to smile.

"You chose differently," he whispered.

A voice answered from the darkness.

"And yet the ending remains."

The Hollow King stood behind the gate.

His crown burned with black fire.

"The boy has forgotten the name."

"That door is closed to you," the Shadow Captain replied.

The Hollow King laughed.

"One door."

"Eleven stars remain."

The old captain looked upward.

Above the gate, eleven distant lights began turning red.

The Hollow King whispered.

"You taught him how to save one star."

"Now let us see whether he can save the other eleven at once."

✦ ✦ ✦

Eleven Stars of War

By sunset, the sea had grown calm.

KOKI's ship was badly damaged.

Its sails were torn.

Its mainmast was held together with ropes and iron.

But everyone was alive.

Milo sat beside the broken railing, holding his wooden ship.

KOKI joined him.

"How's the ship?"

Milo examined its cracked hull.

"Not very good."

KOKI laughed.

"Mine either."

Milo looked up.

"Can we fix them?"

KOKI studied the tiny vessel.

"Of course."

"How do you know?"

KOKI looked toward Finn, Brann, Veyra, and Lyra.

They were already repairing the ship.

"Because we don't have to do it alone."

Milo smiled.

He placed the wooden ship in KOKI's hands.

"Then you keep this until we get home."

KOKI nodded.

"I promise."

Finn approached with the telescope.

"Captain, you should see this."

KOKI raised it toward the sky.

The First Star was shining gold.

But the other eleven were changing.

One became red.

Then another.

Then another.

Within moments, eleven crimson stars burned above the horizon.

Veyra unfolded the ancient map.

Its symbols were moving together.

"That can't be right," she whispered.

Brann leaned closer.

"What is it?"

"The stars aren't falling one at a time anymore."

She looked at KOKI.

"They're gathering."

Far across the sea, an ancient bell began to ring.

Then another.

Then a thousand.

From beneath the black water, forgotten ships began to rise.

Broken warships.

Royal galleons.

Ghostly vessels whose flags had not flown for centuries.

They filled the distant horizon.

An entire fleet of the dead.

Finn lowered his telescope.

"Captain..."

"That's an army."

KOKI stood at the bow.

He looked at the crimson stars.

Then at his crew.

The Shadow Captain had spent forty years trying to prevent a terrible future.

The Hollow King had searched a thousand histories for a way to escape.

And now the war was coming.

KOKI tightened his grip on the wooden ship.

He no longer knew the name he had carried before his life began.

But he knew the name his friends called him.

Captain KOKI.

And that was enough.

He turned toward the crew.

"Raise whatever sails we have left.

If the whole world is about to fall...

then we're going to give it something to hold on to."

Far beneath the sea, the Hollow King opened his eyes.

The eleven red stars flared brighter.

And across the Forgotten Sea, the drums of war began to sound.
