Liquidity Management Policy

KOKI's first liquidity pool launched on Base Mainnet on October 23, 2026. The information below describes the initial deployment and management policy.

LIVE · DEPLOYED OCTOBER 23, 2026

Initial Liquidity Deployment

The initial Uniswap v2 WETH / KOKI pool was created on Base with 0.01 ETH and 1,000,000,000 KOKI. This represents 0.1% of the total KOKI supply. The initial pool value was approximately US$48, based on the starting exchange ratio. Pool balances and value can change with trading.

Liquidity Ownership

The initial liquidity was provided by the founder-operated wallet. At launch, this wallet held 100% of the pool's LP position. This percentage may change as other liquidity providers participate. The founder retains control of the LP tokens.

Liquidity Lock Status

Liquidity is not locked. No third-party liquidity locker or time-locked contract has been implemented. The founder-controlled wallet can withdraw its liquidity subject to the pool's available balances and contract rules.

Founder Token Holdings

Following the initial liquidity deployment, approximately 999 billion KOKI remained in the founder-operated wallet. The proposed allocation categories have not been implemented through separate dedicated wallets or vesting contracts. Wallet balances may change over time.

Management & Disclosure Principles

The project intends to disclose material liquidity additions, withdrawals, ownership changes, and management decisions through official channels. These disclosure intentions do not impose on-chain restrictions on the founder wallet.

Liquidity Risks

The initial pool is extremely small. Even modest trades can cause substantial price movements and slippage. Trading bots, arbitrage, and concentrated token ownership can increase volatility. Liquidity providers face impermanent loss and smart contract risks. There is no guarantee that holders can sell KOKI at an expected price or at all.
