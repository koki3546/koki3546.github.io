Security Classification & Transparency

On November 26, 2026, Blockaid confirmed through an independent on-chain check that the existing KOKI LP position had been successfully locked through PinkLock V2.

Blockaid's existing token warning remains in place. Confirmation of the liquidity lock does not constitute security approval, an independent contract audit, or an endorsement of KOKI.

Blockaid identified the following outstanding concerns:

  • The liquidity pool remains very small, with approximately 0.01 WETH at the time of review.
  • Approximately 99.9% of the total KOKI supply remains concentrated in the deployer wallet.
  • Only three token holders were identified at the time of review.
  • Meaningful organic trading activity has not yet been demonstrated.

Further review requires independently verifiable liquidity in the thousands of US dollars, a more distributed holder structure, transparent project wallet and vesting information, genuine trading activity across 24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day periods, and proof that any newly created LP tokens are also locked or burned.

The project acknowledges these concerns. No claim is made that the existing warning will be removed. Users should assess all risks independently before interacting with the token.

Liquidity Management Policy

KOKI's first liquidity pool launched on Base Mainnet on October 23, 2026. The founder-held LP position was subsequently locked on November 26, 2026. The information below describes the initial deployment and management policy.

LIVE · DEPLOYED OCTOBER 23, 2026

Initial Liquidity Deployment

The initial Uniswap v2 WETH / KOKI pool was created on Base with 0.01 ETH and 1,000,000,000 KOKI. This represents 0.1% of the total KOKI supply. The initial pool value was approximately US$48, based on the starting exchange ratio. Pool balances and value can change with trading.

The initial liquidity was provided by the founder-operated wallet. On November 26, 2026, all LP tokens held by this wallet were transferred into PinkLock V2. The founder no longer directly held LP tokens at the time of verification. The lock owner retains the right to transfer lock ownership and withdraw the locked LP tokens after the unlock date.

LOCKED UNTIL NOVEMBER 26, 2027

Liquidity Lock Status

The existing founder-held LP position is locked through PinkLock V2. The locked LP tokens cannot be withdrawn before November 26, 2027. The lock duration cannot be shortened and the locked amount cannot be reduced. This lock does not automatically cover future LP tokens.

Founder Token Holdings

As of November 26, 2026, approximately 999 billion KOKI remained in the founder-operated wallet. These directly held KOKI tokens are not covered by the LP lock. The proposed allocation categories have not been implemented through separate dedicated wallets or vesting contracts. Wallet balances may change over time.

Future Liquidity Additions

Additional liquidity may be provided in the future, subject to available resources and project development. Any newly created LP tokens will require separate locking or burning to receive equivalent withdrawal restrictions. No additional liquidity amount, deployment date, or future lock period is guaranteed.

Management & Disclosure Principles

The project intends to disclose material liquidity additions, withdrawals, ownership changes, and management decisions through official channels. These disclosure intentions do not impose on-chain restrictions on the founder wallet. The existing PinkLock contract restricts withdrawal of the specified LP tokens only, according to its contract terms.

Liquidity Risks

The initial pool is extremely small. Even modest trades can cause substantial price movements and slippage. Trading bots, arbitrage, and concentrated token ownership can increase volatility. Liquidity providers face impermanent loss and smart contract risks. Locking LP tokens does not prevent token sales, market losses, or price declines. There is no guarantee that holders can sell KOKI at an expected price or at all.
