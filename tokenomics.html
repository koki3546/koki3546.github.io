Liquidity Management Policy

KOKI aims to communicate its liquidity plans and management practices transparently. The information below describes the current proposal and its limitations.

PLANNED · NOT DEPLOYED

Initial Liquidity Proposal

The project is considering an initial Uniswap v2 ETH / KOKI pool on Base with approximately 0.01 ETH and 1,000,000,000 KOKI. These amounts are provisional and have not been deposited.

Liquidity Ownership

If launched as currently proposed, the initial liquidity position would be controlled by the founder-operated project wallet. The founder would retain the ability to manage or withdraw the liquidity position.

Liquidity Lock Status

No liquidity lock has been implemented. No third-party locker or time-locked liquidity contract is currently in use. The proposed liquidity should not be described as locked or permanently secured.

Founder Token Holdings

The initial supply remains under founder control as of this disclosure. The proposed token allocation has not been separated into dedicated wallets. No vesting or token lock is currently active.

Management & Disclosure Principles

The project intends to publish material liquidity additions, withdrawals, ownership changes, and changes to its management approach through official channels. Such announcements are a transparency objective, not an on-chain restriction: the wallet's technical permissions remain unchanged unless a separate enforceable mechanism is implemented.

Liquidity Risks

The proposed pool would be extremely small. Even modest trades could cause substantial price movements and slippage. Trading bots, arbitrage, and concentrated ownership may increase volatility. Liquidity providers can also experience losses relative to holding the assets. There is no guarantee that KOKI holders will be able to sell their tokens at an expected price, or sell them at all.

Liquidity & Trading

Trading Status: Not Officially Launched

KOKI is evaluating a potential Uniswap v2 ETH / KOKI liquidity pool on Base. The current proposal is described in the Liquidity Management Policy above.

An official liquidity pool has not yet been launched by the project. No official trading venue is being announced on this page.

Third parties may independently create pools or trading pairs. Always verify the official contract address, network, and on-chain liquidity before interacting.

Official updates will be published through the KOKI website and KOKI's X account .
