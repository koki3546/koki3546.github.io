THE KOKI CHRONICLES · CHAPTER 11

The Last Bell

The greatest battles are not fought for power, but for the freedom to choose.

The Drowned King opened his eyes.

For a thousand years, the throne room had known only silence.

Now the ancient stones trembled beneath the weight of his voice.

"Would you surrender your freedom to save everyone you love?"

KOKI stood before the throne, the Last Compass burning in his hands.

He looked at the enormous king.

"I would risk my life for them."

The King smiled.

"Then we are not so different."

KOKI shook his head.

"There's one difference."

The golden compass brightened.

"I would never take their freedom away."

The King's smile disappeared.

✦ ✦ ✦

A wave of darkness exploded from the throne.

KOKI was thrown across the floor.

Morcant raised the Shadow Compass, trying to command the awakening ruler.

"I freed you! You owe me your power!"

The Drowned King turned toward him.

"You freed nothing."

The Shadow Compass shattered in Morcant's hand.

Black chains burst from the throne and wrapped around his armor.

Morcant screamed.

The King leaned forward.

"You came to me seeking the dead."

His voice grew cold.

"I shall give you an eternity among them."

Morcant fell to his knees.

For the first time, KOKI saw genuine fear in the villain's eyes.

✦ ✦ ✦

Outside the palace, Finn and Veyra fought their way through the dark guardians.

Thalor smashed the stone soldiers with his enormous fins.

But every shattered statue rose again.

Above the kingdom, the third chain began to glow.

Finn raised the Last Compass's reflected light from the journal's silver pages.

"We have to reach KOKI!"

Veyra pointed toward the palace's highest tower.

"The Bell is there!"

Thalor looked up.

A colossal bronze bell hung between the towers, wrapped in the final chain.

Its surface was covered in ancient writing.

One phrase glowed gold.

"ONLY THE FREE MAY SILENCE THE KING."

Finn stared at the words.

Then he remembered Rowan's warning.

The seals were chains.

The Bell was a prison.

But the prison had been built by choice.

Not force.

✦ ✦ ✦

Inside the throne room, KOKI struggled to stand.

The Drowned King raised his hand.

A hundred ghostly soldiers appeared around him.

Their eyes were empty.

Their swords pointed toward the little elephant.

KOKI lifted the Last Compass.

Golden light spread across the floor.

The soldiers hesitated.

For a moment, their eyes flickered blue.

The King roared.

"OBEY!"

The soldiers advanced.

KOKI lowered the compass.

He looked at the nearest warrior.

"You don't have to."

The warrior stopped.

KOKI stepped closer.

"I won't command you."

The golden light grew stronger.

"But if you want your freedom back, stand with me."

One sword fell.

Then another.

The ghostly soldiers began to kneel.

Not before the King.

Before their own choices.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Drowned King rose from his throne.

His enormous crown struck the ceiling.

The palace shook.

"You would steal my kingdom from me?"

KOKI stood his ground.

"It was never yours to own."

The King raised his trident.

A black wave crashed toward KOKI.

He had nowhere to run.

Suddenly, a crimson figure stepped between them.

Morcant.

The villain raised his shattered compass.

The black wave struck his armor.

He screamed, but did not move.

KOKI stared at him.

"Why?"

Morcant looked back.

His voice was broken.

"Because my daughter would have hated what I became."

The armor cracked.

Red light poured from his chest.

"Don't let him take another child."

Morcant pushed KOKI toward the palace doors.

Then the darkness swallowed him.

✦ ✦ ✦

KOKI crashed into the courtyard.

Finn caught him.

"KOKI!"

Veyra rushed forward.

Behind them, the palace doors exploded outward.

The Drowned King stepped into the courtyard.

His shadow covered the entire kingdom.

Thalor roared and charged.

The guardian struck the King's armor.

But the King seized Thalor by the throat.

Golden scales cracked beneath his enormous fingers.

Finn cried out.

"Thalor!"

The King lifted the guardian from the ground.

"Even guardians must obey."

Thalor struggled.

Then his golden eyes began to turn black.

KOKI looked up at the Last Bell.

The final chain was nearly broken.

He understood.

If the Bell rang, every soul in the sea would belong to the King.

✦ ✦ ✦

KOKI turned to Finn.

"Can you reach the Bell's tower?"

Finn looked at the collapsing bridge.

"Maybe."

Veyra spread her wings.

"I'll take him."

KOKI nodded.

"Find a way to silence it."

Finn grabbed KOKI's hand.

"And you?"

KOKI looked toward the towering King.

"I'll keep him busy."

Finn shook his head.

"Not alone."

A familiar voice echoed from the water.

"Then he won't be."

Captain Brann's ship burst through the surface above the shattered palace.

Behind him came the ghost fleet.

Hundreds of blue lanterns illuminated the darkness.

Brann raised his telescope.

"All ships! Protect your captain!"

Cannon fire lit the kingdom.

The final battle had begun.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Drowned King released Thalor and turned toward the fleet.

Black lightning tore through the water.

Ships shattered.

Ghost sailors were thrown into the deep.

But the fleet did not retreat.

KOKI climbed onto a broken statue.

He raised the Last Compass.

"All captains! Hold the line!"

Brann's ship fired another volley.

The ghost fleet surrounded the King.

Thalor rose again, wounded but alive.

He struck the King's enormous trident.

The weapon cracked.

The King roared.

Above them, Veyra carried Finn toward the Bell.

They landed on the highest tower.

Finn opened Rowan's journal.

The final page began to glow.

A sentence appeared.

"THE BELL CANNOT BE SILENCED BY THE ONE WHO COMMANDS."

Finn turned the page.

"IT MUST BE SILENCED BY THE ONE WHO LETS GO."

Veyra stared at the words.

"What does that mean?"

Finn looked toward the battlefield below.

Then toward KOKI.

His face went pale.

"I think..."

His voice trembled.

"I think the captain has to give up the Last Compass."

✦ ✦ ✦

Below, KOKI felt the compass grow unbearably hot.

A golden vision filled his mind.

He saw the Bell.

He saw the final chain.

And he saw a small elephant standing alone before an endless sea.

A voice whispered.

"The compass can guide every soul to freedom."

KOKI reached toward the vision.

"Then show me how."

The voice answered.

"Release it."

KOKI looked down at the golden instrument.

It had guided him through every danger.

It had saved Finn.

It had brought the ghost fleet together.

Without it, he would have no way to find the path ahead.

The Drowned King turned toward him.

His enormous hand reached across the battlefield.

KOKI closed his eyes.

He remembered the first day he sailed away from home.

He had wanted a compass that would show him where to go.

But now he understood.

A captain was not someone who always knew the way.

A captain was someone who kept sailing even when the path was uncertain.

KOKI opened his eyes.

"I don't need the compass to know what matters."

He raised it toward the Bell.

The golden instrument began to dissolve into a thousand stars.

Light swept across the kingdom.

The ghost sailors looked up.

The dark guardians stopped moving.

Even the Drowned King froze.

Above the palace, the Last Bell began to ring.

Once.

Twice.

And then...

Silence.

✦ ✦ ✦

The golden light vanished.

The Last Compass was gone.

KOKI fell to his knees.

Across the kingdom, the ghost fleet began to glow.

Their chains broke.

Their blue flames rose into the water like stars.

The sailors were finally free.

But the Drowned King was still standing.

His crown cracked down the center.

He looked at KOKI with an expression no one had seen in a thousand years.

Fear.

"What have you done?"

KOKI tried to stand.

His legs failed.

Finn and Veyra descended from the tower.

They rushed toward him.

But before they could reach their captain, the ground opened.

A vast black abyss appeared beneath the throne.

The Drowned King began to fall.

He reached out and seized KOKI.

Finn screamed.

"KOKI!"

The little elephant looked up at his friends.

For one brief moment, he smiled.

"Keep sailing."

Then the darkness swallowed them both.
