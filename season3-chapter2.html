The Adventures of KOKI · Season III
Beyond the Edge of Time
CHAPTER 02

The Island of Two Suns

What if the price of a perfect tomorrow
was the existence of someone you loved?

Milo's heartbeat was disappearing.

KOKI pressed his hand against the boy's chest.

One heartbeat.

Then silence.

Another heartbeat.

Then a silence that lasted far too long.

"Lyra!"

Lyra rushed toward them.

Yan removed the mechanical device from her wrist and fastened it around Milo's arm.

Its hands began spinning wildly.

Milo's body slowly became solid again.

KOKI looked at Yan.

"Is he safe?"

"For now."

"For how long?"

Yan looked toward the island.

"Until the next hour disappears."

✦ ✦ ✦

I. The Harbor of Impossible Things

The ship passed between two enormous golden statues.

One depicted an old man holding a newborn child.

The other depicted a child holding the hand of an old man.

Beyond them lay the harbor of Aevora.

Hundreds of ships floated above the water without touching it.

Fish swam through the air.

Broken buildings repaired themselves as the crew watched.

A shattered window gathered its pieces from the street and became whole again.

Brann stared.

"I could use something like that for our ship."

Nobody laughed.

KOKI carried Milo down the gangplank.

The boy was awake, but his hands were cold.

Nox followed closely behind.

Above the city, two suns moved across the sky.

One rose.

The other descended.

Between them stood a tower taller than any mountain KOKI had ever seen.

Its enormous clock had no hands.

"What is that?" Lyra asked.

Yan looked away.

"The Meridian Tower."

"Does it control time?"

Yan shook her head.

"It used to."

✦ ✦ ✦

II. The Woman Who Waited

The streets of Aevora were filled with beautiful gardens, golden bridges, and fountains that flowed backward.

Yet something was terribly wrong.

There were no children playing.

No merchants calling to customers.

No sailors singing.

Everyone carried a clock.

Everyone watched the sky.

An elderly woman stood beside a fountain, holding two cups of tea.

"Have you seen my husband?" she asked KOKI.

"I'm afraid not."

"He should be here soon."

She smiled.

"We always meet at noon."

Yan stopped walking.

The woman turned toward her.

"Have we met before?"

Yan's eyes filled with sadness.

"Yes."

"How wonderful."

The woman looked toward the fountain.

"Have you seen my husband?"

KOKI stared at her.

Yan gently took his arm.

"Come."

As they walked away, KOKI heard the woman asking another stranger the same question.

"What happened to her?" he whispered.

"Her husband died twelve years ago. She paid the Meridian Council to remove the hour of his death."

"Did it bring him back?"

"No."

Yan lowered her head.

"It trapped her inside the hour before she learned he was gone."

KOKI looked back.

The woman was still smiling.

Still holding two cups of tea.

Still waiting.

✦ ✦ ✦

III. The Price of Yesterday

Yan led them into an abandoned workshop beneath the city.

Thousands of clocks covered its walls.

Some moved forward.

Some moved backward.

Others had stopped completely.

Lyra examined a collection of ancient drawings.

"These machines don't destroy time."

Yan nodded.

"They transfer it."

"Transfer it where?"

Yan opened a heavy iron door.

Behind it stood an enormous glass chamber.

Inside floated hundreds of small spheres of light.

Each contained a moving image.

A wedding.

A battlefield.

A burning house.

A child taking its first steps.

"Every hour removed from Aevora was stored beneath the city," Yan explained.

Finn approached the glass.

"Why would anyone keep their worst memories?"

"Because the hours contain more than suffering. They contain every choice made within them."

KOKI watched a sphere containing a terrible storm.

Inside it, a sailor pulled another sailor from the water.

Yan pointed toward them.

"If you remove the storm, you also remove the rescue."

KOKI understood.

The machine could not distinguish between tragedy and courage.

Between pain and love.

Between losing someone and saving someone.

Milo looked at the floating lights.

"Is one of my hours in there?"

Yan did not answer.

Then the workshop door exploded.

✦ ✦ ✦

IV. The Meridian Guard

Soldiers wearing golden armor poured into the workshop.

Their helmets contained rotating rings of light.

Their weapons resembled long mechanical spears.

A tall figure entered behind them.

He wore a white coat embroidered with golden clocks.

"Yan."

She stepped backward.

"Director Vaelor."

The man smiled.

"You have caused considerable damage to our future."

KOKI drew his sword.

"Stay away from her."

Vaelor studied the elephant captain.

"Ah. The famous pirate."

"You know me?"

"Of course."

Vaelor looked toward Milo.

"Although you arrived earlier than expected."

KOKI moved between them.

"What do you want with him?"

Vaelor raised his hand.

The soldiers surrounded the crew.

"Nothing that belongs to you."

Milo's wrist device began ringing.

Vaelor smiled.

"The boy is living on borrowed time."

Nox drew his silver blade.

"Then he'll keep borrowing it."

The soldiers attacked.

Veyra met the first spear with her sword.

Brann overturned a heavy workbench.

Finn dragged Lyra behind the glass chamber as golden bolts tore through the workshop.

KOKI charged toward Vaelor.

His sword flashed.

But before the blade reached its target, the world changed.

KOKI found himself standing exactly where he had been moments earlier.

Vaelor had not moved.

"Again?" Vaelor asked.

KOKI charged a second time.

Again, he returned to his original position.

Nox stared.

"He's reversing our movements!"

Vaelor adjusted a golden dial on his wrist.

"Every battle is easy when your opponent is never permitted to reach the end of it."

KOKI looked around the workshop.

Then he noticed something.

Every time Vaelor reversed his attack, one of the clocks on the wall shattered.

KOKI smiled.

"Then let's see how many times you can do that."

✦ ✦ ✦

V. The Captain Who Refused to Stop

KOKI charged.

The world reversed.

A clock shattered.

He charged again.

Another clock shattered.

Nox understood.

He joined the attack.

Veyra followed.

Then Brann.

Vaelor's smile disappeared.

"STOP!"

The workshop filled with the sound of breaking glass.

Vaelor could reverse one attack.

Perhaps two.

But he could not reverse every choice made by an entire crew forever.

Lyra grabbed a metal lever beside the glass chamber.

"Everyone down!"

She pulled it.

A wave of blue light swept through the workshop.

Vaelor's device went dark.

KOKI's sword struck the director's golden spear, breaking it in half.

For the first time, Vaelor looked afraid.

Finn opened an emergency passage.

"Captain! This way!"

KOKI lifted Milo into his arms.

The crew escaped through the tunnel.

Behind them, Vaelor shouted an order.

"SEAL THE CITY!"

Golden bells began ringing throughout Aevora.

✦ ✦ ✦

VI. The Boy Who Was Never Born

The tunnel ended beneath a deserted observatory.

Yan locked the entrance.

Everyone was breathing heavily.

KOKI placed Milo on a wooden bench.

The boy's face had become pale.

His wrist device was slowing down.

"Yan."

KOKI's voice was quiet.

"No more secrets."

Yan looked at Milo.

Then she looked at the captain.

"The Meridian Council has been searching through possible futures."

"Why?"

"To find the one in which Aevora survives."

Lyra frowned.

"And what does that have to do with Milo?"

Yan began to cry.

"In every future where you reach the Meridian Tower, the Council loses control of time."

Nox stepped forward.

"So they're trying to stop us."

Yan nodded.

"But they discovered something else."

She looked toward the little boy.

"Milo is the reason you reach the tower."

KOKI felt a terrible coldness in his chest.

"What have they done?"

Yan struggled to speak.

"They are trying to remove the events that led to his birth."

Nobody moved.

Milo looked at KOKI.

"Captain..."

KOKI knelt before him.

"If I disappear, will you forget me?"

KOKI took the boy's hands.

"No."

"But what if I was never here?"

KOKI pulled him close.

"Then I'll sail through every yesterday until I find you."

Outside, the two suns reached opposite ends of the sky.

The city bells stopped.

Milo's wooden ship fell from his pocket.

One of its seven tiny figures vanished.

And beneath the Meridian Tower, a golden clock began counting down.

Twenty-three hours remained.
