THE KOKI CHRONICLES · SEASON TWO

The War of Twelve Stars

The Twelve Stars — Chapter 09

The first cannon fired before sunrise.

It came from a ship that had been dead for three hundred years.

Its hull was black with ancient salt.

Its sails hung like torn funeral cloth.

And beneath its bow, a thousand drowned voices whispered the same command.

"Remember us."

The cannonball struck the sea beside KOKI's ship.

A tower of water rose above the deck.

Finn was thrown against the railing.

Brann caught him before he fell overboard.

"That was a warning shot!" Brann shouted.

Finn looked at the horizon.

Hundreds of black sails were emerging from the morning mist.

"No," he whispered.

"That was the first shot of a war."

✦ ✦ ✦

The Armada of Forgotten Names

KOKI stood at the bow, his red scarf snapping in the wind.

The ship beneath him was barely seaworthy.

Its mainmast had been broken during the fall of the First Star.

Half its sails were patched together.

The hull leaked through a dozen cracks.

Yet the enemy fleet continued advancing.

Veyra unfolded the ancient star map.

Eleven crimson symbols burned across its surface.

"The Hollow King has awakened the fleet of the Forgotten Ages."

Brann stared at her.

"How many ships?"

Veyra hesitated.

"The map cannot count them."

Finn raised his telescope.

"Then I'll try."

He scanned the horizon.

One line of warships.

Then another.

Then another.

He lowered the telescope.

"I stopped at two hundred."

Lyra climbed from the lower deck.

"Milo is safe below. But the star compass is behaving strangely."

KOKI turned.

"What do you mean?"

"Every needle is pointing toward us."

Veyra examined the map.

Her expression changed.

"They're not sailing toward the crater."

"They're following the Thirteenth Light."

Finn looked at KOKI.

"Which means..."

KOKI nodded.

"They're following me."

For a moment, no one spoke.

Then Brann picked up his battered axe.

"Well, that's inconvenient."

KOKI almost smiled.

Almost.

Another cannon fired.

This time, the shot struck the ship's stern.

Wood exploded.

A sailor cried out.

KOKI drew his sword.

"Finn, bring us into the western current!"

"Captain, that's toward the Broken Reefs!"

"Exactly."

KOKI looked at the enormous enemy ships.

"They have more cannons."

"They have more soldiers."

"But they don't know these waters."

"An army can be bigger than us.

That doesn't mean it can think faster."
✦ ✦ ✦

The Broken Reefs

The ship turned west.

The Forgotten Armada followed immediately.

Three enormous galleons broke away from the fleet.

Their black sails carried a crimson symbol:

An eye surrounded by twelve broken stars.

Finn recognized the mark.

"The Crownless Navy."

Brann glanced at him.

"You know them?"

"My grandfather used to tell stories."

"They were a royal fleet that disappeared during the War of Ash."

"Every ship vanished in a single night."

Veyra watched the approaching vessels.

"They didn't vanish."

"They were erased."

A cannonball screamed over the deck.

Finn pulled the wheel.

The ship slipped between two jagged rocks.

The first enemy galleon attempted to follow.

Its enormous hull struck the reef.

A terrible cracking sound echoed across the water.

The ship broke open.

For one hopeful moment, Brann cheered.

Then the wreckage began to rise.

Broken planks floated backward.

Shattered cannons returned to their places.

The torn sails stitched themselves together with black fire.

Within moments, the galleon was whole again.

Brann stopped cheering.

"That's cheating."

Veyra's face grew pale.

"They're not ordinary ghosts."

"The stars are restoring them."

KOKI looked upward.

The eleven crimson stars pulsed together.

Every pulse healed the enemy fleet.

Every pulse brought more ships from beneath the sea.

He understood.

They could sink a hundred warships.

It would change nothing.

They needed to break the connection between the fleet and the stars.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Captain of the Dead

A horn sounded from the center of the enemy armada.

The surrounding ships parted slowly.

Through the opening came a vessel larger than any KOKI had ever seen.

Its hull was built from black stone.

Its masts resembled the bones of ancient giants.

Eleven red lanterns hung above its deck.

At its prow stood a tall figure in silver armor.

A crown of broken anchors rested upon his head.

Veyra whispered his name.

"Admiral Morcant."

Finn looked at her.

"Who?"

"The last commander of the Crownless Navy."

"He led eleven kingdoms against the Hollow King."

Brann frowned.

"Then why is he fighting for him?"

Veyra swallowed.

"Because his name was taken from history."

Morcant raised his silver sword.

His voice rolled across the ocean.

"CAPTAIN KOKI!"

The enemy fleet fell silent.

"THE KING HAS SENT US TO BRING YOU HOME."

KOKI stepped onto the railing.

"I already have a home!"

Morcant laughed.

"THEN WATCH IT BURN."

He lowered his sword.

Every cannon in the fleet fired.

✦ ✦ ✦

Fire Across the Sea

The horizon disappeared behind smoke.

Hundreds of cannonballs tore through the sky.

Finn turned the wheel with all his strength.

"EVERYONE DOWN!"

The first volley struck the reefs.

Stone shattered.

The second volley struck the water.

Waves crashed over the deck.

The third volley came straight toward the ship.

Brann threw himself against the broken mast.

He pushed it sideways, using its heavy spar to deflect the first shot.

The cannonball exploded against the wood.

Brann fell backward.

"BRANN!" KOKI shouted.

The warrior struggled to his feet.

"Still alive!"

He examined his axe.

"Can't say the same for my weapon."

Lyra appeared beside Veyra.

"The star map is reacting to the cannon fire!"

Golden lines spread across the parchment.

Eleven points formed a circle.

At its center was a black symbol.

Veyra stared.

"Of course."

KOKI turned toward her.

"What?"

"The stars aren't controlling the ships one by one."

"They're feeding a single command."

She pointed toward Morcant's vessel.

"The eleven lanterns on his flagship."

"Destroy the lanterns, and the fleet may lose its power."

Finn looked at the enormous ship.

"May?"

Veyra nodded.

"It's the best theory I have."

KOKI studied the enemy formation.

Morcant's flagship was protected by four lines of warships.

There was no direct path.

Not for a ship.

But perhaps for something smaller.

He looked toward Lyra.

"Can your star map find us a path through the wreckage?"

Lyra nodded slowly.

"Not for the whole ship."

"That's all right."

KOKI glanced at the small rescue boat hanging beside the deck.

"We won't take the whole ship."

✦ ✦ ✦

The Plan No One Liked

Brann stared at the little boat.

"Absolutely not."

Finn crossed his arms.

"That's the worst plan you've ever had."

KOKI looked surprised.

"Worse than the ghost ship?"

"Much worse."

"Worse than the Drowned Kingdom?"

Finn pointed toward the enemy fleet.

"THERE ARE TWO HUNDRED WARSHIPS OUT THERE!"

KOKI considered this.

"Then perhaps we should hurry."

Brann groaned.

Veyra interrupted.

"There is another problem."

"The eleven lanterns are protected by a memory seal."

"Anyone who approaches will be forced to relive their greatest regret."

KOKI remembered the Mirror of Origins.

The frightened child.

The forgotten name.

He had surrendered that memory to restore the First Seal.

But there were other regrets.

Plenty of them.

He looked at his crew.

"Then we don't send one person."

"We send a team."

Finn sighed.

"Of course we do."

KOKI divided the work.

Finn would keep the damaged ship moving and draw the enemy fire.

Brann would lead the boarding attack.

Veyra would identify the lanterns' weakness.

Lyra would guide their little boat through the wreckage.

KOKI would face Morcant.

Then Milo appeared from below deck.

"And what about me?"

Everyone turned.

KOKI knelt beside the boy.

"You have the most important job."

Milo straightened.

"What is it?"

"Stay with Finn."

"Help him keep our ship alive."

Milo looked disappointed.

"That's not very heroic."

KOKI shook his head.

"Heroes aren't always the ones who charge into danger.

Sometimes they're the ones who make sure everyone has somewhere to return to."

Milo looked toward the damaged ship.

Then he nodded.

"I'll keep her safe."

✦ ✦ ✦

The Passage Through Fire

The rescue boat entered the smoke.

Lyra stood at its bow, holding the star map.

Golden lines appeared and vanished across the parchment.

"Left!"

Brann pulled the oar.

A cannonball struck the water where they had been.

"Right!"

The little boat slipped between two burning wrecks.

Above them, the enormous hull of a ghost galleon passed through the fog.

KOKI held his sword beneath his coat.

Its golden mark was beginning to glow.

Veyra noticed.

"The Thirteenth Light is reacting."

"To what?"

"Something aboard Morcant's ship."

A terrible cry echoed through the smoke.

From the water, skeletal hands reached toward the boat.

Brann swung his broken axe.

The first hand shattered.

Another seized the oar.

KOKI cut it free.

Lyra shouted.

"The current is changing!"

The water rose into a spinning wall.

Their little boat was pulled sideways.

Directly toward a burning warship.

Brann grabbed the mast of the rescue boat.

He planted his feet.

"EVERYONE HOLD ON!"

The two vessels collided.

The rescue boat split apart.

KOKI caught the broken railing.

Veyra grabbed Lyra.

Brann threw a rope around the warship's mast.

They climbed onto the enemy deck.

Dozens of drowned sailors turned toward them.

Their empty eyes glowed red.

Brann raised his axe.

"Well."

"At least we made it onto a ship."

KOKI drew his sword.

"Not the right one."

Brann smiled.

"One problem at a time."

✦ ✦ ✦

The Soldiers Who Could Not Die

The first drowned sailor charged at KOKI.

He blocked the attack and struck the creature's sword.

The blade shattered.

The sailor fell.

Then stood again.

Brann knocked three soldiers over the railing.

Moments later, they climbed back aboard.

"This is getting ridiculous!" he shouted.

Veyra studied the red light inside their eyes.

"They're not alive."

"They're memories wearing bodies."

KOKI turned toward her.

"Can we free them?"

Veyra hesitated.

"Possibly."

She raised the star map.

Golden symbols floated into the air.

"Who were you?" she called.

The sailors stopped.

For one moment, their red eyes flickered.

A young sailor stepped forward.

His voice was faint.

"I..."

He looked down at his hands.

"I had a daughter."

The others turned toward him.

"Her name..."

He struggled to remember.

The crimson light around him began to crack.

Then a bell rang from Morcant's flagship.

The sailor screamed.

His eyes turned red again.

He attacked.

KOKI blocked the blow.

He could have struck the sailor down.

Instead, he pushed him away.

Veyra understood.

"The lanterns aren't just controlling the fleet."

"They're stealing the sailors' memories."

Brann looked toward the distant flagship.

"Then we break those lanterns."

KOKI nodded.

"And give these people their names back."

✦ ✦ ✦

The Little Ship That Stayed Behind

Far behind them, Finn struggled to keep the damaged ship afloat.

The western current was growing stronger.

Water poured through the lower hull.

Milo carried buckets between the flooded hold and the upper deck.

Again.

And again.

His arms ached.

His legs trembled.

But he refused to stop.

Finn shouted from the wheel.

"Milo! Get below!"

"The mast is going to come down!"

Milo looked up.

The ropes holding the broken mast were beginning to snap.

He remembered the wooden ship he had made for KOKI.

The little vessel with its crooked sails.

He remembered how carefully he had tied the knots.

Then he saw the spare rope beneath the railing.

"Finn!"

"Not now!"

"I know how to fix it!"

Finn glanced down.

Milo pointed toward the damaged rigging.

"If we tie the rope around the lower beam, the mast won't fall toward the deck!"

Finn stared.

Then he smiled.

"You're right."

He threw Milo the end of the rope.

"Show me that knot."

Together, they secured the mast.

The ship groaned.

The ropes tightened.

And the mast held.

Milo looked up, breathing hard.

"Did I do it?"

Finn ruffled his hair.

"You just saved our way home."

Milo grinned.

Then the sea behind them exploded.

A black warship rose from the depths.

Its cannons turned toward their stern.

Finn's smile vanished.

"Not finished yet."

✦ ✦ ✦

The Eleven Lanterns

KOKI and his companions reached Morcant's flagship as the crimson moon rose above the sea.

The ship was so enormous that its deck resembled a stone fortress.

Eleven red lanterns hung from iron towers.

Each lantern contained a small burning star.

At the center stood Admiral Morcant.

He had been waiting.

"Thirteenth Captain."

KOKI stepped forward.

"Let these sailors go."

Morcant tilted his head.

"Let them go?"

"They have no names."

"No homes."

"No histories."

"The Hollow King gave them purpose."

KOKI shook his head.

"He gave them chains."

Morcant's eyes burned brighter.

"Names are chains too."

"Names bind us to people we lose."

"To promises we break."

"To failures we cannot undo."

KOKI remembered the frightened child from his dreams.

The name he had surrendered.

He understood why the admiral believed those words.

But he also remembered Milo's laughter.

Finn's loyalty.

Brann's courage.

The people who had chosen to sail beside him.

"My name doesn't tell me everything I am," KOKI said.

"But the people who call me by it matter."

Morcant raised his sword.

"Then you will lose them."

The eleven lanterns flared.

And the deck disappeared beneath a wave of black light.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Chamber of Regrets

KOKI opened his eye.

He was standing in the village where his journey had begun.

The streets were empty.

The houses were burning.

A familiar voice called from the smoke.

"Why did you leave?"

KOKI turned.

A figure stood before the ruined harbor.

His father.

"You wanted adventure," the figure said.

"And while you sailed, others suffered."

KOKI tried to speak.

But the words would not come.

The scene changed.

He saw Finn falling from a burning ship.

Brann buried beneath a broken mast.

Veyra disappearing into black water.

Lyra reaching for someone she could not save.

Milo sinking beneath the Silver Sea.

Again.

And again.

Every moment KOKI had feared.

Every choice he had questioned.

Every life he might have failed.

A voice whispered.

"You cannot save everyone."

KOKI fell to his knees.

"I know."

"Then why do you keep trying?"

He looked at the burning village.

For a moment, he had no answer.

Then he remembered something Milo had said.

"Did we win?"

And his own reply.

"You're here. That's enough of a victory for me."

KOKI stood.

"I cannot promise that everyone will survive.

But I can promise that no one will be abandoned while I still have a choice."

The burning village began to fade.

The voices grew quiet.

KOKI drew his sword.

Golden light spread from its blade.

The illusion shattered.

✦ ✦ ✦

What Brann Remembered

Brann stood alone on a battlefield.

Rain fell upon broken shields.

A younger version of himself was running toward a burning fortress.

Behind him, someone shouted.

"Wait!"

Brann froze.

He knew that voice.

His brother.

The memory he had spent years trying to forget.

The fortress gates were closing.

Brann had been too frightened to turn back.

He had survived.

His brother had not.

Now the memory played again.

And again.

A voice whispered.

"You ran."

Brann closed his eyes.

"Yes."

"You left him."

Tears mixed with the rain.

"Yes."

"Then you are a coward."

Brann tightened his grip on the broken axe.

For years, he had believed that accusation.

But now he remembered another battlefield.

The Broken Reefs.

KOKI jumping into the sea to save Milo.

Finn steering toward impossible danger.

Lyra holding the star map with trembling hands.

And himself, standing beside them.

Brann raised his head.

"I ran once."

His voice shook.

"I wish I hadn't."

"But I came back."

He lifted his axe.

"A mistake can follow you for a lifetime.

It doesn't have to lead you for the rest of it."

He struck the fortress gate.

The illusion shattered.

Brann returned to the flagship deck.

One of the lanterns had begun to crack.

✦ ✦ ✦

Lyra's Choice

Lyra stood inside a silent library.

Thousands of books floated around her.

Every page contained a different future.

She saw one where KOKI died during the battle.

Another where Finn's ship sank.

Another where the Hollow King escaped.

Then she saw a future where everyone survived.

She reached toward the book.

A voice whispered.

"Choose this one."

Lyra hesitated.

"What is the price?"

The book opened.

Inside was a picture of the entire world, silent and motionless.

No storms.

No battles.

No sorrow.

But no laughter either.

No adventure.

No choices.

Every life was frozen in a perfect moment.

Lyra stepped back.

"That's not survival."

The voice grew colder.

"It is peace."

Lyra closed the book.

"No."

She remembered the City Without Time.

The people who had waited for centuries inside a single moment.

She remembered what KOKI had fought to restore.

Their freedom to live.

Even if living meant uncertainty.

Lyra tore the page in half.

The library collapsed.

She returned to the flagship deck.

A second lantern cracked.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Admiral's Lost Daughter

Morcant staggered.

Two lanterns had gone dark.

His silver armor began to fracture.

KOKI approached him.

"You're losing your control."

Morcant raised his sword.

"You understand nothing!"

He attacked.

Their blades collided.

Gold and crimson sparks filled the air.

Morcant was stronger.

KOKI was faster.

They fought between the iron towers, beneath the eleven stars.

At last, Morcant drove KOKI against the railing.

"Do you know why I serve the king?"

KOKI struggled against the blade.

"Because he stole your memories!"

Morcant shook his head.

"Because he promised to return one."

The admiral's eyes filled with tears.

"My daughter."

KOKI stopped struggling.

Morcant's voice became almost gentle.

"She was seven when the war began."

"She waited at the harbor for my return."

"I promised her I would come home."

The admiral lowered his sword slightly.

"But when the kingdoms fell, the Hollow King erased our histories."

"Her name."

"Her face."

"Even the sound of her laughter."

He looked toward the lanterns.

"The king promised that if I brought him the Thirteenth Captain, he would let me remember her."

KOKI understood the terrible bargain.

The Hollow King had taken everything from Morcant.

Then offered to sell it back.

KOKI slowly lowered his sword.

"Admiral."

"You don't need his permission to love your daughter."

Morcant trembled.

"What?"

"You remember that she waited."

"You remember that you promised to come home."

"You remember that you loved her."

KOKI stepped closer.

"Even if the world forgets someone, the love they gave doesn't become meaningless."

For the first time, the crimson fire in Morcant's eyes began to fade.

A small memory appeared before him.

A little girl standing on a pier.

She held a paper boat.

"Papa!"

The admiral fell to his knees.

He reached toward the image.

"Elara."

He whispered the name again.

"Elara."

Then the eleven lanterns began to scream.

✦ ✦ ✦

The King's True Weapon

The Hollow King's voice filled the sky.

"ENOUGH."

Morcant cried out.

Black chains erupted from the deck and wrapped around his armor.

The lanterns rose into the air.

Their crimson flames merged into an enormous circle.

Veyra looked up.

Her face went white.

"The lanterns were never the weapon."

KOKI turned.

"What do you mean?"

"They were anchors."

"Anchors for what?"

Veyra pointed toward the sky.

The eleven red stars were moving.

Slowly, they formed a single enormous crown.

At its center, a black doorway began to open.

The Hollow King's voice echoed across the sea.

"YOU RESTORED THE FIRST CHAIN."

"SO I FOUND ANOTHER WAY."

KOKI looked at the doorway.

The enemy fleet had not been sent merely to capture him.

The battle itself had been a ritual.

Every cannon fired.

Every ship destroyed.

Every forgotten sailor forced to fight.

All of it had fed the lanterns.

All of it had opened the doorway.

Veyra shouted.

"If the crown completes, the king won't need the broken First Seal!"

"He'll create a second entrance!"

Brann looked at the sky.

"Can we stop it?"

Veyra shook her head.

"Not from here."

Then Morcant spoke.

His voice was weak.

"I can."

✦ ✦ ✦

One Last Command

The admiral struggled to stand.

Black chains still wrapped around his body.

KOKI rushed to help him.

Morcant pushed his hand away.

"No."

"This is my responsibility."

He looked toward the fleet.

The thousands of forgotten sailors waiting for orders.

For centuries, he had commanded them in the Hollow King's name.

Now he remembered the promise he had made long before the war.

To defend the living.

Not enslave the dead.

Morcant raised his broken sword.

"SAILORS OF THE CROWNLESS NAVY!"

The fleet fell silent.

"YOU WERE NOT BORN TO SERVE A KING WHO STOLE YOUR NAMES!"

The red light inside the sailors' eyes began to flicker.

"YOU WERE FATHERS!"

"MOTHERS!"

"SONS!"

"DAUGHTERS!"

"YOU WERE PEOPLE BEFORE YOU WERE SOLDIERS!"

The eleven lanterns shook violently.

Morcant turned toward KOKI.

"Captain."

"Will you give my sailors one final chance to choose?"

KOKI nodded.

"Every one of them."

Morcant smiled.

Then he raised his sword toward the sky.

"CROWNLESS NAVY!

FOR THE FIRST TIME IN THREE HUNDRED YEARS...

YOU ARE FREE TO DISOBEY!"

The fleet erupted in golden light.

One ship turned against the black doorway.

Then ten.

Then a hundred.

The forgotten sailors raised their own flags.

Not the crimson eye.

Not the Hollow King's crown.

Their old banners.

The symbols of the kingdoms they had once defended.

Morcant looked upward.

"ELARA!"

"YOUR FATHER IS COMING HOME!"

He drove his sword into the central lantern.

Golden fire consumed the deck.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Battle Above the Waves

The first lantern exploded.

Then the second.

Then the third.

Across the sky, the enormous crown began to crack.

The Hollow King roared.

"TRAITOR!"

A black hand reached through the unfinished doorway.

It seized Morcant's flagship.

The stone hull began to split.

Brann grabbed Veyra.

"We have to go!"

Lyra pointed toward the approaching Crownless Navy.

"They're coming to help us!"

A smaller ghost ship pulled alongside.

Its captain extended a rope.

KOKI recognized him.

The young sailor who had remembered his daughter.

"CAPTAIN!" the sailor shouted.

"THIS WAY!"

Brann and Lyra jumped across.

Veyra followed.

KOKI turned back.

Morcant remained beside the last lantern.

The admiral's armor was breaking apart.

KOKI ran toward him.

"COME WITH US!"

Morcant shook his head.

"The last lantern is bound to me."

"If I leave, the doorway survives."

KOKI stopped.

There had to be another way.

There was always another way.

But the ship was collapsing.

The black hand was growing stronger.

And Morcant had already made his choice.

The admiral removed a small silver ring from his finger.

He placed it in KOKI's hand.

"My daughter gave me this before I sailed."

"If you ever find the harbor of the Forgotten Kingdoms..."

He paused.

"Tell them we remembered."

KOKI closed his fingers around the ring.

"I promise."

Morcant smiled.

"Then go, Captain."

KOKI hesitated.

The admiral raised his sword.

"GO!"

KOKI jumped.

The ghost sailors caught him as the flagship split apart.

Behind him, Morcant drove his sword through the final lantern.

A golden explosion rose into the heavens.

The black doorway shattered.

And the eleven crimson stars went dark.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Price of Victory

For several minutes, the entire sea was silent.

The cannon fire had stopped.

The black doorway was gone.

The enemy fleet no longer advanced.

Then the forgotten ships began to glow.

Their black sails turned silver.

Their broken hulls became transparent.

One by one, the sailors stepped onto their decks.

No longer soldiers of the Hollow King.

No longer prisoners of stolen memories.

Just people.

People who had waited centuries for their final voyage.

Finn's damaged ship emerged from the smoke.

Milo stood at the railing.

He waved both arms.

"CAPTAIN!"

KOKI waved back.

For one wonderful moment, he felt the relief of coming home.

Then he looked toward the sinking flagship.

Morcant was gone.

KOKI opened his hand.

The silver ring rested in his palm.

Its surface was engraved with a tiny paper boat.

He thought of the admiral's daughter.

The little girl who had waited at the harbor.

And he wondered how many promises the sea had swallowed.

Veyra approached.

"He saved us."

KOKI nodded.

"But he didn't get to go home."

Veyra looked toward the fading fleet.

"Perhaps not the way he wanted."

"But he remembered where home was."

KOKI watched the last silver ship disappear into the morning light.

He placed Morcant's ring beside Milo's wooden ship.

Two promises.

Two reasons to keep sailing.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Star That Did Not Go Dark

By dawn, the surviving crew had gathered on the repaired deck.

Finn examined the ship's sails.

Brann counted the remaining supplies.

Lyra studied the ancient map.

Milo slept beside the wheel.

KOKI stood at the bow, watching the sunrise.

The eleven crimson stars had vanished from the sky.

For the first time since the First Star fell, the ocean looked peaceful.

Then Veyra gasped.

"Captain."

KOKI turned.

The star map was changing.

Eleven symbols had become pale.

The First Star still shone gold.

But at the center of the map, a new symbol had appeared.

A black crown.

Beneath it were four words.

THE KING'S SECOND HEART.

Finn leaned closer.

"That doesn't sound good."

Veyra turned the parchment over.

An ancient inscription appeared on its back.

She read it aloud.

"When the twelve stars are forced into war, the crown shall break..."

She stopped.

KOKI looked at her.

"Keep reading."

Veyra swallowed.

"...and the heart beneath the world shall begin to beat."

A deep sound echoed through the ocean.

THUMP.

The ship trembled.

THUMP.

The water turned black beneath the keel.

THUMP.

Far below, something enormous was waking.

KOKI remembered the stone gate beneath the Silver Sea.

The twelve chains.

The Hollow King's eyes.

And the warning of the Shadow Captain.

Eleven stars remain.

But now the stars were silent.

Something else had begun.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Message From Below

A silver light rose from the water.

It gathered above the deck, forming the outline of a familiar figure.

The Shadow Captain.

His image was weak and flickering.

KOKI stepped forward.

"You're alive!"

The older captain shook his head.

"Not in the way you hope."

He looked toward the horizon.

"You stopped the War of Twelve Stars."

"You freed the forgotten sailors."

"But the Hollow King wanted the stars to fight."

KOKI frowned.

"Why?"

"Because the war awakened something that even he could not reach while the sea remained at peace."

Veyra stepped closer.

"The Second Heart."

The Shadow Captain nodded.

"Yes."

"Where is it?" KOKI asked.

The old captain looked directly at him.

"Beneath the ruins of the Forgotten Kingdoms."

"The place where the first Thirteenth Light was divided."

KOKI thought of Morcant's ring.

The lost harbor.

The promise he had just made.

The Shadow Captain continued.

"You must reach the Heart before the Hollow King."

"And KOKI..."

His voice faltered.

"There is something I should have told you forty years ago."

KOKI stepped forward.

"What?"

The silver figure began to dissolve.

The old captain reached toward him.

"The child you saw in the Mirror..."

"The child whose name you surrendered..."

He paused.

"He was not the only one."

KOKI froze.

"What do you mean?"

The Shadow Captain looked frightened.

For the first time, truly frightened.

"There were two."

The silver light shattered.

The image vanished.

And beneath the ship, the Second Heart beat again.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Other Child

Far beneath the Forgotten Kingdoms, a chamber opened for the first time in forty years.

Its walls were covered with broken mirrors.

At the center stood two empty cradles.

One was golden.

The other was black.

A small figure stood beside them.

A gray elephant.

A pirate's coat.

A familiar face.

But no eyepatch.

And both eyes burned crimson.

He watched the black water rising through the floor.

Then he smiled.

"So..."

"My brother has finally come looking for me."

Behind him, the Hollow King's voice whispered.

"Are you ready?"

The young captain drew a black sword.

"I've been ready for forty years."

Above the ocean, KOKI stood at the bow of his damaged ship.

He did not know that someone with his face was waiting beneath the world.

He knew only that another voyage had begun.

He looked toward his friends.

Then toward the rising sun.

"Finn."

"Captain?"

"Set a course for the Forgotten Kingdoms."

Finn nodded.

"And after that?"

KOKI touched the silver ring in his pocket.

He thought of Morcant.

Of Milo.

Of every sailor who had finally remembered a name.

Then he answered.

"We keep our promises.

Even the ones that lead us into the dark."

The battered ship turned toward the unknown horizon.

And beneath the sea, a second captain opened his eyes.
