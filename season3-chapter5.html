CHAPTER 05

The Kingdom of Unwritten History

Every world deserves a chance to exist.

The guardian's sword did not cut flesh.

It cut history.

KOKI narrowly escaped its first attack.

Behind him, an entire mountain appeared, aged a thousand years, and collapsed into dust.

"Don't let that sword touch you!" Nox shouted.

The guardian raised its enormous weapon again.

"You belong to a history that no longer exists."

KOKI tightened his grip on his sword.

"Then history has made a mistake."

The guardian struck.

KOKI turned the wheel sharply.

The ship sailed beneath the enormous blade and crashed through the first golden doorway.

A brilliant light swallowed everything.

✦ ✦ ✦

I. The Kingdom That Should Not Exist

When KOKI opened his eyes, the ocean was gone.

His ship was falling through the sky.

Below him stretched an enormous kingdom surrounded by mountains and waterfalls.

Golden towers rose above magnificent forests.

Flying vessels traveled between floating islands.

Thousands of colorful flags danced in the wind.

"Nox! The sails!"

The two captains pulled the ropes.

The sails opened just before the ship struck a river flowing through the capital.

Water exploded across the streets.

Royal soldiers surrounded the vessel.

Their commander raised his spear.

"Intruders! Kneel before the Kingdom of Eldoria!"

KOKI climbed onto the railing.

"We're looking for a friend."

The commander ignored him.

"His Majesty will decide your fate."

The soldiers escorted them toward an enormous palace.

Above its entrance stood a golden statue of a mouse wearing a royal crown.

KOKI stopped.

He recognized the face.

"Milo?"

✦ ✦ ✦

II. The Boy Who Became a King

The palace doors opened.

Hundreds of royal guards stood beneath enormous golden pillars.

At the far end of the chamber sat a young mouse king.

He wore a magnificent blue cloak and a crown decorated with silver stars.

KOKI ran forward.

"MILO!"

The guards immediately lowered their spears.

The king raised his hand.

"Who are you?"

KOKI stared at him.

"It's me. Your captain."

The king looked confused.

"I have never sailed aboard a pirate ship."

Nox stepped closer to KOKI.

"Time moves differently here."

KOKI looked around the palace.

Paintings depicted Milo growing from a frightened child into a beloved ruler.

There were portraits of battles, celebrations, and peaceful villages.

In one painting, Milo stood beside his mother.

She was alive.

KOKI slowly lowered his sword.

The Council had not simply imprisoned Milo.

It had given him another life.

A life in which he had everything he had ever lost.

✦ ✦ ✦

III. The Secret Beneath the Crown

That evening, Milo invited the two strangers to dine with him.

He listened patiently as KOKI described their adventures.

The forgotten seas.

The falling stars.

The paper boats.

But none of the stories awakened his memories.

"I'm sorry," Milo said.

"Perhaps you have mistaken me for someone else."

Suddenly, an enormous bell rang outside the palace.

The servants froze.

Milo's expression changed.

"Take our guests somewhere safe."

KOKI stood.

"What's happening?"

"The Night of Renewal."

Beyond the windows, the stars began disappearing.

Across the capital, buildings turned transparent.

An elderly rabbit vanished while holding his granddaughter's hand.

A mother disappeared from the doorway of her home.

Entire streets faded into darkness.

Milo rushed toward the palace balcony.

"Not again!"

KOKI followed him.

"What is happening to your kingdom?"

Milo removed his crown.

Inside it, a golden clock was turning backward.

"Every night, this kingdom loses a piece of its history."

He looked toward the disappearing city.

"And every morning, everyone forgets what was lost."

KOKI understood.

Eldoria was not a second chance.

It was a dying history desperately trying to survive.

✦ ✦ ✦

IV. The Army of Forgotten Kings

The ground beneath the palace trembled.

From the mountains emerged an army of enormous stone warriors.

Each wore the broken crown of a forgotten king.

Their armor carried the names of civilizations that had never existed.

At the center marched a colossal knight with six golden arms.

Its chest contained a burning hourglass.

A voice thundered across the capital.

"THE UNWRITTEN HISTORY MUST END."

Milo drew his royal sword.

"Protect the citizens!"

Thousands of soldiers rushed toward the city walls.

KOKI turned to Nox.

"Bring the ship."

Nox smiled.

"I was hoping you'd say that."

KOKI seized a rope hanging from a passing royal airship.

He swung above the rooftops as the stone army entered the capital.

The colossal knight raised its weapons.

KOKI landed upon its shoulder and struck its golden armor.

The impact shook the entire city.

The knight threw him into the air.

At that moment, the familiar black pirate flag appeared above the rooftops.

Nox stood at the wheel of their flying ship.

"CAPTAIN! YOUR RIDE!"

KOKI grabbed the descending rope with his trunk.

The ship turned sharply between the golden towers.

Its cannons thundered.

Stone warriors collapsed into clouds of ancient dust.

Below them, Milo fought beside his soldiers.

KOKI looked down at the young king.

Even without his memories, Milo had become someone who refused to abandon others.

Some things, KOKI realized, could survive even the destruction of history.

✦ ✦ ✦

V. The Last Paper Boat

The colossal knight shattered the palace gates.

Milo rushed inside.

His mother stood beneath the golden pillars.

Her body was slowly becoming transparent.

"Mother!"

He ran toward her.

She smiled and placed a small paper boat in his hands.

"Do you remember what I taught you?"

Milo began to cry.

"Nothing has to be perfect the first time."

KOKI entered the chamber.

The knight's enormous shadow fell across them.

Milo embraced his mother.

"I won't let you disappear."

She gently touched his cheek.

"My dear boy."

"You cannot spend your entire life trying to keep yesterday alive."

The paper boat began glowing.

Images appeared around Milo.

A pirate ship.

A laughing crew.

A captain with a scar upon his cheek.

A pair of arms embracing him after a terrible battle.

Milo turned toward KOKI.

His eyes widened.

"Captain?"

KOKI smiled.

"Welcome back, Milo."

The young king looked at his mother.

"Will I lose you again?"

She kissed his forehead.

"You will carry me wherever you go."

Her golden light flowed into the paper boat.

Milo closed his eyes.

For the first time, he understood that remembering someone was different from refusing to let them go.

✦ ✦ ✦

VI. The Captain Who Refused to Choose

The golden knight entered the palace.

Its hourglass burned brighter.

"THE KINGDOM WILL BE ERASED."

Milo gripped his paper boat.

KOKI raised his sword.

"No."

The knight stopped.

"THIS HISTORY HAS NO RIGHT TO EXIST."

KOKI looked at the frightened citizens gathering outside.

"Tell that to the people living here."

Nox brought the ship beside the palace balcony.

"Captain! We have to leave!"

KOKI looked toward the thousands of citizens.

He could rescue Milo.

He could escape before the kingdom disappeared.

But he could not abandon an entire world simply because someone had decided it should never have existed.

"Milo, does this kingdom have a harbor?"

The young king nodded.

"Then tell everyone to bring their ships."

KOKI climbed aboard his vessel.

Nox stared at him.

"You're planning to evacuate an entire kingdom?"

KOKI raised the pirate flag.

"We've done more impossible things."

The royal fleet gathered beneath the collapsing sky.

Hundreds of vessels followed KOKI toward the golden doorway.

The colossal knight pursued them.

KOKI turned his ship around.

"NOX! TAKE THEM THROUGH!"

He sailed directly toward the knight.

The creature raised its six enormous weapons.

KOKI aimed his cannons at the burning hourglass.

"FIRE!"

The hourglass shattered.

A storm of golden sand swept across the kingdom.

The doorway expanded.

One by one, the royal vessels escaped into the ocean of discarded histories.

KOKI's ship followed just as Eldoria disappeared behind them.

But its people survived.

Milo stood beside KOKI, holding his mother's paper boat.

Behind them sailed an entire fleet of refugees from a history that had never been written.

Nox laughed.

"We came here to rescue one mouse."

KOKI smiled.

"Plans change."

Suddenly, the paper boat pointed toward another distant light.

A terrible sound echoed across the darkness.

Thousands of hammers striking metal.

Beyond the next doorway stood an enormous fortress surrounded by rivers of molten iron.

At its center, a familiar figure was chained to a gigantic machine.

Brann.

But something was terribly wrong.

His enormous hammer was being used to forge weapons capable of destroying entire histories.

And standing beside him was Director Vaelor.

Nineteen hours remained.
