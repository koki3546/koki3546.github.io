THE KOKI CHRONICLES · CHAPTER 05

The Betrayal

Sometimes the deepest wound comes from someone you trusted.

"At last," the masked figure whispered.

The Mourning Crown fell silent.

At the foot of the staircase stood a towering stranger in black armor. A silver mask concealed his face, and a compass of dark glass trembled in his hand.

Its needle pointed directly at Finn.

"Rowan's son. Come forward."

Finn stumbled behind KOKI.

"Who are you?"

The stranger tilted his head.

"I am Lord Morcant, Keeper of the Drowned Crown."

Captain Veyra went rigid.

KOKI noticed the change immediately.

"You know him?"

Veyra did not answer.

Morcant gave a low laugh.

"Of course she does. She brought me here."

✦ ✦ ✦

KOKI turned toward Veyra.

The raven captain stood motionless, her silver sword hanging at her side.

"Tell me he's lying."

Veyra's eyes remained fixed on the wet wooden deck.

"KOKI..."

"Tell me!"

She closed her eyes.

"I sent the signal."

The words struck harder than any sword.

Finn stepped backward.

"You led us onto this ship?"

Veyra nodded.

Morcant extended one armored hand.

"The key, child."

Finn clutched the brass key against his blue scarf.

KOKI stepped between them.

"You'll have to get through me."

Morcant lifted his black-glass blade.

The ship shuddered.

Chains erupted from the planks and raced toward Finn.

KOKI caught one with his trunk and dragged it sideways.

Veyra struck another with her sword.

For a moment, KOKI thought she had chosen their side.

Then Veyra seized Finn's wrist.

She took the brass key.

And threw it to Morcant.

✦ ✦ ✦

KOKI stared at her.

The key spun through the air.

Morcant caught it.

Somewhere beneath the ocean, a bell answered.

"Veyra..." Finn whispered.

His voice was so small that KOKI barely heard it.

Morcant raised the key.

Blue fire spread across the Mourning Crown's rigging.

The skeleton officer who had helped them in the previous battle fell to his knees.

"Captain! The chains are returning!"

KOKI rushed toward Morcant.

The masked lord struck him with the flat of his sword.

KOKI flew across the deck and crashed into the railing.

Pain shot through his shoulder.

The wood splintered beneath him.

For an instant, he hung above the black sea.

Finn screamed his name.

KOKI caught the broken railing with his trunk and pulled himself up.

When he reached the deck, Morcant was already dragging Finn toward the captain's staircase.

"KOKI! Don't let him take me!"

KOKI lunged forward.

Veyra blocked his path.

"Stop! You cannot defeat him!"

KOKI looked at her as though she were a stranger.

"Then get out of my way."

✦ ✦ ✦

Veyra's sword trembled.

For years, she had imagined this moment.

The moment when her debt would finally be paid.

But she had never imagined the look in Finn's eyes.

Or the fury in KOKI's.

"You don't understand," she said.

"Then make me understand!"

Veyra lowered her weapon.

Rain began to fall.

"A hundred years ago, Rowan and I sailed together. We found the Bell of the Deep beneath the Forgotten Island."

KOKI listened, breathing heavily.

"We thought it was treasure. We thought it could give us the power to command the seas."

She looked toward the dark water.

"But the Bell was a prison. Something ancient had been sealed beneath the ocean."

The deck shook again.

"Morcant wanted to open it. Rowan refused. And I..."

Her voice broke.

"I betrayed Rowan."

KOKI said nothing.

"I gave Morcant the location of Rowan's ship. I believed he would only take the map."

Veyra's feathers trembled.

"Instead, the ship was destroyed. Rowan disappeared. And the sea swallowed everyone who tried to stop Morcant."

She reached into her coat.

Inside was a faded drawing of Rowan holding a newborn Finn.

"I have spent every year since trying to undo what I did."

KOKI looked at the picture.

"So you betrayed his son too?"

Veyra flinched.

"Morcant promised to tell me where Rowan is."

KOKI's voice grew quiet.

"And you believed him."

✦ ✦ ✦

A scream echoed from the upper deck.

Finn.

KOKI ran.

Veyra called after him, but he did not stop.

Above the captain's cabin, Morcant had fastened Finn to an ancient iron chair.

The brass key glowed inside a lock shaped like a bell.

Blue chains rose from the floor and circled the little fox.

"The child carries Rowan's blood," Morcant said.

He turned the key.

"And Rowan was the last navigator who knew the way to the prison."

Finn cried out.

The black compass flashed.

A map appeared in the air, made entirely of blue light.

At its center stood a mountain shaped like a crown.

Morcant smiled beneath his mask.

"The Last Compass. At last, its path is mine."

KOKI seized a loose rope and swung toward the platform.

Morcant struck the rope with his black blade.

It snapped.

KOKI fell.

He landed hard on the lower deck.

A crack ran through the planks beneath him.

He tried to stand.

His injured shoulder gave way.

Finn looked down at him.

"KOKI! Please, run!"

KOKI shook his head.

"I'm not leaving you."

Morcant raised his sword.

"Then you will die here together."

✦ ✦ ✦

A silver blade struck Morcant's black sword.

Veyra stood between the masked lord and KOKI.

"You promised me Rowan!"

Morcant laughed.

"And you believed a promise from a man who commands the dead?"

He struck her across the deck.

Veyra crashed into the mast.

Her sword slid away.

Morcant turned toward Finn.

The glowing map collapsed into his black compass.

"I no longer need the child."

He lifted his blade.

KOKI saw the movement.

There was no time to climb.

No time to think.

He ran straight toward the base of the platform.

With all his remaining strength, he wrapped his trunk around a supporting beam.

And pulled.

The old timber groaned.

The platform tilted.

Morcant stumbled.

His sword struck empty air.

Finn's chair slid toward the broken edge.

KOKI leaped.

He caught Finn with his trunk just as the platform collapsed.

Both fell through the shattered deck.

Darkness swallowed them.

✦ ✦ ✦

KOKI woke in cold seawater.

He was floating beside a piece of broken timber.

Finn clung to his shoulder.

Above them, the Mourning Crown drifted into the mist.

Its sails glowed blue.

The black compass was gone.

So was the brass key.

KOKI searched the darkness.

There was no sign of Veyra.

No boat.

No map.

No way home.

Finn began to cry.

"I'm sorry, KOKI. You lost everything because of me."

KOKI pulled the little fox closer to the floating timber.

His shoulder burned. His body trembled from the cold.

For the first time since leaving home, he did not know what to do.

He looked toward the horizon.

The stars were hidden.

The sea seemed endless.

He wanted to tell Finn that everything would be fine.

But he could not.

So he told him the truth.

"I'm scared too."

Finn looked up.

KOKI managed a tired smile.

"But I'm still here. And so are you."

He wrapped one arm around the trembling fox.

"As long as we're together, we haven't lost everything."

✦ ✦ ✦

Hours passed.

The ocean grew colder.

Finn finally fell asleep against KOKI's shoulder.

KOKI kept his head above the waves.

He thought of Veyra.

Of the secrets she had hidden. Of the terrible choices she had made.

He was angry.

Angrier than he had ever been.

Yet he remembered how she had stood between Morcant and his sword.

She had betrayed them.

And then she had tried to save them.

KOKI did not know whether he could forgive her.

But he knew one thing.

If she was still alive, he would not leave her to Morcant's mercy.

Suddenly, Finn stirred.

"KOKI..."

His eyes were still closed.

"My father used to say... the true compass never points north."

KOKI looked down.

"What?"

Finn opened his eyes.

"It points to the thing you are willing to lose everything to protect."

Beneath them, the ocean began to glow.

Two enormous golden eyes opened in the deep.

KOKI tightened his grip around Finn.

But this time, the creature did not attack.

A vast shape moved beneath them.

Slowly, gently, the water lifted.

The broken timber rose onto the back of something larger than any ship.

And from the darkness came a voice older than the sea.

"At last... a captain who understands."
