CHAPTER 07

The Admiral of the Endless Fleet

A safe ocean means nothing if no one is free to sail it.

The first cannonball carried KOKI's name.

It struck the ocean beside his ship and exploded into a thousand silver chains.

Nox turned the wheel.

Brann raised his hammer.

Milo nearly dropped his paper boat.

Beyond the horizon, thousands of warships emerged from the mist.

Their sails carried the symbol of a silver compass.

At the center of the fleet stood an enormous battleship.

A familiar figure watched from its highest deck.

Admiral Veyra.

"Captain KOKI," her voice echoed across the ocean.

"By order of the Meridian Council, your voyage ends here."

KOKI smiled sadly.

"That's not how you used to greet me."

✦ ✦ ✦

I. The Ocean Without Pirates

The warships moved into formation.

Every cannon pointed toward KOKI.

Beyond the fleet lay a magnificent civilization built upon floating cities.

Merchant vessels traveled safely between golden harbors.

There were no pirates.

No sea monsters.

No abandoned ships.

Under Admiral Veyra's command, the ocean had become the safest place in the world.

But something was missing.

Every vessel followed an invisible path.

Whenever a ship attempted to leave its assigned route, silver chains emerged from the water and dragged it back.

Milo watched a small fishing boat struggling against the chains.

"Why can't they go wherever they want?"

Nox lowered his telescope.

"Because someone has decided where everyone belongs."

KOKI looked toward Veyra's battleship.

"Let's find out who."

✦ ✦ ✦

II. The Admiral's Promise

Veyra stood inside her command chamber.

An enormous map covered the walls.

Every ship appeared as a tiny silver light.

Not a single vessel had been lost in seventeen years.

Director Vaelor appeared within a golden mirror.

"The pirate threatens everything you have built."

Veyra studied KOKI's approaching ship.

"Why does he know my name?"

"He comes from a discarded history."

Vaelor paused.

"In that history, you failed to protect those who trusted you."

The mirror revealed a terrible storm.

Ships sank beneath enormous waves.

A younger Veyra screamed as sailors disappeared into the darkness.

She turned away.

"Enough."

Vaelor smiled.

"Here, you have the power to prevent such tragedies."

Veyra looked toward the ocean.

"Then I will protect it."

She raised her hand.

"Prepare the entire fleet."

✦ ✦ ✦

III. A Captain Against a Thousand Ships

The ocean erupted.

Silver cannonballs filled the sky.

KOKI's ship sailed between enormous battleships as Brann shattered the chains with his hammer.

Nox steered through a narrow opening.

Milo pointed toward Veyra's flagship.

"There!"

KOKI climbed the mast.

A passing cannonball unfolded into a mechanical bird.

He jumped onto its back and seized its silver wings.

The creature carried him above the battlefield.

Below him, hundreds of cannons turned upward.

KOKI released one wing.

The mechanical bird spun wildly through the air.

He leapt onto Veyra's flagship just as the creature crashed into an empty observation tower.

Veyra drew her sword.

KOKI landed before her.

For a moment, neither moved.

Then Veyra attacked.

Their swords collided.

"You are endangering innocent lives!"

KOKI blocked another strike.

"And you're keeping them in chains!"

"I have saved thousands!"

"Then why are they afraid to leave?"

Veyra hesitated.

KOKI lowered his sword.

"Come with me. I'll show you."

✦ ✦ ✦

IV. The Little Ship Beyond the Line

An alarm suddenly echoed across the flagship.

A small passenger vessel had crossed the boundary of the permitted ocean.

Beyond it, an enormous storm was forming.

Veyra immediately turned toward her officers.

"Send a rescue ship!"

No one moved.

A golden message appeared upon the command map.

UNAUTHORIZED HISTORY. RESCUE PROHIBITED.

Veyra stared at the words.

"Override the command."

REQUEST DENIED.

Through her telescope, she saw children standing on the deck of the little vessel.

Silver chains wrapped around its hull.

Instead of pulling it to safety, they dragged it beneath the waves.

Veyra dropped her telescope.

KOKI was already running.

He jumped from the flagship.

Nox brought their pirate ship alongside him.

"Going somewhere, Captain?"

KOKI grabbed the rope.

"Someone needs us."

Veyra watched the black pirate flag disappear into the storm.

Something inside her began to break.

✦ ✦ ✦

V. The Day the Admiral Became a Pirate

The waves rose higher than mountains.

Brann held the damaged mast together.

Nox fought against the storm.

KOKI climbed onto the sinking passenger vessel and carried its children toward his ship.

But the silver chains continued pulling both vessels into the darkness.

Suddenly, an enormous battleship appeared through the rain.

Veyra stood at its bow.

"ALL CANNONS! TARGET THE CHAINS!"

Her officers stared at her.

"Admiral, that violates the Council's orders!"

Veyra drew her sword.

"Then the Council can find another admiral."

The cannons thundered.

The chains shattered.

Across the ocean, thousands of warships received a new command.

PROTECT EVERY SHIP.

The golden compass above Veyra's flagship cracked.

Memories flooded her mind.

A black pirate flag.

An elephant laughing beneath the stars.

A crew that had crossed impossible oceans.

A promise carved into a wooden deck.

No one left behind.

Veyra fell to her knees.

"Captain..."

KOKI climbed aboard her battleship.

He offered his hand.

"Welcome back."

Veyra took it.

For the first time in seventeen years of borrowed history, she felt free.

✦ ✦ ✦

VI. The Library of Tomorrow

Across the ocean, silver chains disappeared.

Merchant vessels changed their courses.

Fishing boats sailed beyond their familiar waters.

The children from the rescued vessel waved toward KOKI.

Veyra removed her admiral's insignia and placed it upon the wheel of her flagship.

"What will happen to this fleet?" Milo asked.

Veyra smiled.

"They don't need an admiral anymore."

She looked toward the liberated ocean.

"They need the freedom to choose their own captains."

As the pirate ship prepared to depart, a golden feather descended from the sky.

It landed upon Milo's paper boat.

The ocean transformed into a reflection of countless books.

Beyond the next doorway stood a library larger than an entire planet.

Its shelves stretched between stars.

At its entrance, a familiar lantern burned.

Lyra.

But every book contained the story of a future that had not yet happened.

One book lay open upon a silver table.

Its title was written in black letters.

THE DEATH OF CAPTAIN KOKI.

Veyra looked at KOKI.

He stared at the book for a long moment.

Then he smiled.

"Someone's been writing without my permission."

Far beneath the Meridian Tower, Vaelor turned another page.

The little fox inside the glass chamber opened her eyes.

"Father?"

Vaelor began to cry.

But behind him, the ancient clock continued turning.

Seventeen hours remained.
